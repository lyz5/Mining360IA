1.0 Introduction  
This Best Practice is aimed at restoring liners on Ultra Class trucks using Plateau Honing. 
Scope  
C175 and 3500 liners from Finish Hone to Plateau Honing, considering the reusability tolerances 
of the liner in terms of honing angle, roughness and internal diameter. 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
This Best Practice details the procedure and standards for performing liner honing on C175 and 
3500 engines. It provides a comprehensive guide from the necessary preliminary steps prior to 
honing through to acceptance criteria for dimensional evaluation, visual inspection and roughness 
measurement after the process. This  document also sets out precise protocols to ensure quality 
and precision at every stage of the process, ensuring that honed liners meet the required standards 
to guarantee optimum performance. 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
● Preliminary steps (carried out in the CRC workshop) 
Acceptance criteria for dimensional assessment according to GSP-STT-VB-0003.

1. Parameters for Honing in Rottler H85A 
● Sensitivity: 6.00  
● Choice of stones: [514-9-14R] + [514-9-14J] 
● Brush selection: [514-9-14H] 
● Roughing stone (stone 1) operating ranges:  
○ 514-9-14R  
○ 50% rough load 
○ 40% finish load  
○ 10% plateau load  
○ 180 RPM.  
○ 0.020 mm 
● Finishing stone (stone 2) operating ranges: 
○ 514-9-14J 
○ 30% rough load  
○ 20% finish load  
○ 10% plateau load  
○ 180 RPM 
○ 0.005 mm 
● Plateau honing (brushes) operating ranges: 
○ 514-9-14H 
○ 30% rough load 
○ 20% finish load 
○ 8% plateau load 
○ 4 strokes 
○ 80 RPM. 
● Setting angle: 40° 
● Stone: 
○ Upper and lower height margin: 22.00 mm

4.0 Benefits 
● It provides a clear and standardized framework for performing C175 and 3500 liner honing, 
ensuring quality in the process. 
 
● It helps to optimize the use of resources such as time, labor and tools, resulting in greater 
efficiency and cost reduction, with annual savings of: 
○ $386 per C175 liner recovered. 
○ $276 per 3500 liner recovered.
