MINING DEALER BEST PRACTICE 
1.0 lntroduction 
When transmissions are brought to the workshop to be revised, the Valve GP- Modulating should 
be tested to see whether it is properly working. Normally, an electrical test is performed. This test 
measures the current on the solenoid, and if it is between specific values, we believe it works 
properly. However, this test can only help us decide if the Valve GP- Modulating is working 
electrically; it does not give a clue about whether or not it is working physically. This means the 
Valve GP- Modulating could have physical damage, but the electrical test would not be able to 
detect it, and since we don't test the whole transmission, old valves were assembled without 
physical testing after revision. That is a huge risk and could lead to other part failures and costly 
and inefficient rework. Therefore, to detect any kind of physical damage, one of our engineers 
designed a test tool for the Valve GP- Modulating. 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
The basic logic behind the test is to simulate the working principle of the Valve GP- Modulating 
by using this tool. After connecting the Valve GP- Modulating, the pressure gauge and the 
signal generator to each other, the voltage is applied to the modulation valve in increasing 
incremental steps. Then the pressure gauge is checked to see whether the pressure is 
increasing step by step and if it reaches the peak point (about 400 PSI). lf it reaches the peak 
point, it is working properly. lf the Valve GP- Modulating couldn't make it to the peak pressure 
point, that means it is not working properly. You can view the process in the attached video. 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
1. Create the test tool according to the attached engineering print.
2. Connect the Valve GP- Modulating to the test tool. (Tank and pump lines are specified.)

4.0 Benefits 
• Eliminates the risk of reassembling physically damaged valves;
• Prevents damage to other parts of equipment that would be caused by faulty and
damaged valves;
• Decreases rework cost and time if the valve is found to be physically damaged;
• Delivers higher quality and more efficient service back to the customer.
