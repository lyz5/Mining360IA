1.0 Introduction  
 
Removing the camshaft from the C32 engines of D11 fusion tractors has always been a time-
consuming and high-risk activity due to the movement of heavy parts, making it costly and 
dangerous. To replace the camshaft, it is necessary to remove the implement pumps or the fan 
and front mask of the equipment. 
 
The need to remove or tilt the front mask with the engine radiator further increases the complexity 
and dangers associated with this activity. By exploring new strategies and techniques for 
camshaft removal, we aim to not only increase operational efficiency but also ensure the safety of 
technicians, minimize costs associated with the activity, and eliminate the need to move the 
radiator. 
 
After the maintenance and repair of D11 fusion tractors through the implementation of this 
procedure, it was found that there was not only a reduction in risks but also costs with labor and 
machine downtime. 
 
This procedure will present the removal of the camshaft, identifying its points and associated 
challenges. Finally, the expected benefits of this approach include increased safety for the 
technicians involved, a significant reduction in maintenance costs, and a significant increase in 
the efficiency of maintenance operations for tractors of the D11 KSN series.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
After the release of service letter PS47495 for the D11 KSN, it was identified that there was an urgent 
need to optimize the repair process in order to minimize equipment downtime. In June 2023, 
measurements, and evaluations of the new configuration of the machine's basement components 
were carried out. This included the relocation of groups and pumps in the new series, ensuring 
enough space for the shaft removal through the rear of the engine. In order to speed up the process 
and ensure quick availability of the equipment, the procedure was implemented as planned, resulting 
in a 2/3 reduction in repair time. The collaboration between engineering and field technicians was 
fundamental to develop and implement this improvement. Due to the nature of the adaptation of the 
procedures, this implementation can be easily replicated in other dealerships, as it is a process re-
adaptation that does not require additional tools. 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
1. Increased Safety: 
• Eliminates the step of tilting or removing the radiator guard, reducing the risk of accidents 
and injuries for technicians. 
 
2. Reduced Machine Downtime: 
• Repair time for camshaft removal is reduced by 2/3, from 80 to 24 hours. 
• This means that the machine is available for operation more quickly, increasing 
productivity and efficiency. 
 
3. Reduced Labor Costs: 
• The reduction in repair time leads to a 2/3 reduction in labor costs. 
• This means that the company saves money on each repair, which can lead to significant 
savings over time.
