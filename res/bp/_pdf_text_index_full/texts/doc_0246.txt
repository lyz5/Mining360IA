1.0 Introduction  
 
Due to their irregular ge ometry, weight, and quantity by e quipment, the track s hoes are one of the 
most difficult and awkward components to handle during the track assembly and disa ssembly 
operations on Track Type Tractors. The track shoe weights vary according to the model, ranging from 
30kg (D6 series) to 85kg (D11 series) in large groups from 41 to 53 track shoes in the D11 series and 
D9 series respectively. This results in a slow, risky , and stressful process for the technical personnel  
involved in the handling of this component. 
 
This Best Practice provides the design of a tool that allows easy manipulation of the track shoes in 
any process that involves lifting, turning and moving of this. 
 
Editor’s Note:  The use of this installation tool and the processes discussed within this Best Practice 
is not a substitute for applicable laws, standards and regulations and does not alter or limit the 
obligation of individuals who utilize the tool to fully comply with federal, state and local law and 
prudent safety measures relating to the use of lifting devices. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
For the lifting and handling of the track shoes in any install and removal operations; a lift chain 
assembly is currently used (see Fig. 1).

The new proposed tool is designed to prevent the drawbacks described previously.  It consists of 
two symmetrical assemblies reinforced with AISI 1045 steel, three teeth which form a clamping 
grip in ASTM A36 steel connected by a AISI 1045 steel plate .  These two assemblies are joined 
by an ASTM – 1020 steel bar-tube telescopic system, which allows an opening and closing range 
of the clamps from 24 to 36 inches maximum (measured from the bottom of each clamp) covering 
all the models of track shoes that are within a length of this range. The three teeth of the clamp 
secure the track shoe on the lateral profile of the main grouser, allowing easy 180° rotation on the 
axis of the grouser by means of a ¾ inch pin (AISI 1045) which connects the clamp to the lateral 
assemblies. 
 
At the upper ends of the tool we can see ter minals made of 1045 steel sheet having ¾" diameter 
bore, as anchor points. These are located to permit the installation and precise auto center of the 
bolt shackle threaded up to 5/8".  This way, the closing pin of these can easily turn over the bore, 
always having symmetry between the two anchor points.  F orming 45° to 60° angles for the 
opening of a 36" and 24" tool respectively. Two steel wire slings of 26.5" long with a low 
resistance of 300kg each, close the lifting triangle by means of a master link or shackle of 7/8" 
(see Figure 3). 
 
On the lateral plates, the tool consists of rectangular rings on a 3/8"  rod for the optional 
installation of ropes as lifting guides and it also serves as clamping handles for manipulating the 
tool. 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
 Fabricate the tool according to the drawings , taking into account the selection of the materials 
given. (See attached drawings). 
 Select the lifting accessories (steel slings, shackles, etc.) as recommended by l oading 
regulations listed below, taking into account the availability of local suppliers: 
o Total Lifting load 120kg 
o Correct length of sling to keep lifting angle less than 90° for the largest shoe 
o S ymmetrical Lifting 
o 26.5 " long steel slings 
o 300kg tension maximum steel slings (each) 
o Screwed pin lower shackles (See Fig. 3) 
o Pin upper shackl e, nut and safety pin (See Fig. 3) . It can also use a master link 
assembly 
 Before attaching the track shoe, check the condition of the tool for cracks, misuse or any 
defect that do not guarantee its design properties and proper operation.  Also check the label 
or specification plate, making sure the loading capacity covers the weight of the shoe to be 
hoisted.

4.0 Benefits 
 
 P ractical design easy to manufacture. 
 Economically feasible. Tool cost USD $500 plus lifting attachments costs USD $250 for a total 
cost of USD $750. 
 Light tool and easy to transport, as it is easily detachable. The heaviest part is the sub-
assembly, which is only 8kg (including clamp, see Fig. 4). 
 C lamping claws that allow the shoe to rotate 80 °  (both ways) is on the axis of the grouser .  
This allows you to easily align the shoe with the master link bores in the installation procedure 
of the bolts. 
 Auto-lock of the telescopic system by symmetric lifting which causes force components into 
compression between the pipe and the bar ensuring the safe fastening of the shoe. 
 Its telescopic design allows to cover shoe models from 24" to 36" inches wide on single 
grouser shoe. 
 It requires less physical effort from the technical personnel for its handling in the disassembly 
and assembly shoe process, making them safer, comfortable and faster. 
 It allows the installation of guiding ropes for a greater control of the movements when lifting, 
and at the same time these rectangular rings serve as handles for manipulating the sub -
assemblies of the tool.
