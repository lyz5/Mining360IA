1.0 Introduction  
 
A new method to improve the protection of parts from corrosion has been implemented at 
Machining Workshop in Ferreyros S.A.  This product is called VCI (Vapor Corrosion Inhibitors) 
Stretch Film. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
 
Before the use of VCI Stretch Film, the method of protecting parts after machining was to apply a 
liquid rust inhibitor then cover with shrink-wrap.   After test and analysis of the cost versus benefit, 
the machining center now uses the VCI Stretch film.  See Figure 1. 
 
 
              
      
                                                           
 
Figure 1. Products used before this best practice. 4A Rust Preventive (First) and Stretch Film 
without anticorrosive protection (Second)

Testing of the VCI Stretch Film as shown below in Figures 2 and 3 demonstrate that the 
material is very effective in the prevention of corrosion. By using the new film protection, it is 
not necessary to also use the 4A Rust Preventive because the VCI is incorporated into the VCI 
Stretch Film. Finally, this product is suitable for rust protection on iron based metals and 
products composed of different metals.  The overall thickness of the film is 1 millimeter 
standard. 
 
In the figure 2, half of the crankshaft was protected just with VCI Stretch Film.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Dual action: Contact and VCI protection                        
• Excellent clarity, cling, stretch and strength. 
• No messy residues to remove 
• Eliminate the use of 4A Rust Preventive and the Stretch Film without anticorrosive protection. 
• Easy inspection and identification of shipping labels/bar codes (Work order). 
• Reduce the cost of protection in 10.68% by year  (See the table 1). 
 
 
 
 
Table 1.  Cost between Stretch Film without VCI vs VCI Stretch Film 
 
The first four rows in the table above represent material costs of previous process using inhibitor 
and standard wrap. 
 
The center rows are the costs to re-wash and clean parts when they are ready to be used. 
 
The bottom of the table is the costs and savings using VCI Stretch Film.
