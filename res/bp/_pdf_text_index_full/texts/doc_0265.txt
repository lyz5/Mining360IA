1.0 Introduction  
 
A tool was developed to eliminate the damage to the aftercooler housing (P/N 210-2603 & 210-
2581) when removing adapters (P/N 371-5945 & 371-5944.) Due to the small size of the 
adapters, it is often difficult to remove them without the risk of causing damage to other 
components. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Install the developed tool inside the space between the aftercooler core and the adapter. Make 
sure to adjust the nails and pull the adapter.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
Design for the tool is shown below. Further dimensions are shown in the attachments section 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
   
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Through the implementation of this tool, the following benefits have been observed: 
 
 Extraction time reduction: 
o Before: 1 hour 
o Now:  8 minutes 
 Significant safety increase by reducing the risk of hand injuries to technicians 
 Significant reduction to adapter and aftercooler housing damage
