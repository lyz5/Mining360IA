1.0 Introduction  
 
The dryer filter of the air conditioning system is a component composed of desiccant particles 
whose purpose is to retain solid impurities and absorb moisture.   To identify when the filter needs 
to be changed , just examine the inlet and outlet pipes of the filter, the temperature must be the 
same in both tubes, these checks are carried out  in the preventive maintenance of the 793F . An 
analysis of the downtime drivers of the PM process identified that a large amount of time is spent 
with refrigerant dryer replacement, due to the complexity of the component connections. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This BP provides information for installing a quick connect coupling for the refrigerant dryer (257-
3226) of the air conditioning system of  a 793F.  Without a quick connect coupling, the refrigerant 
gas is drained (exhausted) by not having a check valve. Thus, it is always necessary to carry out 
the refrigerant recovery fully, which increases the maintenance time and costs.  
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
The implementation of this BP consists of replacing the rigid piping interconnecting the filter dr yer 
with a quick connect coupling hose system. 
 
 
Figure 01 – Refrigerant Dryer as Shipped on 793F. 
 
 
• Collect the refrigerant gas using the PN 285-7352 tool, remove the PN 357-2994 tube and 
the 257-3226 filter dryer.
