1.0 Introduction  
 
Dealers can improve their Contamination Control Program by expanding the use of plastic caps 
and plugs. During repair, every machine component or system that is opened/disassembled has 
to be protected to avoid contamination. Caterpillar supplies a variety of plastic caps and plugs 
through the normal parts system. This practice leads to Contamination Control practices that 
have proven to effectively contribution to component durability. 
 
Our task as maintenance personnel is to embrace and implement the correct repair/service 
procedures that will ensure the best possible results in terms of repair quality and efficiency.  
 
A dealer and customer partnered to launch a strong project to introduce and implement all 
necessary Contamination Control initiatives. This effort started to give positive results that can be 
seen in both component durability and failures reduction. Implementing this aspect of 
Contamination Control has aided this dealer in achieving 5 Stars in the Caterpillar Contamination 
Control Audit program during the last two years, making it the first mining maintenance operation 
to achieve this recognition.    
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This Best Practice facilitates the supply of plastic plugs and caps (P&C) by locating them close to 
all repairs in progress. A Latin American dealer designed and fabricated two portable cabinets that 
can be easily moved to the repair bays. The cabinets are stocked with a complete set of plastic 
plugs and caps for quick access.

Plastic plugs and caps are stored in plastic bags and the cabinet doors are sealed to eliminate any external contamination. 
 
     
  
 
All P&C compartments are identified and replenishment is the responsibility of the on-site warehouse. 
 
When a repair is in progress, the technicians move the cabinet close to the work area to have the 
P&C close and easily available when needed. Whenever a compartment/system/component is 
open, the appropriate plastic plugs and caps are selected and installed over the openings. 
 
Warehouse personnel check the P&C Cabinet quantities as part of their daily check routine.  
 
All shop personnel follow this practice and its use is constantly supervised through the 
Supervisors, Staff Personnel, and Project Manager.  
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
1. Based on the magnitude of your operation (number of bays and typical workload) define 
the number of cabinets needed.

2. Fabricate the plugs & caps cabinet. The cost to fabricate each cabinet locally was 
approximately US $1,500 
 
3. Define the set of plastic plugs and caps to maintain in the cabinet, both part numbers and 
quantity. You can follow the recommendations included in Section 6.0. 
 
4. Order a complete set of Plugs & Caps. Define the inventory limits for replenishment. Make 
the warehouse personnel responsible for replenishment. 
 
5. Always use new Plugs & Caps. Experience shows that recycling caps is not worth the 
effort and introduces possibilities for contamination. 
 
6. Prepare the cabinets and stock with the plugs and caps. Assign them to the shop. 
 
7. Train personnel in the use of the plugs and caps and the cabinet system. 
 
8. Instruct supervisors to reinforce the need to properly utilize these protective devices. 
 
9. Make shop supervisors responsible for the maintenance of the carts and their correct use. 
 
10. Include the carts in all Shop Facilities & Equipment Audits.       
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Contamination Control 
o Ensures proper storage of open machine systems / components. 
o Eliminate oil spillages, improve cleanliness of working area. 
o Contributes to component durability. 
 
• Work quality - by having plugs and caps easily visible and available, reduces occurrences of 
work being performed without caps. 
 
• Work Efficiency - contributes to improve work efficiency (Mean Time to Repair). Items are 
available for immediate use and don’t have to be searched for. 
 
• Environmental Control - prevents oil spills, contributes to a better and cleaner environment. 
 
• Image - contributes to enhance the dealer image, shows professional work done following all 
recommended procedures. 
 
• Easy to manage. This supply system for the plugs and caps is very visible and easy to 
supervise. Everyone can see it in the shop.
