1.0 Introduction  
 
In January 2020, the new fleet of 994K loaders started operating at the S11D mine, northern Brazil, 
where the extraction modal is heavily based on the mobile crushers feeding system, in a system 
called Loading and Carriage. 
 
The 04 994K loaders operating at the S11D mine started their operation as part of a customer effort 
to increase iron ore production, and increase the flexibility of operations, which at that time were 
mainly composed of TRUCKLESS. 
 
Since the beginning of the operation, GAPS in the application of the assets were identified, especially 
in the turbochargers and other Engine components that suffer from the lack of adequate lubrication 
during starts. The pre-lubrication system automatically enters before starts when one of the following 
conditions is not present: 
 
• Engine oil pressure more than or equal to 3 kPa. (4 psi) 
• Engine run for the last (02) two minutes. 
• The Coolant temperature is more than 50° C (122° F) 
 
The improper use of the pre-lubrication system can cause catastrophic failures in the turbocharger 
and accelerated wear in the engine's moving components.  
 
As part of an initiative by the SOTREQ Engineering and Product Support team, to reduce the impacts 
caused by an operation of this nature, some training plans were implemented to identify possible 
problems in the system: 
 
• Training Plans. 
• Operational Monitoring.  
• Negotiations with the Maintenance Team.  
 
We identified a failure in the electric engine, Circuit Breakers, and leaks in the pumps. Tests are 
currently available for the pump, relay, and electric engine power supply, however, there are no 
actual tests for the engine or information available about them. 
 
The new approach was made on the Dealer, focusing, on the Project, People Development, and 
Process. 
 
o PROJECT: To efficiently control the service life and carry out tests of the components of the 
pre-lubrication system. 
o PEOPLE DEVELOPMENT : Identification of operators, with a higher incidence of pre-
lubrication system bypass, relating the records to the Operator's Time/Registration, and 
weekly handling of alarms and analysis of VIMS  memories with the person responsible for 
the operation. 
o PROCESS: Identification of system failures and operation problems, and elaboration of a 
procedure that seeks the moment to replace system components before failures occur so 
that potential risks can be prevented.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Some equipment fleet 994K Wheel Loaders in S11D mine had several failures in the pre-lubrication 
system. These failures can be confused with normal situations, in which the system is not activated. 
This situation is common. An electric engine failure will not generate failure codes. 
 
Early premature failures in the system's engine have made it difficult to solve and detect problems in 
the lubrication system, as there are no parameters to define the failure. No failure codes show the 
engine has a problem. 
 
Failures in electric engines are not always simple to detect and solve, the coils do not have resistance 
close to 0 Ω or overload (indicating open circuit) or close to 0 Ω (0.1 Ω indicating short circuit). There 
is no parameter for us to effectively define that the electric engine has a problem.

Analyzing the structure of a new machine it is possible to define the default parameters to be 
followed, as the Failure Analysis consists of defining default values and verifying their evolution over 
time. 
 
The resistance of a cable and a coil can change quickly as they are used, however, the previous 
analysis of this information can serve as parameters to be compared. 
 
After analyzing the resistance of the defined points, the same points must be checked on the 
equipment every 250 hours, based on the reference values, the changes in each point are visible. 
 
This data is used to define the best time to replace system components before failure occurs and to 
be able to quantify the useful life of each component.

After carrying out the measurements and setting the default values, the resistance of the points must 
be checked every 250 hours, the measurement procedure takes approximately 5 minutes.  
 
All measurements must be made with the equipment off, the parking brake applied, the main switch 
open and the circuit breaker output cable removed. 
 
It is possible to define the circuit activity and the time to replace the components of the system. In the 
example, it is possible to verify that the resistance of the electric motors of the 994K (PC30 and 
PC32) is very low as specified, not enough to be considered short circuits, but low enough to define 
that the motors are not working and must be replaced.

4.0 Benefits 
 
Some benefits can be listed, after applying measurements to the 994K: 
 
 Feedback from mechanics on simple and effective testing of system components. 
 Removal of components with failures not identified by the machine system and scheduling 
the replacement of components that are out of the standard. 
 Assurance that the pre-lubrication system is working. 
 Accurate lifetime measurement and replacement planning for damaged components.

The measurement requires a PN 257-9140 Multimeter
