1.0 Introduction  
 
This Best Practice reduces possible work and material accidents by changing the position of this 
switch reducing the height by approximately 30 cm (11,8" Inches) in relation to original position. 
 
In the Brazil North region, the median height of operators is frequently under the world average, 
sometimes the support structure is required for improving the ergonomics condition and mitigating 
risks for the employees. 
 
Implementing this Best Practice makes it possible for more people to access the shutdown switch 
safely and more efficiently. 
 
 
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Original position of the Shutdown Switch – Source: SOTREQ/Giesi Nascimento.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
For reducing the difficulty of accessing the switch by the operator, SOTREQ moved the shutdown 
switch to the front right side (near the access ladder) of the CAT 793D mining truck, thus eliminating 
the use of any auxiliary device to access the switch. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
New shutdown switch position – Source: SOTREQ/Giesi Nascimento.

4.0 Benefits 
 
This change position, reduce the distance range by 16% (1.88 m to 1.57 m) by the 
operator/technician who is working on the maintenance of the equipment. 
 
Additionally, a time reduction (New Time: Approximately 30 seconds) was observed after 
implementation of this Best Practice: 
 
• Productivity – Time Reduction for Switch Actuation. 
 
• Personal Safety – Engine Shutdown process simplified in risk situations. 
 
 
Testing after installation: 
 
• Operator Height 1.51 m. 
 
Before the change: Difficulty accessing and viewing the switch
