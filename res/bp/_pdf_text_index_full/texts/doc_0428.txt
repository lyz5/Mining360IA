1.0 Introduction 
D
ifferent types of pump groups come to our workshop for maintenance or overhaul. The pumps 
sent by the customer are unloaded from the transport vehicle with the forklift. Most of the time the 
pumps are placed inside the cage or on the pallet. In this way, it is shipped between workshops. 
2
.0 Best Practice Description 
T
he pumps that come to our workshop are placed on a cage or pallet and moved between the 
workshops by pallet truck or forklift. During this delivery phase, the pumps are not stationary in 
the cage or on the pallet. At this stage, situations such as overturning and falling may occur in the 
pumps. These accidents can result in high costs. This study was carried out because the pumps 
did not stay in a fixed position in the cage before this transportation apparatus was made and 
they were damaged during transportation between workshops. A new tool has been made to 
prevent these situations. In the new process, an apparatus was made to carry the pumps. This 
carrying apparatus is suitable for pumps of all sizes. The pumps are placed on the transport 
apparatus and fixed with bolts. In this way, the pump will not be damaged even if events such as 
overturning or falling occur in the pump carrying apparatus. 
3
.0 Implementation Steps 
S
tep 1: Pumps are prepared for shipping between workshops. 
S
tep 2: Pump carrying apparatus is prepared.
