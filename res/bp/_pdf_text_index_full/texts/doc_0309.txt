1. Introduction  
 
Ferreyros has developed a tool to improve the assembly process in 3500 engines, by installing 
the piston and connecting rod. The tool lifts the piston and connecting rod, reducing the stress 
applied by the technician to lift the component, and places the piston and connecting rod directly 
into the cylinder block. Caterpillar does not have a tool to lift the piston and connecting rod in 
3500 engines. 
 
 
2. Best Practice Description 
         
The lifting of the piston and connecting rod for installation in 3500 engines was very manual by 
the technician. It involved loading the piston and connecting rod from his car to block cylinder. 
 
The safety of this procedure could be improved, and it also introduces back fatigue for the 
technician.

The piston and connecting rod are safely installed in the cylinder block without losing its stability. 
This tool minimizes risks during the operation for the piston and connecting rod and the 
technician. 
 
3. Implementation Steps 
 
• Design a tool. 
• EDITOR’S NOTE:  The side of the tooling will make contact with the piston rings and create 
potential stress on the piston ring when you move the piston / rod from the vertical into the 
horizontal position to install the group into the cylinder liner. Stops could be incorporated in 
this tooling to eliminate tooling / ring contact. 
• Caterpillar also recommends developing a locking mechanism to lock grip when holding 
piston. 
   
4. Benefits 
 
• Improve operator safety. 
• I mprove process efficiency. 
• Prevents damage to the piston and connecting rod during install. 
 
5. Resources Required 
 
• Minimal expense to fabricate the tool.
