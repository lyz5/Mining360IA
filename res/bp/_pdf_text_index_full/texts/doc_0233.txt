1.0 Introduction 
 
Brake operation is very important and any problem in the assembly can potentially lead to braking 
issues in the field and at the Finning Chile (CRC Antofagasta) before installing the brake group on 
the spindle, pressure tests are performed on the service and park brake release systems for 
leakage. 
 
One hydraulic pump is used, to supply regulated oil pressure to the service and park brake oil 
supply ports.

2.1 Install hoses in the parking and service brake ports.

 2.7 Apply and release parking brake pressure (st eps 2.5 - 2.6) again and repeat this sequence at

2.8 To check for leakage, leave the parking brake pressure applied (close off parking brake valve

pressure to the service brake piston port per the Caterpillar specifications.

3.3 External to the brake hose. 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Quickly identifies any potential leak source. 
• Checks application of the park and service brake pistons. 
• Avoid unnecessary field repairs and machine downtime. 
       
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• Test Tooling (fabricated) 
• Staff training.
