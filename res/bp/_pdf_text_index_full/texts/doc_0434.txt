1.0 Introduction  
 
The Installation of the sprocket segments on TTT is a task in which we must be especially careful 
when tightening the nuts because of the potential safety risks to the technical personnel performing 
the task. Technicians are exposed to pinch points, arm fatigue, and potential tool slippage. 
 
Currently for the torque procedure of the bolts a lever resting on the final drive is used in order to hold 
the nuts and ensure correct torque is applied.  This exposes the technician to a possible safety issues 
should the lever slip Fig.1.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This BP coves the fabrication and use of a simple tool to hold the sprocket segment bolts (D10N, 
D10R & D10T) replacing the typically used lever, simplifying the process, and reducing the 
associated risks of sprocket segment changes. 
 
Geometrically, the tool has a “U” shape, in one of its ends, it has a 1 ¼ " and 4 1/2" diameter bar 
in length and in the other end it has a diameter of 2.4" and a length of 1.4" bored concentrically 
with the silhouette of the head of the bolt to be installed. The two ends are joined by a 1" plate 
triangle shaped having rounded edges due to the difference in diameters of the two ends. Made 
entirely of ASTM A36 steel, this tool also has a handle so that the technician can comfortably hold 
it with his hand when installing or transporting. 
 
In Fig. 4. We can see the way it holds the head of the bolt, and it takes advantage of the 
segments teeth in order to lock itself and thus it reduces the exposure of the technicians to 
possible accidents, and it improves the torque of the bolts reducing the installation time by up to 
40 %.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
•   Manufacturing of the tool. 
• 
 Operation of the tool for applying torque to the screw’s segments sprocket (see Figure 4) 
 
1. From the inside of the sprocket, place the largest diameter end of the tool in order to hold the 
bolt head with the bored silhouette. 
2. Make sure the opposite end of the tool (1 ¼ " bar) is placed in one of the spaces between the 
sprocket teeth. 
3. install the torque meter along with the adapters required to tighten the nut on the outside of the 
sprocket, make sure when rotating the tool that it is locked with one of the sprocket teeth 
thereby preventing the rotation of the bolt.

• It improves the torque application. 
• There is higher availability of the equipment.
