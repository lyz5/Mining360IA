MINING DEALER BEST PRACTICE  
 
1.0 Introduction  
 
Borusan Makina Kazakhstan has designed the tool to easily and safely assemble/disassemble 
the bearings of the C15 & C18 engines camshaft with one technician.  
 
Before creating this tool, it involved two technicians and took about 40 minutes to assemble the 
bearings of the camshaft. It was inconvenient and required more effort and man-hours, mostly 
because a manual tool was used and it had to measure the bearings one by one so that the oil 
grooves would match during assembling.  
 
Thus, this new tool or stand enables to safely and simultaneously assemble/ disassemble the 
bearings of the camshaft and reduce the efforts spent and optimize the man hours allocated.  
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This stand consists of the table, 7 x pressing shafts, 2 x studs, 6 x cylinder head mounting bolts 
(Part No. 6I4025), 1 x retainer, hydraulic press and additional adjusting fixture (Part No. 4N4941 – 
Adjustment Rod (1 pc) & 6B6684 – Nut (2 pcs)). 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
The Table of the stand – the main part of the stand 
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
The stand in use (the Bearings Assembly Process).  
 
1. Prepare the hydraulic press for use.  
 
2. Prepare the tools required to assemble the bearings.

This stand can also be used to disassemble bearings, simply reverse sequence of work. For

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Easy and safe bearings assembly/ disassembly processes. 
• Reduced efforts spent by the technician 
• Optimized man hours for these processes.
