1.0 Introduction  
 
The Operation and Maintenance Manual, SEBU8242, “789D and 789D XQ Off -Highway Trucks”, 
Engine Air Pre-Cleaner – Clean, recommends cleaning the pre-cleaner bowl with compressed air 
pressure and only when required. This compressed air cleaning process disperses dust i n the 
environment and is not very efficient for cleaning the pre- cleaner: it contributes to the air filter 
clogging more quickly, increasing the changes of air filters that are changed by condition. 
 
Due to these conditions and  the risk of contamination by dust in the intake lines , a tool was 
developed with parts of the primary filter and parts of the secondary filter of the truck to isolate the 
intake lines, which prevents dust and water ingres s during the preventive maintenanc e washing 
process. This condition leads to better cleaning of the pre -cleaner, thus increasing the estimated 
life of the primary and secondary air filters. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Tool made with parts of the secondary filter and the handle of the primary air filter of the 789D off-
highway truck by cutting the parts that fit in the air intake line  and creating the tool to isolate the 
line and seal the entrance of impurities and water from the air intake lines  when cleaning the pre-
cleaner. 
 
 
Picture 1: Tool to isolate air intake lines for pre-cleaner washing process. 
 
The truck used as example in this Best Practice is a 789D SPD prefix, but  the idea can be 
implemented in every machine that has similar pattern of air filters and pre-cleaner.

• Install tool in the air intake line;

• Wash the air pre-cleaner with water at low pressure. Dry it with compressed air According to the 
procedure written in Operation and Maintenance Manual, SEBU8242, “789D and 789D XQ Off-
Highway Trucks”, Engine Air Pre-Cleaner – Clean. 
 
For 789Ds, fabricate two tools, one for the right side and one for the left side.  For other machines in 
which this Best Practice can be implemented, fabricate a number of tools that requires the shortest time 
to complete the washing process. 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Pre-Cleaner is cleaned in window of opportunity during the machine-washing process, 
eliminating downtime in this specific task (saving of 2 labor hours every 250 hours PM). 
• Contamination control in air intake lines. 
• Less contaminant accumulation on pre-cleaner inner walls with consequent. 
I. Longer service life of air filters; and 
II. Less probability of air filter restriction events. 
• Less dispersion of dust in the working environment. 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• Two used primary air filters 
• Two used secondary air filters 
• .Measuring tape 
• Silicone 
• Waterproof tape 
• Drill, 6mm drill bit.
