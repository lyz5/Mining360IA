1.0 Introduction 
T
he MD6420-16 drilling rigs in operation at the Conceição Mine use the PN467-7871 roller to 
guide the pulley carrier. These rollers have been constantly breaking during operation and falling 
from the mast reaching equipment components. 
T
he Roller pulley carrier guides presented the first failure after approximately 6000 hours of 
operation and after this failure, consecutive failures have begun in this component. In some 
cases, the roller failure has resulted in damage to beam I which has the function of along with 
rollers keep the pulley carrier aligned. 
A
fter checking the fixing, roller alignment, adjustment of pulldown cables and correct adjustment 
of the auto tensioner of the pulldown cables, it was identified that the failures of the roller guides 
of the pulley carrier were still recurrent.  
Las
t year we received two MD6310 in Brazil and we realized that the guide system of the pulley 
carrier was different from the MD6420-16 equipment. In view of this, The Sotreq engineering 
team carried out a study and realized that it would be possible to install the sliding guide system 
used in the MD6310 in the MD6420-16 equipment, making some adaptations. 
2.
0 Best Practice Description 
T
he Project consists of the replacement of the roller guides of the pulley carrier currently installed 
in the equipment by a set of sliding guides that uses polyurethane guides.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Installation process 
Inspection of beams I that received the sliding guides. If they are damaged replacement of the 
beams will be required.  
 
To install the improvement, it is necessary to rework the support 527-8190 of the sliding guides 
as shown in the figure below.  
After reworking the support, it needs to weld in the guide carrier and install the other parts. 
all other items is installed similar of the MD6310 equipment.

Estimated time of application of the set of 24 hours of work, 3 days.  
Note: To perform the task it is recommended to remove the mast for greater agility and ease of 
services to be performed. 
 
Care for release of equipment after installation of the project 
 
Check the conditions of Hoist and pulldown cables, if they are near of end lifespan replace them. 
Perform inspections on Hoist and pulldown cables in the 250-hour equipment preventive 
maintenance. 
Check pressure adjustment of the auto tensioning system of the pulldown cables and after 
release perform check every 500 hours of operation. 
 
Inspection  
 
For the project, it was defined that inspection would be necessary every 250 hours of operation. 
This inspection consists of checking the wear of the sliding shim and the fixing of all components 
present in the new guide installed.

4.0 Benefits 
 
The use of the new guides results: 
 
In a greater contact with the I-beam, thus distributing the load and unloaded force on the beam. 
Increasement MTBR 
Better operation security  
Decrease the risk of parts falling
