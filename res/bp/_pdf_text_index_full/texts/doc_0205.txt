1.0 Introduction  
 
A new process to remove rust from a fuel tank has been implemented at Machining Workshop in 
Ferreyros S.A using a chemical rust solvent instead of sandblasting.  The process reduces cost 
to clean fuels tanks by 70%. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The focus of this Best Practice is a new method to remove the rust from a fuel tank by applying a 
chemical solvent (see the picture 1).  Solvent selected does not have any environmental or safety 
hazards, is very effective, and can be reused. This operation takes two hours for each side of fuel 
tank and can be performed by one technician.  
 
Dealer uses Evapo-rust.  Evapo-rust information and specifications is ready available on internet 
and product available through multiple sources. 
 
 
                                                                              
Picture 1. Evapo-rust barrel liquid (55 gallons) 
 
Our former process to clean rust from fuel tanks was to cut a plate out of the fuel tank for 
access and sandblasting the inside of the tank to remove rust.  The process required up to 
four technicians (two welders, two handlers) and took a long time (five days approximately). 
 
With the new process using solvent, time to clean the fuel tanks was reduced from five days down 
to 1.5 days.  Costs include the cost of the solvent was reduced from $6500 to $818.
