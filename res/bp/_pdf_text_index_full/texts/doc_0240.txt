1.0 Introduction  
 
Many dealers still transport parts and materials delivered to the Component Rebuild Centers 
(CRC) manually using hand pallet trucks.  Some dealers have replaced the manual pallet 
handlers with electric pallet trucks.   
This Best Practice is an economic comparison of “manual” versus “electric” material handling 
equipment.  This Best Practice comes from Ferreyros – Lima, Peru. 
 
                                      
 
 
 
 
 
 
                                        
 
                            
 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Tests were conducted over a controlled period to measure the speed of delivery of parts baskets 
to the CRC (average distance 50 Meters) using electric pallet trucks versus manual pallet 
handlers.  The results showed that the electric pallet trucks were 2.3 times faster than manual 
transport.   
There were other advantages including: 
• Less parts handling staff fatigue. 
• Reduced / eliminated back pain incidents. 
• Quick delivery means fewer parts need to be staged  in the bay – delivered when needed 
(just in time). 
• Reduced technician “wait times”. 
• Handlers were small and maneuverable – able to wor k in narrow spaces. 
Investment analysis was based on “time recovered” in transporting materials from the CRC parts 
receiving area to the work bays and back to receiving.  
Dealer realized a 44% NPV return on investment and cost recovery period of 2 years 11 months.

Savings are then compared to purchase, owning and operating costs, taxes, depreciation, etc. to 
determine a return on investment.

• Reduced time required to deliver parts to work bay s. 
• Reduced technician “waiting time” for parts. 
• Reduced use of bay space to stage parts – just in time delivery process. 
• Reduced fatigue and back pain for parts handling s taff.
