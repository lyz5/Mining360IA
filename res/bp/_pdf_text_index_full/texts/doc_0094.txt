1.0 Introduction  
 
The maintenance and repair of the sensors of the front jacks to the activity in the MD6640 is done 
at a height of approximately 3 meters in a condition of difficult access, without a fixed anchor 
point, without a support base appropriate, exposing the technician to a high risk of falling during 
the execution of the activity.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The jack sensor box was removed and reinstalled in an easily accessible position for technicians 
now located at the bottom of the machine, allowing ground level access, thus eliminating the risk 
of falling from work at height. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
1.With the equipment stopped and the equipment's electrical control system blocked, remove the   
front Jack Sensor Box from their original factory position. 
 
 
 
 
 
2. Install the 2 front Jack Sensor Box in the bottom of the machine close to the ground, as shown 
in the figure below.
