1.0 Introduction  
 
This best practice is dealing with Component life management.  The current process was inefficient 
and resulted in high levels of variability.  By implementing this Best Practice, there will be fewer 
repairs after failure and longer component life.  This is important to both the dealer and customer, as 
it will improve customer satisfaction and profitability through reduced down time. 
 
The key benefit from this Best Practice is improved quality.  This Best Practice is not available for 
front suspension testing.  
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The current process at our CRC is that rear suspensions are not being tested for leaks after rebuild. 
Components are stripped cleaned and inspected and then stored until approval is received for 
assembly. 
 
We found that we required a test bench in order to ensure that the cylinders would hold pressure, 
were rebuilt correctly, and would meet projected life.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
- Determine area in shop to be designated for the c ylinder testing bench 
- Assure the area has adequate space and access for  equipment to move the component 
- Confirm the layout for equipment and make changes  to the shop to install 
- Promote change feedback as new operation problems  may surface with the procedure 
- Train employees to use the new procedures/equipme nt 
- Review process/equipment performance once establi sh 
- Make any adjustment(s) as needed 
- Drawings are attached in this best practice
