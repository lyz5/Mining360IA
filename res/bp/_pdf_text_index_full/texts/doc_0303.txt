1.0 Introduction  
 
For dealers that rebuild their own cylinder packs, piston pins often meet reuse criteria and only 
require a surface buff to clean the pin.  Ferreyros uses a simple tool to hold the pin along with a 
bench grinder and 8T-7748 buffing wheel to clean piston pins. 
 
 
 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The dealer fabricated tool to hold the piston pin w hile buffing the surface consists of two plastic 
handles and a threaded metal rod.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Fabricate tools sized for the engine models required.  See SEBF8051 for reuse criteria, 
dimensions, and cleaning procedure. 
 
4.0 Benefit 
 
• Allows smooth and even polishing – better quality 
• Easier to handle pin in the polishing process- bet ter efficiency 
• Technicians’ hands are away from the polishing whe el 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
Minimal expense to fabricate the tool.
