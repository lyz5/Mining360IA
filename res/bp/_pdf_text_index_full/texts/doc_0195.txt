1.0 Introduction  
 
During the repair process in the CRC machining cent er, workflow interruptions and delays can 
occur due to a variety of reasons, such as issues w ith tooling, parts, logistics, technicians, etc.  
Quick and effective response by shop management to resolve the interruptions maintain the 
workflow and minimizes downtime of equipment and pe rsonnel, reducing the cost of downtime 
and improving the repair Turn Around Time (TAT). 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
The Ferreyros CRC machining center uses a wireless system to alert quality inspectors and 
supervisors of any problem in the production workflow process.  This gives the operator the ability 
to call for the required assistance at the moment a  problem occurs. For this purpose, the 
technicians need a system that allows instant commu nication with the proper resource to solve 
the problem and allow the continuity of production. 
 
The machining workstations also require the transpo rt of large parts into and out of the 
workstation.  The wireless system is used to alert the parts runners when a job is completed to 
removed the completed component and bring in the next job. 
 
This Best Practice presents a wireless system of alerts that allows direct communication between 
the workstation operator and the supervisor, quality inspector, or runner who will attend the call. 
 
The system is set up so that each workstation has a  wireless paging device that allows the 
operator to page the Supervisor, Quality Inspector, or Runner. 
 
 
The team receives the call through a wireless wrist  band receiver indicating attention is needed 
and which work station.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
• Define the work area zone and number or work stati ons in the zone 
• Define the responders for each zone: Supervisors, Quality Inspectors, Runners 
• Acquire hardware and software and install 
• Train personnel 
• Implement 
 
The wireless system project was conducted as Caterpillar Production Systems project to reduce 
excess motion, time, over process, inventory, and transport.

4.0 Benefit 
• Communicate a delay in seconds to Supervisors, Qua lity Inspectors, and Runners 
• Lets you route the call to the people assigned to each point 
• Reduces travel time and increases shop productivit y, which reduces cost and increases 
profitability 
• Vibrating wristbands alert responder no matter whe re he may be located.  Much more 
efficient than the “visual” Andon light system 
• Reduce the risk of accidents. The workers are not distracted by looking at traffic lights 
• The workshop can measure delay times and number of  calls of each operator, optimizing 
time and human resources work. This will allow opti mal management within each 
workshop 
• More organized workshop and good communication 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• Call buttons for each workstation for every workst ation 
• Pager wristband for each Supervisor, Quality Inspe ctor or Runner 
• Transmission master VM2700 multi functions 
• 2 relays (01 repeater VBR-100H and 01 repeater VPR -100H)  
• Custom software 
• Total Investment US$ 8,200 for 35 workstations and  8 pagers.
