1.0 Introduction  
 
This Best Practice document contains information on how to build a mounting fixture (as shown 
here for a C175 engine block - Figure 1 and 9) to affix a metal spray gun to an industrial robot.  
 
The use of industrial robot for metal spray purposes allows for precise control and accurate material 
velocity to create a strong bond coat which is evenly distributed. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Use the fixture to locate the metal spray gun in a way that the robot can utilize all available axes 
keeping the correct orientation of the spray gun to complete the spray coat.   
 
The manufacture process to build the fixture consists of cutting a 0.5-inch aluminum plate to create 
an assembly shown as follows:

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Use 0.5-inch aluminum plate and machine the different pieces of the assembly. 
 
Drill and tap the holes. 
  
Assemble the mounting fixture by welding all pieces together. 
 
 
Clamp the fixture into the 6
th axis of the robot, using mounting screws. 
 
       Assemble the Smart Arc Gun onto the fixture as shown.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Increases the reach capabilities of the robot 
• Allows spraying of the cylinder block liner bores of C175 engines 
• Improves the metal spray Set-Up times, improvement by 30% 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
Aluminum plate (0.5-inch thickness) 
Milling machine 
Metal spray equipment 
Industrial robot 
Welder with aluminum welding capabilities 
Manufacturing Price ~ 650 USD
