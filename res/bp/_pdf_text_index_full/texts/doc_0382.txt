1.0 Introduction  
 
This improvement contributes to a safer and more efficient washing and storage process for 
C175-16 and C175-20 engine mounts and accessories. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The improvement consists of implementing a basket with exclusive divisions and compartments 
for each of the parts that make up the C175 engine mount assembly: mounts, plates, studs, bolts 
and nuts. This allows that none of the parts placed in it suffer any kind of damage by bumping 
into each other during washing and/or storage.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Basket design for C175-16/20 engine beds. 
 
 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Eliminate the danger or risk of stored parts bumping and damaging each other. 
• Store in a safer, more orderly, and efficient manner the parts that constitute the C175 
engine mount assembly: mounts, plates, studs, bolts and nuts.
