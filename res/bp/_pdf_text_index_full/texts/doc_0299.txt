1.0 Introduction  
A common practice in the repair process of a component is to reuse the component part where 
possible.   
 
Friction discs, which are part of the brake packs, can be reused under the conditions that are 
referenced in the guide of reusability SEBF8095-06 Service Brakes for Off-Highway Trucks and 
Tractors. The guide notes that friction discs that are exposed to water can be damaged and 
must be replaced, so it is necessary to protect them from water in the pre-wash area. 
 
This Best Practice suggests the use of a brake group protection tool for trucks during the pre-
wash process.

The paper disc is made of a chain cellulose (paper) and, for that reason, exposure to water 
causes the disc to expand thus damaging its adhesive layer.  In addition, the friction material 
may easily come off during handling and/or installation. 
 
Caterpillar prohibits the use of water, steam, water based solutions, freon, thinner, or ketones to 
clean the friction discs.  It is recommended to clean with a chlorinated solvent cleaner, diesel 
fuel, kerosene or petroleum based distillates. 
 
 
Table 1. Uses of Friction Material 
 
 
Given the damage to the paper disc that is caused by exposure to water, we developed a cover 
that protects the component during the prewash (Figure 3). 
 
The protective cover includes a circular sheet, steel ring, duo-cone seal and O-ring. The plate is 
jointed to the circular sheet by welding points and sealed using epoxy (Figures 4 and 5). 
Between the seal and plate, it is also epoxyed. The O-ring ensures the cover is sealed (See 
Figure 7). 
 
For a quick setup the cover has a couple of grab handles. 
 
There are three sizes currently in operation (depending on the brake package to be washed) but 
the design can be adapted to other components using the duo-cone seal respective (Figure 6).

3.0 Implementation Steps 
1. Identify the model of the machine to determine the brake pack. 
2. Select the correct protective cover and O-ring according to Table 2. 
3. Place the protective cover using the handles. 
4. Adjust for a tight fit to prevent water from entering the brake housing. 
 
Note: The tool should be washed before and after use. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
• The discs are fully protected from water. 
• Increases the reusability rate of the paper discs. 
• Protect the other internal components of the brake pack.
