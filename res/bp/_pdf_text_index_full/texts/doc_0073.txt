1.0 Introduction  
 
This Best Practice introduces a simple workstation that has improved both the efficiency of 
workshop schematic diagnosis, tidiness of the workshop bays and office, and helped protect 
printed and laminated schematic diagrams. 
 
Schematics are frequently used in the repair bays to diagnose system faults. The workstation 
provides a dedicated, well-lit working surface, protects and houses laminated schematics 
(organized by model), plus has eliminated lost time and frustration brought about by trying to find 
the required schematic diagrams. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
As depicted in Illustration 1, the workstation has a sloped work surface that is illuminated from 
above. This surface is dedicated for schematic work and is at the height requested by the 
technicians. The station includes 27 horizontally positioned storage canisters for housing the 
schematics by machine model. These canisters are made from 100 mm PVC threaded end water 
pipe. Threaded pipe ends chained to the front panel act as end caps.  Beneath the canisters are 
two cupboards for extra storage of manuals and spare schematics. The workstation has lockable 
wheels but, in this situation, remains dedicated to the auto-electrician area.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
• Build workstation as per diagram in section 6.0 below. 
• Print and laminate schematics in the largest size practical. 
• Dedicate a location in the workshop for the workstation. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
Prior to this workstation, schematics were hung from a metal frame on newspaper-style clamps.  
This consumed a large area of the workshop office or bay space. It was untidy and often resulted 
in lost time by having to walk to the office from the repair bay and then try to find the required 
schematics. The new workstation has realized the following benefits: 
• Less space required for storage 
• Laminated schematics are better protected and no longer require folding 
• Schematics are stored by model 
• Dedicated and lit schematic reviewing surface 
• Workstation is now located in the repair bay area (rather than the office) 
• Technicians are 
happier
 
 with the new arrangement. This should not be underestimated.

5.0 Resources Required 
 
The workstation costs approximately AUD $4,500 to construct.  Refer to the diagrams of the 
workstation for dimensions and some basic instructions. Below is the required bill of materials: 
• 27 lengths of 100 mm Diameter PVC water pipe with one treaded end 
• 27 x 100 mm diameter threaded end caps 
• 27 x chains to connect end caps to canisters 
• 3 mm black steel as per diagrams 
• 6mm rivet nuts as per attached 
• Fluorescent tube for lighting with associated power supply and sealed plug 
• 1” steel RHS for lighting bracket 
• RHS for strengthening as per diagrams 
• 4 x heavy duty castors / wheels 
• Rubber matting for bench top 
• Lock, handles, hinges & urethane door seals for cupboards
