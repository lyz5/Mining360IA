1.0 Introduction  
At the Xstrata Tintaya mine site there are other Ground Engaging Tool (GET) suppliers besides 
Caterpillar, such as Metalogenia and BladeMaster.  As a result of the presence of several suppliers, 
the consumption and invoicing of Caterpillar’s GET has decreased.  Through mechanic technicians, 
planners, or workshop managers, the customer has several people making the decisions for product 
purchasing. 
 
To differentiate Caterpillar GET, Ferreyros has proposed an alternative solution.  This proposal 
consists of a chart of all Caterpillar GET consumables for every piece of mine equipment.  The goal is 
to increase Caterpillar GET’s visibility, and make it easier for customers to choose Caterpillar GET. 
 
This improvement has allowed Ferreyros to increase participation in current market and grow their 
sales considerably. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
The chart below shows the equipment model with its Caterpillar parts number, graphics, quantity of 
pieces, proper fit, and customer’s ellipse code.   
 
In addition to the information in the chart below, it may be helpful to include: 
• I nformation relating to any reuse of the parts or if they are replaced 100% of the time 
• Any GET Kits that are available 
• Any tooling required for the removal and installation of the GET 
• Reference to any heal shrouds, MAWPs, or other wear products (Chock bars, Chocky buttons, 
Chocky Blocks, or Roll bars)

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
1.   Park machine requiring GET 
2.   Locate part number and amount required per machine 
3.   Perform a boneyard analysis to identify if current GET is correct fit for machine/application 
4.   Create table for each type of equipment/part and insert images 
5.   Enter customer’s code for every part number 
6.   Perform a search for the torque and tooling needed to install/remove GET 
7.   Create chart for mine site by combining information for all equipment on site 
8.   Print large size chart (poster) 
9.   Format chart to be displayed and identify place to set in mine Workshop 
 
Optional: 
If the customer used PartStore
tm, set up the GET groups in the PartStoretm system to further facilitate 
the ordering and replacement process.

4.0 Benefits 
• Reduced searching time in parts manual 
• Reduced time spent by planner when searching customer (ellipse) codes 
• Reduced time to request spare parts  
• Reduced time to identify proper adjustment and welding procedures 
• Provides increase visibility to Caterpillar GET resulting in increased business/revenue for the 
dealer 
• Provides an approximate time savings of 67% for the customer (Chart 1), significantly 
reducing the time to request GET for scheduled maintenance
