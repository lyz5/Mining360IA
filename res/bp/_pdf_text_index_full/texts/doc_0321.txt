1.0 Introduction  
 
Long lead times and large costs associated with unplanned downtime of mine loading tools mandate 
that Component Rebuild Center (CRC) process owners have a clear line of sight to information 
required to manage work in progress associated with electric rope shovels, draglines and drills 
components being repaired at the CRC. SAP modules implemented in the legacy Bucyrus service 
centers did not come with turnkey metrics and reports geared towards CRC management, forcing 
individual regions to develop ad hoc reports. 
 
It is possible to purchase software geared towards managing the constantly changing environment in 
a CRC. Typical software costs with installation are approximately $75,000 and have an estimated 
total start to finish time of one year. The transition period associated with the transfer of Caterpillar 
service facilities to dealers presents a short window to improve performance within the current SAP 
enterprise resource planning software currently used at legacy Bucyrus service facilities prior to 
Dealer Day One.  
 
This best practice discusses metrics and reports implemented at Caterpillar Canada’s Edmonton 
CRC using existing reports from SAP. These metrics and reports help establish baselines for CRC 
performance and can be used to drive Continuous Improvement projects to increase efficiency, 
quality, and turnaround with Six Sigma, 5S, and CPS methodologies. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
In order to facilitate an improvement initiative, it is critical that the project team be able to measure 
and record metrics and Key Performance Indicators (KPI’s). These measurements are then used to 
analyze, improve and control performance.  
 
Lack of metrics for CRC Work In Process (WIP) can lead to missed customer promise dates, poor 
customer experience, cost overruns and excessive delays from last labor to work order invoicing. It is 
common for WIP to stagnate if work orders requiring parts with long lead times are not followed up on 
a regular basis. Often these work orders are not started until the repair becomes a burning issue and 
result in overtime costs and damaged relations with the end customer.  
 
In facilities where service exchange components are sold, it is critical to track cores owed from 
customers to ensure that they are returned in a timely manner. By establishing visual reports and 
metrics, process owners can follow up on potential problems before they escalate into machine down 
situations.

Metrics included in this report are: 
 
2.1.1 Service work order margin % 
Process owners can avoid losses by reviewing margin associated with orders in WIP. Any 
work orders at risk of not meeting margin targets can be managed to mitigate losses. 
 
2.1.2 Promise Date Adherence 
Work orders at risk of not meeting the date promised to customers are flagged for process 
owner action. Orders at risk of missing the promised delivery date can be identified and 
addressed. 
 
2.1.3 Total Days Order Open 
Orders open for an excessive period of time tie up WIP funds and are indicators of process 
issues. Monitoring total days since order creation can help identify these issues.  
 
2.1.4 Total Days since Last Labor to Invoice (LLI) 
Excessive LLI delays the timing of cash flows, negatively impacting a company’s financial 
position. Reducing this metric results in improved profitability by eliminating WIP holding 
charges and increases customer satisfaction via efficient invoicing practices. Benchmarks for 
LLI range from 6-10 days. The appropriate goal will vary based on factors surrounding 
obtaining customer purchase order numbers, parts/core return processes, report reviews, and 
many others.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
The high level process steps involved with implementation of these metrics are:  
- Establish work in progress management team 
- Identify and assign ownership of WIP reporting 
- Create reports by combining standard SAP reports utilizing feedback from process owners 
- Measure and communicate metrics 
- Document benefits 
- Identify Continuous Improvement (CI) projects based on collected metrics 
 
The SAP transaction codes required to compile the metrics are:  
- CATS_DA 
– Display Working Times  
- CJI3 – Display Actual Cost Line Items 
- IWBK – Material Availability Information 
- IW37 – Change Operations 
- IW38 – Change Planned Maintenance (PM) Orders 
- ZSD033 – Sales Order Report 
- Z_DEV_51000056 – Invoiced Sales Report  
- Z_CORES – Cores Owed By Customer 
- ZCUST_CONSIGN – Consignment inventory 
- Z_PLANT_INVENTORY – Plant Inventory 
 
Output from these sessions is used to create the required metrics, see attached spreadsheets for 
sample calculations of each metric from the standard SAP Reports 
 
3.1 WIP Dashboard Report:  
This report is run from SAP transaction code IW38 (See attached example). The default report layout 
will have to be modified to include all the required fields. Layouts can be saved by the user for future 
use.  
 
3.1.1 Service work order margin % 
Compare the actual cost for work orders on IW38 by totaling all costs for parent and child 
orders from CJI3. CJI3 will have to be run with all order numbers from IW38 entered into the 
order field. Compare to the contract value for associated sales order in ZSD033 for margin 
calculation.   
 
3.1.2 Promise Date Adherence 
Generate by comparing the sales order due date in ZSD033 to the current date. Cross 
reference with work order from IW38 for ease of recognition.  
 
3.1.3 Total Days Order Open 
Calculate total days that each order has been open order by comparing the current date to the 
order creation date on IW38

3.2 Total Days since Last Labor to Invoice (LLI) 
Determine total days LLI by comparing the last labor charged data from CJI3 for each service 
document invoice creation date on invoice report.. Obtain service orders for all invoiced service 
documents from IW38 (include completed and historical orders). Use service orders numbers to run 
CJI3 for last labor dates (It is important to only include labor lines). Compare last labor date to invoice 
billing date to determine LLI. In the case of multiple invoice dates, eliminate duplicates and only 
include last invoice date. 
   
3.3 Labor Utilization Report: 
Use a combination of data from CJI3 and CATS_DA to determine labor utilization. By using 
CATS_DA, it is possible to determine all labor charged to work orders for a given time period. This 
value can then be combined with CJI3 data for overhead accounts to determine utilization. Note: It 
will be necessary to determine local GL code for indirect labor. Consult with costs analyst in finance 
for GL code.   
 
3.4 Service Exchange Core Tracker: 
This report is derived by running transaction code Z_Cores. The standard report from SAP will show 
all service exchange components and will indicate exchange posted date for components that have 
been shipped to the customer. The report can be further formatted to include account representative 
information and core charge information that is maintained in a manual table.  
 
3.5 Service Exchange Dashboard: 
Use plant specific out put from Z_Plant_Inventory,  and WIP level from IW38. You will also need 
Sales Backlog to populate part numbers associated with IW38 orders

4.0 Benefits 
Defined KPI’s provide the process owner a clear line of sight to service operation performance. These 
metrics can then be used for future continuous improvement projects.  
 
The reports generated from this project were used to manage a component repair contract with a 
customer resulting in the CRC meeting the customer’s on-time delivery expectations and achieving a 
3 month rolling average improvement from 42% to 86%.  
 
 
 
In addition, along with some 5S activities, a reduction in active WIP resulted in improved utilization of 
shop floor space and a better work environment, as indicated in Figure 1 and 2 below.  Note work 
bays and work in process parts staging are much better organized, improving efficiency and work 
flow.
