MINING DEALER BEST PRACTICE  
 
1.0 Introduction  
 
Creating a standard work is essential to control quality in workshops. Considering that there is a 
different level of experience among technicians, it becomes more challenging. For non-English 
speaking countries due to the language barrier, SIS cannot be used effectively by the technician 
on a daily basis on the floor.  
 
This Best Practice presents a different approach to efficiently handle the in-place training of the 
technicians by using the visual (with video support) instructions together with the processes & 
procedures written, which helps to keep the quality of the work done and reduce the rework.  
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
During the daily operation on the shop floor, the technicians frequently refer to SIS and other in-
house developed standard work procedures.  
 
CRC Expert TV combines SIS information (Disassembly-Assembly-Reusability Guidelines) with 
CRC specific processes and demonstrates it visually with video support. It represents a Step-by-
step Operating Instructions/ Procedures with videos for engines and drive train components 
relating to Disassembly & Assembly processes. The instruction/procedure consist of: 
 
• Step Number 
• Step description 
• Reference (the source of the information described) 
• Video (showing how to do) 
• Control Checklists

Screenshot view from 3500 SERIES ENGINE DISASSEMBLY PROCEDURE 
 
Note: In addition to the common components’ disassembly/assembly processes, some key points 
are described in the steps and shown in the videos accordingly. These are:  
 
• Critical areas to pay attention while preparing defect list 
• How to measure parts to determine reusability 
• How to perform special tests on the parts (i.e. crack testing) 
• Selecting correct tools for the job and instructions to use them 
 
For quick access and friendly use of the CRC Expert TV, the customized software was 
developed. Every technician has their own login and password to enter the software at any time 
for self-training, updating the knowledge and skills or basically for assistance while going through 
the component disassembly/assembly processes.  
Each work bay is equipped with touch screen Kiosk terminals which make the system available 
all the time. Also, team leaders/Supervisors use an iPad application that allows them to check & 
control checklists as well as show the training video required to the technicians. 
 
As the Expert TV is based on the software, it enables timely tracking and easy updating of the 
existing instructions/procedures and/or adding new ones if some changes are made and/or a new 
component/equipment is received by CRC.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
• Gather a team/committee to create/review processes including Training, HSE and I.T. 
departments. 
• Determine the scope of the project. 
• Determine/map “As Is” rebuild processes. 
• Evaluate the current practice for improvements.  
• Prepare the new standard work (instruction/ procedure) based on SIS, and the dealer’s own 
experience. 
• Review “To Be” processes (instruction/procedure) with the Training Department and HSE 
Department in terms of safety updates. Make the necessary corrections/additions if they are 
required. 
• Develop/improve current checklists according to new updates. 
• Organize necessary training for ready and approved standard instruction/procedure with the 
technicians, whose work process has been chosen. 
• After implementation of new processes, schedule the work area for video shooting of all the 
steps in the standard instruction/procedure. 
• As soon as all the steps of the standard instruction/procedure are shot in video, they should be 
uploaded into the CRC Expert TV software, which has been created by I.T. Department for this 
purpose. 
 
The Expert TV has a feedback system to contact with the technical support team. It is 
continuously updated according to the CRC operation needs. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Providing a good technical visual training for a new or experienced technician, which 
saves time and money compared to the training in class 
• Increase sustainability of standard work in processes 
• Maintain the high quality of the work done 
• Reduce the rework percentage 
• Training material for class trainings 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• O n average, $600 USD per procedure to be shot by an Operator. 
• N ecessary tooling determined, support equipment (overhead cranes, benches, etc.). 
• Technicians, Training department, Technical support department and Safety Engineer. 
• IT support.
