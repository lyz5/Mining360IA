1.0 Introduction  
 
Whilst carrying out a Hydraulic Test process, the recommended oil leak detection method is to use 
a dye that glows bright green under ultraviolet light. The dye will show the specific leak path of the 
oil when a black light is used. The dye is not visible in the oil under normal light. After normal 
testing is completed, an oil leakage inspection is carried out in the dark while the component is on 
the test unit but not working. 
Since the oil leakage testing is carried out in darkness, work area around the component is not 
clear and can create a trip and collision hazard.
