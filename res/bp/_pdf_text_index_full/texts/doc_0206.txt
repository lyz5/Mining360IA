1.0 Introduction  
 
Many dealer Component Rebuild Centers study shop processes and re-align them to a factory-
like workflow. The focus is to create standardized processes and uninterrupted work efficiency. 
 
This Best Practice contains Ferreyros’ shortage/parts delivery process and short block rebuild kit 
for 3500 series engines. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description   
 
This Best Practice is an engine block rebuild kit with special storage/delivery carts. 
 
The short block kit contains both new and reused parts. The parts are arranged on the cart in the 
order that they are used. This saves time for the technician when all parts required are unwrapped 
and unboxed, ready to install, and arranged in the order that they will be needed. 
 
The storage/delivery cart itself is sealed for contamination control with reusable plastic covers and 
portable vapor capsules that contain corrosion inhibitor. This saves wrapping and unwrapping time 
and reduces the consumption of plastic wrap. 
 
The cart is stored in a staging area and only delivered to the work bay at the moment that it is 
required.
