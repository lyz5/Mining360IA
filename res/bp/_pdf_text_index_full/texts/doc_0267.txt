1.0 Introduction 
D
uring the revision process of the 980H Steering Pumps, there has been trouble both while 
removing and torqueing the pistons. While one of the two pistons in the Steering Pump could be 
disassembled thanks to hexagonal bolt gap, the other piston’s grip point was not reliable. The 
tightening tool could damage the piston using this method.  
2.
0 Best Practice Description 
D
uring the revision process of the Steering Pump shown in the below image, the seal O-ring 
located under the pistons must be removed to replace it with a new one. Although an allen bolt 
hole is made in the design for disassembling the right piston, this hole is not available in the left 
piston. Tightening and loosening with the help of a wrench may cause to scratch and damage the 
part. With the help of the tool we have implemented, this process does not damage the part and 
can be easily adjusted to the desired torque value.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
1. Follow the assembly procedure as outlined in Specification media number UENR2001, section 
“Piston Pump (Steering)”. 
a. Place the tool over the maximum angle piston and engage the flat areas for tightening.  
b. Torque the part properly, as specified in UENR2001.  It is tightened more securely 
thanks to its full grip. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
It minimizes the risk of damage. 
 
Torque can be adjusted more easily in tightening. 
 
It provides easier working opportunity for the technician.
