1.0 Introduction  
 
There is the safety requirement in mine sites that all rubber-tired equipment must be equipped 
with stand chocks. Borusan Makina Kazakhstan has decided to take this opportunity and develop 
its own stand chocks at a place on the equipment where they will be kept if they are not used.  
 
There is the Equipment Rebuild Shop (ERS) in the CRC where old machines are renewed to the 
status of the new ones. During a R1300G UG Loader rebuild, we designed and fabricated the first 
stand chock set for this machine. In the future stand chocks will be developed for other wheel 
machines (R1600G, R1700, R2900, AD30, AD45, AD55, AD60, 980H, 988H, 992G, 16H, 160H, 
140H, etc.). 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The R1300G stand chock set consists of 2 x Stand Chocks and 1 x Chock Holder. The stand 
chocks and their holder were designed to meet the following criteria: ergonomics, weight, 
installation place in the equipment, and tire dimensions of the equipment.  
 
The Chock Holder (1) is installed on the splashguard of the wheel and fastened to the walking 
platform of the loader with its bolts. The holder is removable to provide access to the filter behind 
it during PMs. Each Stand Chock (2) has a convenient handle (3) to carry and the locking 
sleeve (4) that keeps the stand chock in place by connecting it to the bolt (5), which is welded to 
the splashguard beforehand. Each locking sleeve has the chain so that the customer would not 
lose them in the mine site during the operation.
