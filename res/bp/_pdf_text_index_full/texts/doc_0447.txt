1.0 Introduction  
 
In the routine activity of removing and installing a Wear Cap - WC 62 (PN398-3042) for the ERS 7495 
Dipper lip adapter, an accident occurred involving an employee's hand and fingers. It was observed 
that the activity of replacing the WC to protect the penetration adapters presents ergonomic risks 
related to hand and finger crushing. The activity was performed manually by only one employee. This 
incident was widely publicized among the leaders of the client Vale S11D and Sotreq, as shown in 
Figure 1 below.  
 
Figure 1: Installation Procedure Comparison 
 
 
The Wear Cap used at the time is the WC-62 with 28kg. Dimensions are described in Figure 3 below.  
 
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Figure 2: Wear Cap Information 
 
 
A method was developed to mitigate the possibility of future accidents. The solution is a magnetic 
base and support with a lifting capacity of 400kg that could be used by two people more safely.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The idea of this best practice was to provide a safe way of removing and installing Wear Caps.   
 
A method and structure was developed that allows workers to avoid moving the Wear Cap directly 
with his hands and at the same time mitigate ergonomic damage by dividing the hoisted load between 
two people. 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
The implementation of this best practice can be divided into 3 steps, outlined below. 
 
STEP 1:  Determine Magnet Based on Lifting Requirements 
 
The magnetic base has: 
1) The capacity for lifting plates weighing up to 400kg 
2) A safety factor of 3.5 
3) An operating temperature of up to 80ºC.   
 
Figure 3: Magnet 
 
The dimension of the chosen magnet are 1) a height of 96mm, 2) a width of 94mm and 3) a length of 
168mm. It does not need electrical power as it is built with high energy permanent magnets.  
STEP 2:  Build Lifting Structure 
 
The support is made of 1/2" ABNT 1020 steel tubes for greater strength. It has two 500mm lifting rods 
and a 314.9 mm "V". Using two technicians, the support is coupled to the magnetic base and the 
lifting is carried out by them.

STEP 3: How to Use 
 
The lifting tool developed specifically for this best practice is inserted into the upper eye of the 
magnetic lifter. A technician performs the fitting using a rod to pull the wear cap as shown in Figure 6 
below. Using two technicians, the dipper adapter wear cap can be lifted. 
  
4.0 Benefits 
 
For the Wear Cap R&I: 
 
• Greater security in carrying out the R&I activities 
• Decreased risk of crushing and pinching fingers 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• 1 magnetic base with a capacity of 400kg with eyelet 
• 1 lifting rod with two handles 
• 1 rod to pull the helmet already attached to the magnetic lifter
