1.0 Introduction  
 
During the installation activity of the XL front brake assembly of the 793D or F after being rebuilt, it is 
common to find a misalignment between the splines of the discs and plates in relation to the brake 
assembly due to the lack of an appropriate tool for proper alignment. 
 
Since this misalignment takes place during component PCR,  it is necessary to fix  it in the field upon  
the brake assembly removal, placing it horizontally for alignment between the parts before component 
installation in the suspension and front wheel set, ending with the bearings preload. 
 
This problem significantly increases the time spent for the front brake assembly replacement process. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This best pract ice describes the development  and use of a device/tool  to mitigate the impact of two 
types of misalignments during the assembly process, as follows: 
  
A. Disc spline misalignment: 
Since Hub, PN 265-3189 is not always delivered from customer site to the CRC  together with 
the brake assembly, it often leads to misalignment. 
 
 
 
Figure 1 - Disc spline misalignment in relation to the 265-3189 hub during the component installation. 
 
 
B. Decentralization of the plates and disks in relation to the hub: 
As much as the hub is used, this will ensure that the splines are aligned during assembly  
(previous example), however the plates and discs can still be installed centered in relation to 
the brake assembly (housing).  
 
The images below show a set of brake with the splines  of the discs aligned but showing the 
decentralization in relation to the set (housing) which makes it impossible to install the Hub 
during the assembly of the component in the field.

• Utilization procedure for the tooling: 
 
1 - Position the brake assembly in a horizontal position on a service bench; 
2 - Use an appropriate tool to release the parking brake in order to keep the plates and discs 
free to move; 
3 - Install an appropriate lifting device for the tooling (customized hub with welded spacers); 
4 - Position and slide the tooling between the splines of the brake discs; 
5 - Make sure the brake discs are correctly aligned and centered; 
6 - Apply the parking brake; 
7 - Remove the tooling from the brake assembly. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Safety:  Eliminate the risk of injuries by avoiding rework in the field and preventing technician's 
exposure to the risks inherent to correlated activities. 
 
• Contamination control:  Once the component is received/ready for installation in the machine, there 
is no need for disassembling it thus eliminating exposure to external environmental contaminants. 
 
• Reliability of CRC service:  Use of the proposed tool for brake assembly alignment provides a fast, 
safe and quality installation; damage to the parts is avoided. 
 
• Elimination of rework during component installation:   This process ensures that the front brake 
assembly is installed on the machine in the standard time as it eliminates the possibility of field 
rework. 
 
The chart below shows the installation time considering customer's standard time to carry out the R&I, 
that is, from the equipment stop in the maintenance box to the release of the machine for operation, 
including the tire repair and lubrication works.  
 
The process without using this suitability tool (total time with rework +R&I) spends 10,5h (161%) more 
than optimized one (Optimized R&I).
