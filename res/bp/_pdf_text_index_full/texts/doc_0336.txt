1.0 Introduction  
 
During the process of rebuilding major components, select parts are often sent to a machine shop 
for salvage work. The machine shop may be dealer owned, or an outside contractor.  Lead-time 
for the salvage work to be completed causes a delay in the rebuild process.  Delays and 
interruptions to the rebuild workflow further cause inefficiencies, which impact operational 
performance and delivery. 
   
The following inefficiencies are resultant from excessive delays in the workflow.  
•  Parts often need to be moved for temporary storag e.  
•  Parts may need to be cleaned again before assembl y. 
•  Parts can get lost or disorganized.   
• The work in process time is increased thus impacti ng shop capacity. 
 
An effective solution is to create an “exchange” inventory of commonly salvaged subcomponents, 
readily available to eliminate machine shop salvage delays. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
One dealer conducted an analysis of turnaround time for subcomponents, which were sent to the 
machine shop for salvage.  Research found that some subcomponents typically experienced a 10-
day lead-time from the machine shop back to the rebuild center. The delays were causing 
bottlenecks, subsequently affecting shop efficiency. 
 
The decision was made to create a “subcomponent exchange” program for those parts identified 
as causing significant delays to the rebuild process.  
 
Criterion established: 
 
If Salvage Lead-time: 
 
1. Greater than 5 days, and 
2. Turns more than 10 times annually. 
 
Then: 
** Stock spare subcomponent inventory at the Machine Shop  
 
Subcomponents, which fell into this scenario typically, included: 
• Engine blocks 
• Crankshafts 
• Spindles.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
The “machine shop subcomponent exchange” concept makes a good 6 Sigma project.  
Implementation steps would include: 
 
• Define  the impact on shop efficiency as result of delays in waiting for machine shop 
salvage. 
• Measure  the delays and identify which subcomponents cause the delays. 
o Measure the impact in terms of less labor efficien cy and impact on work in process.   
o If you have a major component exchange inventory, then improving component 
turnaround time may allow you to reduce your component inventory level. 
• Analyze  cost versus benefit of stocking exchange subcomponents and payback 
time/volume. 
• Improve .  Create subcomponent exchange program and document the benefits. 
• Control.   Monitor impact on labor efficiency and shop turnaround time. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Labor efficiency due to uninterrupted workflow, le ss material handling, less re-cleaning of 
parts. 
• Increased shop capacity due to better component tu rnaround time. 
• Future reduction to major component exchange inven tories due to better turnaround time. 
• Less shop space utilized for temporary storage.   
• Better overall use of assets. 
   
5.0 Resources Required 
Investment in exchange subcomponents.
