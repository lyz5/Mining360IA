1.0 Introduction  
 
The Studs (hollow studs) of base oil filters and fuel in 3500 Series engines are installed with difficulty using 
pipe wrenches, not applying the required torque; running the risk of being lazy and in the worst case damage 
the threads of these, causing rework and wasted time (see Figure 1a, 1b, 1c.) 
 
  
Figure 1.a. Missing Stud in base oil filter. Figure 1.b. The stud came off with the filter after 
improper installation.  
 
Figure 1.c. Stud installation with pipe wrench, with this tool is most likely to damage the threads.  
 
 
This Best Practice aims to facilitate the development of this task to design a user-friendly, safe and quick 
use. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The required torque without the risk of damaging the threads: a toolkit for proper installation of the studs of the 
base oil filters and fuel, which allows installing properly designed. 
Several tools were manufactured according to the length and diameter of each stud. The tool consists of two 
main parts, the top and both steel socket adapter ASTM4140. 
The cover surrounds the socket adapter to join the two symmetrical parts of this eventually forming the 
hexagonal head 9/16 overhanging a superior hole in the lid and where the torque meter is installed. It is 
bringing threaded stud inside it will be installed.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Then the use of the tool is detailed. 
The tool is assembled by joining the two halves of the socket adapter and placing the lid on top. The screws 
are tightened only to touch the upper surface of the socket adapter. 
 
 
Figure 3. 
 
The stud is screwed into the socket adapter to take him to stop. 
 
 
Figure 4. 
 
Loctite 271 is applied at the end of the thread, as indicated in the SIS manual and the stud is installed in the 
base. Finally torque required by this applies.

• Easy fabrication 
• Easy operation 
• Prevents damage to the threads of the stud
