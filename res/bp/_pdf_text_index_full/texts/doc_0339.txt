1.0 Introduction  
 
In the world of high competition, to be different from your competitors and more attractive for your 
customers are key factors if you want to be successful and sustainable in the market.  
 
Previously our PSSRs (Parts Sales and Service Representatives) provided their customers with 
the information of the CRC capabilities via presentations, brochures and face-to-face 
conversation. That way to describe and demonstrate the capabilities of our Component Rebuild 
Center (CRC) was too common and did not make that effect on customers we wanted. 
 
Thus, the idea to shoot short videos about each service in the CRC came into mind. This project 
has received the name of the E-PSSR. This project hits two main targets.  
• I t gives a clear understanding of the CRC processes to customers, PSSRs, and 
newcomers in an interesting way 
• I t is a powerful marketing tool for PSSRs to sell the CRC service. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The E-PSSR is a man or woman who tells about the CRC rebuild capabilities with reasonable 
detail in a relaxed and interesting manner. The videos are short and last 3-4 minutes, i.e. all 
production cycles with technical aspects are shown in a fast rewind.  3D modelling was applied in 
the videos to make them more entertaining and easier to perceive. 
 
When this project was under the stage of preparation. We paid special attention to the selection 
of the person who would be an E-PSSR. We made a customer survey and asked them, who they 
wanted to see in videos and who they would trust. The survey results have given us the following 
profile of the E-PSSR: 
• A 40-year-old man, an engineering expert;  
• A simple person with a simple language of statement;  
• S omeone who is watching him should feel that it is their man, and this man is on the same 
page with them.  
The choice of the right person as the E-PSSR has become the key aspect for the success and 
benefits that we have got thanks to those videos. 
 
This project started in the beginning of 2015. We decided to start with the CRC new businesses. 
They were Hydraulic Cylinders and Bucket Rebuild. By now, we have shot the videos about 
hydraulic cylinders, buckets, CCR product. In the future, we are planning to make videos of the 
SOS Lab operation, turbochargers, hydraulic pumps and hydraulic motors. CAT asked us to 
provide the Bucket Rebuild Video with the English subtitles in order to show this video to other 
CAT Dealers.  
 
All videos are available in YouTube Borusan Makina Channel. We also send direct e-mails to 
potential and active customers (our database consists of around 2,000 e-mail addresses). You 
can see E-PSSR video as an example: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WhDcXxByqRk

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
• Find a good camera man and the right person for the role of E-PSSR 
• Select the rebuild process for video shooting 
• Write the speech for the E-PSSR relating to the rebuild process 
• Approve the budget for video shooting 
• Shoot the rebuild process selected 
• Share the ready short video in YouTube or any other channel of your company 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Customers become more loyal to CRC products, because they can see with their own 
eyes how the processes are performed and what CRC capabilities are used within 3-4 
minutes. 
• Both powerful marketing and training tool for our PSSRs. Now they better understand the 
CRC capabilities and can show all advantages of the CRC rebuild processes to their 
customers. 
• E- PSSR is a good “CRC Capabilities” presentation tool for CRC newcomers and guests. 
• E- PSSR project has become the first among competitors who has shown rebuild 
processes as they are. 
 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• Marketing Specialist, Operator with camera and a person who will be E-PSSR. 
• Support of the technical personnel. 
• Investments to shoot short videos for E-PSSR.
