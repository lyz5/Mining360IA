1.0 Introduction  
 
Maintenance and repair work on a drilling rig can be risky and time-consuming. Working over the trench 
of the drilling platform while add proper name for risky activity here “t ensioning pulldown” is just one 
key example of such an activity.   It Is important for the mechanic to have a safe footing while conducting 
this maintenance activity so he/she can concentrate on the activity and not have to worry about falling 
into the trench. 
 
The typical solution  used by customers  is placing scrap wood over the “ trench” which also present 
additional risk and time challenges -  proper size, proper thickness, good condition- not rotted, being 
secure, searching for the scrap wood each time maintenance is needed, handling the improvised 
platform, etc.  The photos in Figure 1 show this situation. 
 
The customer and maintenance personnel asked Sotreq to come up with a solution to support efficient 
and effective, risk and time minimizing maintenance on the drilling deck.   
 
 
 
 
Figure 1: Scenario before Best Practice implementation 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This Best Practice describes Sotreq’s solution of a Drilling Deck Maintenance Platform that provides 
and supports efficient, effective, and safe maintenance work behind the mast when the mast is lowered.  
 
The development and implementation of the platform is the result of collaboration between 
engineering teams and product support from SOTREQ and the customer's drilling team at the mine.

While this solution for this site was for the MD6420, it can easily be developed for other drill models. 
 
Some notes: 
Each machine will have its own platform. 
• When not in use, the platforms should be stored in a known/staged location so that they are 
always readily available. 
• To avoid loss or damage to the equipment, supports were installed on the handrails on the left and 
right sides of the platform to allow lifting and moving to position it in a safe manner. 
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Implementation of this best practice solution is broken into two key steps 
1. Design and Build the Platform 
2. Assembly  
 
 
Step 1: Design & Build Platform 
• Interview the mechanics and analyze the problem locally 
• Review potential solutions / Choose the best solution 
• Measure work area (gap/trench) 
• Determine best material to make the platform out of 
• Design in Autocad (see diagrams below) 
• Build main planform 
• Check for Proper Fit / Adjust as necessary. 
  
 
Step 2:  Assembly 
• Add handles/supports (allowing it to be mobile) for handling during maintenance.

4.0 Benefits 
 
Key Benefits of implementing this best practice include the following: 
• Improved Safety in working area 
• Improved Efficiency and Effectiveness (confidence in stability of working area) 
• Easy installation and replication for other machine models
