MINING DEALER BEST PRACTICE  
 
1.0 Introduction  
 
Installing aftermarket electrical devices (e.g. warning systems) on a 793B by modifying the 
machine's electrical harnesses can generate faults in the electrical system due to loose 
contact, overloaded circuit system, etc. 
  
This Best Practice is achieved by using CAT parts without making modification to the system. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Installing third party accessories may cause a delay in the commissioning of the 793B. 
Electrical faults crated by third party devices may also cause lower machine availability due to 
lack of information and knowledge of the original circuitry. This Best Practice is to have the 
work performed by a CAT dealer using original Cat parts. 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
There have been complaints of electrical faults generated after installing third party devices 
after the delivery of the 793B to customer’s mine site. Electrical faults were generated by third 
party technicians handling the 793B circuits. A list of additional accessories installed by the 
customer:  
 
• Siren boot. (sounds during startup)  
• Alert sound inside the cabin when the machine is stopped (for 20 sec.) without parking  
• External visual alert when the machine is operating but with the parking brake applied  
• Passing light (used to indicate to other vehicles that can overtake the truck)  
 
With this information a schematic was drawn with Cat parts.

4.0 Benefits 
• V isual alert ensures that the truck is parked. 
• The truck passing light is used to authorize a light vehicle to overtake the truck. 
• Can be installed without changing the wiring, using original spare Cat parts. 
• Improves electrical monitoring to maintain the original wiring. 
• Improved availability in the early hours compared to a third party. 
• I mproves the truck delivery time, when installed during repair. 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• For this Best Practice, Caterpillar technical information coupled with the experience of the 
technical staff with support from supervisor of the workshop was used.  
• 60 hours of work.
