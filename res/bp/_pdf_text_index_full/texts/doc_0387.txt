1.0 Introduction  
 
The models “D” and “E” super bolts  that are mounted on the left hoist drum pedestal and rear lugs of 
the gearcase base after the completion of gearcase assembly become inaccessible for torque checking 
in maintenance.  
 
Sotreq presented an option for  mounting super bolts  inversely to CAT  that made the super bolts  
accessible for torque checking without removing the hoist gearcase.  This solution was approved and 
implemented in a machine assembly in Brazil.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The purpose of this Best Practice is to change the mounting orientation of the hoist pedestal hardware 
fixture shown in the drawings  S006762 and S006970  to facilitates access for the prevent ative 
maintenance team performing torque verification on the ERS  7495 hoist pedestal super bolts.  This 
results in less maintenance and inspection time and consequently less overall downtime.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
To implement this best practice, follow the steps below: 
 
1. Assemble the super bolts PN 443-2362 model “D” and PN 467-0893 model “E” in the 
position shown in this BP during the equipment assembly process or intervention to replace 
the super bolts.  
2. At the time of installation, use suitable to rque equipment and technicians with proper 
confined space training.
