1.0 Introduction  
 
This Best Practice illustrates how to use Vital Information Management System (VIMS) data for 
effective and cost-efficient Planned Maintenance (PM). PM events will better contribute to 
optimum machine productivity when the actual downtime associated with the PM is curtailed.  
 
Downtime (associated with PM events) must be minimized, and all necessary work completed, to 
effectively increase machine productivity. Successful PM events also require reviewing VIMS 
data shortly after the machine leaves the shop. Reviewing the data afterward will ensure the 
proper maintenance issues were corrected, and further help to reduce unscheduled downtime.   
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
1. Three days prior to PM 
a. Download VIMS data, using VIMSpc, 
b. Take oil samples 
c. Review backlog 
d. Mechanical inspection 
e. Analyze the VIMS data and complete the VIMS PM Planner (see Figure 1 on next 
page)  
f. Schedule the proper number of people required to complete the PM 
g. Order and receive the required parts to fix any mechanical issues discovered. 
h. Establish an overall plan to complete the PM in the most effective manner 
 
 
2. One day after PM. 
a. Download the VIMS data and fill out the PM Planner again 
b. Ensure all maintenance completed was accurate. (This can also serve as a training 
opportunity for newer technicians.)

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Three P’s are the key steps to getting started: 
 
1. People – Having the proper people dedicated to gathering, analyzing, and reporting the finding 
of the VIMS data to the maintenance manager. 
2. Parts – Ordering and having the needed parts on hand when the machine enters the shop. 
3. Plan – Having an over all plan to schedule enough people and time to complete the PM in the 
most efficient manner. 
   
4.0 Benefits 
 
The benefits of utilizing the VIMS PM planner are: 
 
• The ability to complete maintenance needs during a scheduled downtime.

5.0 Resources Required 
 
People dedicated to gathering and analyzing VIMS data, are the primary resource.  
 
VIMS Application training may also be required to assist with the data collection step of the 
process, using the VIMSpc software.
