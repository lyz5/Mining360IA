1.0 Introduction  
The overheating of machines in the mining industry happens on a regular basis. With the updates in 
technology, dealerships have been able to take advantage of that to further business with the use of 
ECM’s (electronic control module). ECM’s allow customer/technicians to check the engine/machine 
for any damage, abuse, available updates or reoccurring events.  
 
Several engine types do not have an ECM (3400 & 3500) which in turn makes it very difficult from a 
dealer’s standpoint to know if the machine is being overheated or abused. The engine heat tabs are 
used to track each cylinder head for overheating. The placement of the heat tabs varies depending on 
the style/series of engine, but primarily they are placed by the water port/thermostat housing of the 
cylinder head. For engines with ECM’s (C-Series) using a heat tab is another way, along with the 
ECM,  to track the overheating of the cylinder heads.   
 
In this Best Practice we will discuss the placement and installation of engine heat tabs on 3400, 3500 
and C-Series engines to better document overheating/abuse.  
 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
Many dealers that are associated with the mining industry have debated with customers about 
overheating and/or abuse of machines. Engine heat tabs allow for the dealer to have reinforcement 
due to the fact that if the heat tab is melted or missing, that specific cylinder head reached an 
overheating temperature in excess of 235 degrees Fahrenheit. 
 
This Best Practice will guide dealers to show how to clean the cylinder head, prep the tab, dispense 
the adhesive and place the tab on the cylinder head. All of these steps will allow for a more efficient 
and safe work environment to reduce overheating/abuse to the engine/machine.   
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
The placement of heat tabs vary according to which style engine is being worked on. Prior to 
installation the area in which the tab is to be placed needs to be cleaned thoroughly with Loctite 7387 
activator. Next apply the Loctite 330 adhesive to back of heat tab. Lastly place heat tab on to 
designated area and hold for 30-60 seconds until solidly in place.
