1.0 Introduction  
 
The manipulation of the mining equipment components becomes a challenge because of its 
weight and volume, which can lead to accidents during the installation and removal process.  A 
device is needed to reduce the risk and accident probability. 
 
The process of removing the pin that holds the cable (while the body is up) has generated 
accidents caused by beatings and/or smashes due to the manipulation of these elements. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The solution consists of designing a device that reduces accidents to hands, which keeps the 
hands away from the danger zone;  allowing the mechanic to carry out this activity  safely and 
quickly.       (Figure 1)

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
• Design and manufacture the tool shown. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Reduce the accident risks while the 793 trucks are under maintenance. 
• Maintenance process standards improvements. 
• Improving the conditions of workers exposed to ergonomically incorrect working positions. 
• Improved customer´s perception. 
 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
The necessary elements to implement the Best Practice are: 
• Workforce 
• Materials (according to the drawings) 
• Tools
