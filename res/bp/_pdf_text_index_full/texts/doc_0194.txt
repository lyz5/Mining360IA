1.0 Introduction  
 
CRC processes and uninterrupted workflow are vital to optimizing component rebuild efficiency, 
component turnaround time, controlling costs, and maintaining quality. Process mapping is an 
important tool to help establish “as is” and “to be” processes.

This Best Practice discusses Ferreyros program using portable video cameras in the CRC to 
document the actual processes, study bottlenecks, determine root causes for delays and waste, and 
develop a Continuous Improvement plan. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description    
   
Dealer uses video cameras to study specific areas of activity in the CRC. The time and motion activity 
is recorded over a period of time and reviewed by a CI team. 
 
The video can be fast-forwarded to significantly shorten the time required to study the processes. 
The cameras are mounted on an adjustable stand which can be easily moved and relocated 
anywhere in the shop. They are used to study general processes as well as specific situations where 
an improvement is desired.
