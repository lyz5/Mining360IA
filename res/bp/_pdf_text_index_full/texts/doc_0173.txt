1.0 Introduction 
 
This improvement has been entered into the production line serviceable components Finning 495HR 
CRC Shovels and Drilling. Finning had to manufacture oversize bolts which have the function of 
extending the use of the Drum and Crown components. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Generally in the assembling processes of the hoist drum (Drum P/N S006620 and Crown P/N 
E020030-01), the following pin is used: bolt P/N B006876-01 - Standard measurement (1.750") or bolt 
P/N B006876-02 – Standard measurement (1.810”).  
When the bolt B006876-01/02 cannot be used because of the bolt bore is out of line and will not fit, 
we need to manufacture an oversize bolt to reuse components.
