1.0 Introduction  
 
A new process to pre-clean and remove paint from major components has been implemented at 
Trakindo. This process uses a commercially available recycled ground plastic as a sand blast 
media. The plastic is ground into tiny particles with a consistency similar to sugar grains and is an 
excellent sand blast medium. 
 
The plastic does not harm non-metallic parts such as plastic, rubber, and wiring harnesses. It also 
does not harm soft metals such as aluminum. It efficiently and effectively removes paint down to 
bare metal.

Step one is to clean the component with high-pressure hot water and blow dry with compressed 
air. 
 
 
 
Next, in the case of engines, the component is mounted on a rotating stand that can rotate the 
engine through 270 degrees. Custom adapters were made to accommodate a better degree of 
rotation. (See attachment for details). 
 
 
 
The component is then blasted using the plastic media and then blown off with compressed air.
