1.0 Introduction  
 
To reduce the risk breakdown of the second reduction´s carrier during the remove of shaft with the 
press in Caterpillar trucks , this Best Practice is a simple fabricated tool that is compatible with the 
Models 793B, 793C, 793D and 793F. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Ferreyros has developed a simple tool that supports the carrier, to perform a safety job of 
disassembly during the use of the hydraulic press.
