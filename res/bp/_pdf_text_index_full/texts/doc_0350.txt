1.0 Introduction  
 
A few dealers have installed remote control cameras in the dyno test cell. The test cell operator 
can conduct the dyno test, monitor changes, and visually monitor the engine, all without leaving 
the control room. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description   
                  
This Best Practice has been implemented by Ferreyros.  Three wall mount remote control 
cameras are located in the dyno test cell. The cameras can operate by a toggle switch and pan 
from side to side and zoom in as required. Ferreyros has installed one camera on each side of the 
engine, and another in front of the engine. A video monitor in the control room displays images. 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Acquire cameras, monitors, and remote control hardware. Approximate cost $1500 per camera.
