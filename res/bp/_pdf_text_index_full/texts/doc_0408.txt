1.0 Introduction  
 
In most mining sites around the world, the safety policy, as well as compliance issues, push 
mining companies to set up continuous improvement (CI) projects to make the maintenance 
process better, protecting the lives of technicians during the execution. 
 
In 2020, the first 994K loaders began operating at the S11D mine in Brazil, as part of the customer’s 
effort to increase iron ore production and increase asset flexibility. The 994K Wheel Loader has a 
steering frame lock link  that maintains alignment between both fr ames. Sotreq has identified an 
improved process in handling this link.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The 994K Wheel Loader steering frame lock link is used during maintenance to avoid crush risk 
and increase the security of the technicians. The steering lock link has two pins, one in each end.  
 
The image below, shows the details of the steering frame lock link: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Figure 2 – 994K steering frame lock link 
 
 
 
To improve the mechanics' safety, a handling device was welded onto the top of each steering 
lock pin and on two locations of the steering lock link. These handling devices will reduce the 
risk of serious injury to personnel.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
SOTREQ removed both pins PN 124-3820 and the link 391-2310 from the machine. A steel bar 
of 3/8” was welded on the top of both pins and at two locations on the link to create handles. 
When welding, use pre-heat and low hydrogen wire to reduce the risk of cracking. Inspect the 
heat affected zone of pins and link for surface cracks up to 48 hours after welding.
