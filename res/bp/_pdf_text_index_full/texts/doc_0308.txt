1.0 Introduction  
 
It is a difficult task to install the 6I-9790 bolt dampers into the clutch housings of transmissions, 
such as in the 793C.   
 
  
The bolt damper must be compressed with a vise grip in order to install in the bore of the housing.  
It is difficult to grip the bolt damper with the vise grip. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Ferreyros has developed a very simple tool (collet)  to compress the damper bolt.  The collet is 
sized for the damper bolt that has a tapered bore. 
  
 
 
The collet holds and compresses the bolt damper while it is being installed.

The upper diameter of the collet is slightly larger  than the bolt damper diameter.  The taper of the 
collet compresses the bolt damper.   The bottom dia meter is 0.3mm smaller than the clutch 
housing bore.     
 
Process: 
 
Lightly tap the bolt damper into the collet with a rubber mallet. 
 
Place the collet and compressed bolt damper over th e clutch housing bore.  Tap in the bolt 
damper with a mallet. 
 
Remove the collet.  Finish installation by tapping the bolt damper in position with the rubber 
mallet.

3.0 Benefit 
 
• Allows quicker and easier installation of bolt dam pers. More efficient. 
• Less chance of damaging the bolt damper. 
• Decreases risk of injury while inserting the bolt damper. 
 
 
4.0 Resources Required 
 
• Fabrication of collet. 
• Minimal expense to fabricate the tool.
