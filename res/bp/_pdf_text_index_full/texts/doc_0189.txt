1.0 Introduction  
 
The Product Support Division of Ferreyros is continuously searching for ways to optimize the cycle 
time of component rebuilds in the CRC using CPS processes.  Improving component turnaround 
time increases efficiencies, capacity, and lowers cost resulting in higher levels of customer 
satisfaction.  
 
In the dyno test process, there was an opportunity to improve the set-up times, ordering process, 
and developing standardized set up kits, thus avoid unnecessary time lost looking for these 
materials. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
CRC Dynamometer had their hoses and accessories storage in one place but without 
identification. That process generates excessive search times to find connecting tooling required.  
According to the measurement performed in December, the time to look for and hook up 
accessories was out of standards. 
 
To remove the waste mentioned in this area, accessory parts kits were created for each model of 
engine in order to install the engine to the dyno, color-coded for identification, and stored 
organized by engine model.

II. Determine frequency of engine models tested in dynamometers (Pareto).  
• Historical data was used to determine the most frequent engine models.  
• Then, an engine classification was performed to identify subclasses of engine.  
• Finally, a Pareto analysis was made to focus on the 80% most common engines.

4.0 Benefits 
♦ Excess and duplication of accessories and hoses were removed.  
♦ Search time and installing component for testing was reduced.  
♦ Excessive time to look for and installation of accessories (about 20% of the complete test) 
were removed. 
♦ Work areas are neat and clean 
♦ Appropriate storage of hoses and accessories, keeping them and avoid being 
contaminated.  
♦ Using ergonomics principles, ways of working were improved 
♦ Reduced test time by WO.
