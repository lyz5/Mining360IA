MINING DEALER BEST PRACTICE  
 
 
1.0 Introduction  
 
The objective of this Best Practice is to outline the procedure and tooling required to effectively 
and safely remove/install steering cylinders and tie rods from Large Off-Highway Trucks (LOHT). 
A procedure currently exists in SIS for this process; however this Best Practice is designed to 
improve the tooling setup used to facilitate removal and installation. The benefits are primarily to 
ensure mechanic/fitter safety and reduce the physical exertion required in the removal/installation 
process.  
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Secure the steering cylinder/tie rod support frame by bolting it to the turntable of a common belly 
guard hoist. The turntable allows 360 degree movement of the support frame to easily align it with 
the steering cylinder or tie rod before they are disconnected. The belly guard hoist has a 
pneumatically operated motor to drive the wheels, eliminating the need for the operator to push 
and pull the hoist. The hydraulic lift system is contained within the hoist and is manually operated 
by the lever arm. These factors combine to produce an efficient, safe and an easy to execute 
method of steering cylinder and tie rod removal and installation in LOHT. 
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
3.1 Hoist  
 
Source an appropriately sized and rated hoist with turntable in accordance with the size of the 
steering cylinder or tie rod that is to be removed/installed from the truck. The hoist is hydraulically 
driven from a pneumatically operated motor, allowing the entire assembly to be moved easily. 
The flat surface of the hoist is raised or lowered by physically pumping the lever arm up and 
down, or via a separate lever (depending on model), to the desired level, as per a standard pallet 
jack. Refer to Sections 6 and Section 9 of this document for specifications and other details.

3.3 Assembly 
  
Secure the support frame to the hoist by bolting it to the turntable. Ensure that the type and 
number of bolts is in accordance with any relevant local standard to support the steering cylinder 
or tie rod. Note that the weight of a steering cylinder from a 797F is approximately 329 kg (725 
lbs).  
 
 
Figure 3.3 Support frame bolted to turntable on hoist. 
 
3.4 Install & Remove  
 
Install/remove the steering cylinder and tie rods using the hoist/support frame assembly in 
accordance with the Removal & Installation Instructions outlined in document KENR8579-04 
located in SIS.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Assembly is robust and secure, reducing the likelihood of tooling failure or dropping of the 
steering cylinder or tie rod. 
• Pneumatic drive motor eliminates manual pushing and pulling of the hoist, reducing operator 
fatigue. 
• Increased efficiency by reducing component change out time. 
• Since the hoist was originally designed to be used to remove and install the belly pan of 
various machines, it can also be used for this purpose. 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• 1 x Steering cylinder/tie rod hoist with pneumatic motor drive control. 
• 1 x Suitable support frame. 
• 4- 8 x Bolts (size and number as required) to secure support frame to the turntable on the hoist. 
• Shifter/Pneumatic drill to secure support frame to the hoist. 
• Pneumatic air supply to drive the hoist. 
 
6.0 Supporting Attachments / References
