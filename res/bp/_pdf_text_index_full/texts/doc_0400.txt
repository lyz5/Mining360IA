1.0 Introduction 
  
To successfully assemble and disassemble the 330 Excavator side final drive, it is necessary to install 
the slide bearing in the housing and tighten the nut. However, the nut must be tightened to a torque of 
2950 ± 295 lb-ft (4000 ± 400 N-m), which cannot be done with a hand tool because of the high torque 
value. Special equipment must be used to perform this operation. 
 
 
Fig. 1 Nut 
 
Fig. 2 Stand for onboard final drive disassembly and assembly 
 
Other dealers who assemble this onboard gearbox have to use a special bench to perform this 
operation. However, at our dealership, repairs on these bevel gears are quite rare, so we have 
developed an alternative assembly fixture that allows us to successfully perform this operation without 
the use of a special bench. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This Best Practice describes a tool that is designed to tighten the nut on an onboard final drive using a 
bench that is normally used for hydraulic cylinder assembly. Using it will avoid the need to purchase a 
specialized on-board final drive disassembly and assembly stand, as you can make an alternative stand 
yourself.

To work with this wrench, you must use a special hydraulic cylinder assembly and disassembly stand. 
Follow the instructions below to complete the task: 
 
1. Bolt the onboard gearbox to the bracket. 
2. Lift the onboard final drive to stand height with slings and crane and secure it to the stand. Leave 
the slings taut for insurance. 
3. Install the wrench in the hydraulic motor and lock it in place. 
4. Start turning the hydraulic motor in the desired direction to tighten or loosen the nut. 
5. Dismantle this construction from the stand. 
 
 
When working with a hydraulic wrench, safety precautions and proper standards of procedure must be 
followed to avoid injury or damage. In addition, it is recommended that all necessary tools and 
accessories are checked , and that the equipment is visually inspected and tested before beginning 
work.

4.0 Benefits 
 
A tool consisting of a wrench clamped into a low-torque hydraulic motor mounted on the machine 
and a locking bracket has several advantages over buying a special stand: 
 
Reduced costs. It is much cheaper to assemble a wrench set with a hydraulic motor and fixing 
bracket than to buy a special stand for assembly and disassembly of hydraulic cylinders 
separately which is available at many dealers. This allows you to save a significant amount of 
money on production equipment. 
 
Space saving. Using this tool saves space in the shop, as it takes up much less space than a 
separate stand. This is especially important for small workshops where every square meter 
counts.
