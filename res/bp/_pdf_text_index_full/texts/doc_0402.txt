1.0 Introduction  
 
It is difficult to carry out maintenance/repairs in the PLC cabinet room due to the confined space, 
lack of access to sufficient lighting and trying to do repair while holding flashlights.  Currently PLC 
cabinets do not have an internal light source; the PLC cabinet having its own light source would 
help mitigate the last two of three difficulties and would allow for more efficient and effective 
maintenance/repairs and inspections. 
  
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The Best Practice is the simple but effective illumination of the PLC cabinet using a 24V Headlight 
to provide sufficient lighting supporting more efficient and effective maintenance/repair work and 
inspections. 
 
With commonly available parts and a little manpower, sufficient lighting can be provided for the 
PLC cabinet.   Implementation of the Best Practice can be done easily during a PM to minimize 
additional downtime of the machine. 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
A. Install the 24V Headlight 
The 24V Headlight – PN 487-7586 - was chosen as it was a part commonly used on site.  
Each dealer can choose the appropriate option/part number for their site.  The cable should 
be appropriately routed and tied down. 
 
 
 
B. Install the switch in the cabinet door. 
The switch – PN 530-6765 - was chosen as it was a part commonly used on site.  Each 
dealer can choose the appropriate option/part number for their site.   
  
C. Direct power from PS1 (24V source). 
Power can be taken direction from the PS1 (24V source).

4.0 Benefits 
 
This best practice results in the following: 
• A source of sufficient lighting in the PLC cabinet that is available with “the flip of a switch”. 
• More efficient and effective maintenance/repair work and inspections as the 
technicians/inspectors can focus on the actual work/inspections instead of trying to provide 
sufficient lighting to be able to work/inspect.
