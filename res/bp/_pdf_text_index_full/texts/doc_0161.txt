1.0 Introduction  
Trakindo Equipment Management & Service Team of the Kaltim Prima Coal project has made a 
tool to safely, easily, and quickly remove and install tires from the rim of a 740 truck on site. 
  
Previously, two technicians were needed to do this task. One technician to operate the tool and 
the other technician to hold the torque tool. 
 
This supporting tool helps the service team remove and install tires from the rim quickly, safely 
and by using only the efforts of one technician.  Moreover, this over can be modified for other 
models of trucks if it is required.
