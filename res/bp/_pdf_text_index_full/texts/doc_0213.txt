1.0 Introduction  
 
New cleaning equipment which improves the control, removal, and recycling of metallic waste has 
been implemented in the machine shop in Ferreyros SA. The equipment is an industrial vacuum. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
When large components (salvaged by welding) are machined, a large amount of metallic metal 
shavings are created. This residue hinders the work of the operators, poses a contamination 
control risk, and creates an unprofessional shop image.
