1.0 Introduction  
 
The cooling system  performs several functions which are critical to proper machine operation:  It 
maintains proper engine temperature for optimum performance, cools compressed inlet air to 
optimize combustion and dissipates excess heat  from other machine systems and components. 
 
Caterpillar has implemented hydraulically driven demand fan on certain products to improve fuel 
efficiency and provide more efficient cooling, but there are plenty of machines still in operation 
which use a mechanically-operated  cooling fan. 
 
A properly functioning fan drive is critical to engine reliability, as it works to maitain a safe 
temperature range. A failing fan drive bearings can cause complication in the repair due to fan and 
radiator core damage and result in expensive cost repairs.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The fan hub assembly plays an integral roll in keeping the fan drive in place so it can maintain the 
optimal speed of the system. To prevent the fan drive from failing off, this Best Practice provides a 
procedure to measure end play for fan drive and accuratelly diagnose the problem and fix it as 
efficiently as possible.  
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
1. Park the machine on a level surface and engage the parking brake; 
2. In order to relieve the tension on the fan drive belts, Insert suitable 19.1 mm (0.75 inch) tooling 
386-3359 into square hole  in the belt tensioner, and pry the arm of the belt tensioner in a 
counterclockwise Direction; 
3. Remove the belts; 
4. Attach the magnetic base of a dial indicator to the support of the fan drive; 
 
 
Figure 2. Position of magnetic base on fan drive Support.

 Monitoring fan drive end play;
