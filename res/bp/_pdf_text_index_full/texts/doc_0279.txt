1.0 Introduction 
 
Many dealers today use a process to assemble and install the 3500 engine cylinder heads along with intake 
and exhaust manifolds as one complete group - the entire group on the block in one step.   The process of 
installing the cylinder head group to the engine needs to be done with precision. Often, aligning the bolt’s 
holes to the intake manifold with the aftercooler requires time and effort.    
 
 
 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Ferreyros has developed a simple gauge to check the alignment of the intake flange bolt holes to assure that 
the flanges are mounted level at 180 degrees before installation begins.  This check saves time spent 
adjusting the alignment while the group is installed on the engine block. 
 
Since tool prints are not currently available, the following photos show the fixture and the process.
