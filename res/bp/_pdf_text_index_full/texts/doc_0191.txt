CAT GLOBAL MINING 
 
BEST PRACTICE SERIES 
 
1.0 Introduction  
 
It is important that after a wheel group has been rebuilt, it is properly sealed and protected for 
storage in an exchange program inventory or for shipping to a job site. 
 
This Best Practice contains a list of part numbers for all the covers, caps, plugs, seals, and 
hardware used by the factory to ship and store wheel groups. 
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The attachment to this Best Practice is a spreadsheet for all the part numbers required for 
professional storage and shipping of wheel groups.  The only part that does not have a Caterpillar 
part number is the plastic caps used to cover the wheel studs.  The supplier and supplier part 
number are provided in the attachment. 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Standardize a preparation for shipping process to include the installation of seals, covers, plugs, 
etc.  Most of these parts are reusable.
