1. Introduction  
 
Ferreyros has developed a simple tool to improve the process of assembling the C175 engines in 
installing the exhaust manifold. The tool allows greater stability during installation and prevents 
the exhaust manifold to flex during lifting. Align the contact faces exhaust manifold with respect to 
the cylinder head, providing the proper angle, reducing the time and effort applied by the 
technician. Caterpillar does not have a special tool for this process.

Install the gasket and the bolts securing the exhaust manifold to the cylinder head at the top. 
Remove the tool and apply the appropriate torque to the bolts securing the exhaust manifold. 
 
 
The exhaust manifold is safely installed without losing its stability at all times. This same tool 
should be used for the removal process, because it minimizes risks during the operation for the 
exhaust manifold and the people around. 
 
3. Implementation Steps 
 
Design the tool. 
   
4. Benefits 
 
• Improve operator safety. 
• Improve process efficiency. 
• Prevents damage to the exhaust manifold during remove or install. 
 
5. Resources Required 
 
• Minimal expense to fabricate the tool.
