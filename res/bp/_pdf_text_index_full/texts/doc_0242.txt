MINING DEALER BEST PRACTICE  
 
1.0 Introduction  
 
Borusan Makina Kazakhstan designed the adapter to the original CAT revolver for assembling C 
series and smaller engines that has improved the disassembly/assembly process of 3500 series 
engine front housings. Also the plate has been developed to this adapter that allows 
disassembling/assembling 3500 series and C32 engines fan group as well.  
 
Without using the revolver with the adapter, the front housing was disassembled/ assembled on 
the table involving two technicians. It was inconvenient and required more efforts and man-hours.  
 
Thus, the new tool is easier and safer to assemble front housings and fan group with one 
technician. 
 
EDITOR’S NOTE:  Given the cantilever load, proper safety precautions should be taken to 
prevent accidental falling of the loads. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
It is a mobile tool with a 360

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Makes the work on disassembling/assembling front housings and fan group easier, more 
convenient & safer. 
• Reduces significantly man-hours (now only one technician can handle this job). 
 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• The CAT revolver consisting of Stand (Part No 1U5750) & 2 x Adapters (Part Nos: 1U-
5749, 1U-7508); 
• All required parts (name, size, number & material) to fabricate the adapter and the plate 
are indicated in the drawings attached.
