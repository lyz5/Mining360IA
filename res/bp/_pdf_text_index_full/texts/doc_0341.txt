1.0 Introduction  
 
During the rebuild of suspension cylinders, adding oil to the cylinder in the final step is time 
consuming.  The processes in most of the Disassembly / Assembly guides are to mount the 
cylinder in a vertical position, add the oil, and then add install the head of the cylinder. 
 
Today, the hydraulic shop has fabricated a stand for the lubrication of the cylinder. 
 
Before this Best Practice, the cylinder was placed vertically using a jib crane all the time, in that 
position the cylinder was filled with oil. The average time of this operation was two hours. At the 
present time, it only takes ten minutes.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
A horizontal stand has been designed to hold the suspension cylinder.  The stand has a tray in the 
base to make sure no hydraulic oil can spill on the shop floor.  
 
The use of the stand reduces the time required by 90% and the bay jib crane is only required for a 
few minutes to mount the cylinder instead of the normal 2 hours.  The crane can then be used for 
other tasks.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Reduce lubrication time by 90%. 
• Before this Best Practice, the jib crane was used all the time during lubrication process.  Now 
the jib crane is just used for eight minutes approximately. 
• Improve ergonomics and safe handling. 
 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• Fabrication Workshop. 
• The average cost of this device is $ 1,500 USD (based on $50/hour labor rate)
