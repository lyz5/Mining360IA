1.0 Introduction  
 
This tool facilitates the disassembly of the servo piston that controls the angle of the main hydraulic 
pumps of the 320-324D models and ensures disassembly without damage. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The servo piston needs to be removed for rebuild. The servo piston is not a fixed part. Therefore, it 
moves left and right during the removal. These movements can cause the servo piston pin to break 
during removal. 
 
In addition, it was difficult to tighten according to the test procedures SIS 2.0 (cat.com)
 during the 
assembly phase. The breakage of the servo piston pin is a high additional cost because the broken 
pin does not have a single sale. If the pin breaks, the piston, and pins must be purchased and 
replaced. 
  
A tool has been designed to prevent the pin from breaking and to facilitate the removal of the servo 
piston. The tool is placed around the servo piston during removal and prevents the piston from 
moving in either direction. The tool allows removal without breaking the pin. The piston that can be 
removed without damage is shown in the figure below.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Step 1:  
The piston in the image must be fixed because it is unstable and moves to its right-left directions. 
The pin under the servo piston engages in the fixing slot. The pin is connected to the servo piston. 
When the servo piston moves, it forces the pin to move and begins to operate in the fixing split. 
Because the fixing split is stronger than the pin, it breaks the pin during assembly and disassembly. 
The tool holds the head of the servo piston in place and prevents movement during 
assembly/disassembly.
