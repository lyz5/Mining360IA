1.0 Introduction  
 
Performance and maintenance are key issues that are affected by communication, monitoring, 
and accountability. In the age of communication and technology a focus on specific problems and 
issues can be developed to enhance machine performance and maintenance.  This can be done 
in a way that is understandable and attainable across the organization.      
 
One can see this method of execution with the Albian Sands’ Mobile Maintenance Practice 
focused on improving Physical Availability.  As the process exists now, Pre-PMs are conducted 5 
days prior to major service; this practice entails bringing the intended truck into the service shop 
for inspection and S-O-S™ .  However, this did not maximize machine downtime aligned with 
Albian’s Maintenance Strategy.  Reliability and inefficient downtime indicated that further 
investigation needed to occur. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
The performance metrics in use at Albian today utilizes time as an essential part of the 
calculation.  Analysis of shutdowns and stoppages are required when attempting to understand 
what is affecting physical availability.  The following is the site definition of a Shutdown/Stoppage: 
 
“Shutdown / Stoppage:  An event that take a machine out of service 
Shutdowns may be scheduled or unscheduled and include all types of 
maintenance and repair activities excluding daily lubes, refueling and 
inspections executed during lube or refueling activities.” 
 
Observational analysis was completed with machine fueling, lubrication, and operator break time.  
Utilizing this time for inspections are considered opportune down time and do not count against 
MTTR, MTBS and hence Physical Availability. 
 
A Daily Inspection Guide and a modified Pre-PM Program were developed to maximize the 
opportune downtime.   
 
A custom facility was built for personnel to maximize execution during these short windows.  The 
requirements of this facility included an office space, staging area for the Pre-Pm, and a small 
parts facility to execute minor repairs that could be completed during the daily inspection 
(Headlights, clamps, etc). 
* Appendix A gives detailed layout and illustrations of proper layout. 
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
• Process mapping and analysis of opportune downtime and with the mine site. 
• Develop downtime best practices with current activates and to-be activities. 
• Develop process and check sheets for the plan 
• Develop plan for the facility depending on the level of work required

4.0 Benefits 
At Albian the team was able to eliminate Pre-PM downtime by utilizing time during breaks, 
lubrication, and shift changes.  This resulted in $6,000,000 per 1.0%, and for the fleet of 797s 
they were able to save $4,794,521 on a yearly basis (based on .8%). 
 
The Daily inspections are allowing Albian to capture critical Backlogs that were not being 
captured.  Minor repairs that can be completed during fueling are also completed as needed at 
the correct item.
