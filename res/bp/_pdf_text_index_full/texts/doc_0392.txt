1.0 Introduction  
 
In order to improve adjustment history of table/circle assembly, an inspection and adjustment procedure 
was created. 
 
After some circle drive failures, it was identified that it was not evident if the assembly had been adjusted 
and, if so, by whom. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
After an occurrence of a fracture in the circle drive, it was observed some of the circle fixing shoes were 
out of specification. 
 
Since the mentioned episode , a standard inspection document was created with the Q R code of the 
procedure for table/circle assembly and the circle drive adjustment. 
 
By using the phone to read the QR code, technicians may have access to the procedure for table/circle 
assembly adjustment in SIS and/or access to the video on channel 1, which shows how the procedure 
should be performed. 
 
During the service, the technician fills out a header about the equipment’s data, found/adjusted 
values, and activity's technician identification aiming to create a maintenance history document 
(Image 1) of the table/circle set, allowing a better control of the implement's maintenance activities.
