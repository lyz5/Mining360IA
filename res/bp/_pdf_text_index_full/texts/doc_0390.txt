1.0 Introduction  
 
This Best Practice was developed with the purpose of performing the test and optimal, effective, and 
reliable adjustment in Cylinder Liner Projection, guaranteeing its specification in accordance with the 
criteria CAT Cylinder Liner Projection and Head Bolt Torque Procedure for the Spacer Plate on 
Certain 3500 Engines (M0095046-02). 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Illustration # 01 the measures that must be done in Allowable Liner Projection during the assembly 
process are observed. 
 
 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
In the Barloworld Mongolia machinery workshop, we developed a device that allows testing and 
adjustment in Allowable Liner Projection for CAT 3500 engine models, in a safe, fast, and reliable 
way, reducing the time of this test by 40%<.
