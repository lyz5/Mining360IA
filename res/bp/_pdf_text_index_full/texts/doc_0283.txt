1.0 Introduction  
 
 
During final drive rebuild, it is necessary to inspect and evaluate the duo cone seal ramp for 
reusability or salvage according to SEBF8132.  This Best Practice is a simple gauge that can be 
fabricated and used by a technician to quickly and accurately evaluate the seal ramp. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
 
The key dimensions of the duo cone that need to be checked are shown in Figure 1 below.   
These checks are required to determine is the ramp can be reuse, salvaged, or must be replaced.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
 
• Fabricate a gauge.
 
• Locate the duo cone seal ramp in the component.  
• Place the gauge in the duo cone profile (See Figure 3 and 4 below).  
• Use a light to check for any gaps between the gauge and the ramp.  That light 
represents the wear on the ramp.
