1.0 Introduction  
 
Site has experienced some instances of missing 994K bucket shrouds CapSure system returning 
to the “unlock” position. 
To prevent these occurrences the Technical Team is using a section of Seal-O-Ring (PN 6G-
1546) in the CapSure slot. This prevents material from accumulating in the CapSure slot ensuring 
the locking system is fully actuated and remains in the “locked” position thereby preventing 
shroud loss. 
Additionally, by preventing the accumulation and packing of material in the CapSure slot, 
downtime associated with shroud changes is reduced.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
1) Cut a 95 mm long section of Seal-O-Ring PN 6G-1546 or similar with cross section diameter 
of 12.7 mm. 
2) Use screwdriver to install Seal-O-Ring into slot.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Ensures CapSure system is placed and remains in “Lock” position. 
• Prevents the accumulation of material in the CapSure slot. 
• Easy slot cleaning when maintaining shrouds.  
• Reduces shrouds change time. 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• Seal-O-Ring 6G-1546 or similar with cross section diameter of 12.7 mm.
