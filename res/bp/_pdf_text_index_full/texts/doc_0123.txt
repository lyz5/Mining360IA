1.0 Introduction  
 
This Best Practice will describe the steps taken by Marcosa to recruit, train and develop residents 
from the community in the operation of mining equipment, with social responsibility to meet the 
customer’s needs. The goal of this training process is the local development and qualification of 
manpower. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Marcosa, in partnership with the customer and municipalities near the mine site region, created a 
project to recruit people from the local communities and train them in mining equipment operation, 
directed to the CAT fleet. The project was called Operators Training School. Over five hundred 
candidates participated in the selection process, competing for forty vacancies. The process 
consists of the following steps: 
• C urriculum analysis 
• T heoretical evaluations 
• Practice on CAT simulators 
• P sychological test 
• Clinical evaluation 
 
The candidates must have a valid driver’s license to participate; previous experience in operation 
is not required.  
 
During the process, the instructors from Marcosa evaluate the candidates with the greatest ability 
for equipment operation. The training program lasts one year, and the apprentices are constantly 
monitored to identify and close skill gaps. After the training program, the operators who have 
successfully accomplished the requirements are certified by Marcosa and are able to operate 
equipment such as: OHTs, Track Type Tractors, Wheel Loaders and Motor Graders.  Once 
certified, they will be hired by the customer.

DEALER BEST PRACTICE SERIES 
 
Responsibilities:  
 
Dealer: 
- Provide theoretical and practical training for the participants 
- Strictly comply with the legislation in force 
- Send monthly reports with detailed information regarding the performed activities, performance of 
the participants, strategy and recommendations to the customer 
- Provide qualified instructor(s), one human analyst and other necessary manpower to lead the 
training and activities regarding the Operators Training School 
- Participate in monthly meetings with the customer to discuss the results and present the monthly 
report 
- Provide simulators for the selection process and training 
 
Customer: 
- Provide a safety technician for the monitoring of the activities on the jobsite 
- Provide meal, transportation, uniform and PPE’s during the training program 
- Pay the participants monthly scholarship assistance 
- Create a commitment and conduct term for the training period 
- Perform the selection process with the support of the Dealer 
- Provide the necessary equipment for the practical training 
- Provide access authorization to mine for the participants during the practical training, according to 
internal procedure 
- Supervise and evaluate the quality of the training 
- Monitor the development of the program, suggest improvements and give recommendations 
- Provide an employee to assist the instructor full time, during the period of practical training 
- Compensate the Dealer with the costs derived from the Operators School 
- Provide transportation for the participants on the jobsite during the period of practical training 
- Provide one diesel vehicle 4x4 for the Dealer’s instructor 
 
Municipalities: 
- Provide one adequate room for the execution of the in classroom and simulators training in their 
jurisdiction; (the room shall be equipped with electricity, water, coffee, air conditioner, tables and 
chairs in sufficient quantity, for the perfect development of the activities) 
- Provide adequate sanitary facilities (male and female) 
- Ensure proper lighting and comfort to all people involved 
- Publicize the program in their jurisdiction

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
• Set up a meeting with the customer and municipalities to discuss the scope of the project 
• Define responsibilities 
• Define number of participants and duration of the program 
• Elaborate the training schedule 
• Define the selection process and criteria 
• Publicize the program (Operators School) Media outlets: outdoors, local radio and newspaper, 
t-shirts, etc 
• Execute the selection process 
• Begin the training program 
• Evaluate and certify the participants 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• For the Customer: 
 
 Increased operational safety 
 Costs reduction with training and facilities 
 Increased productivity 
 Better inspection quality by the operators 
 Establishment of a new mining culture: Maximum zeal for the equipment and operational 
excellence of the mining equipment 
 O perator turnover reduction 
 Additional dealer support and strengthening of the partnership between dealer and 
customer; 
 Specific training schedule according to the customer’s needs 
 Individual follow up 
 Greater sense of responsibility by the operators 
 Greater development of the manpower 
 Introduction of new practices, skills and knowledge 
 Accelerate the implementation of operational improvements 
 Commitment to social responsibility 
 Utilization of local talents 
 
 
• For the Community: 
 
 Greater professional development and recognition 
 Greater incentive for self development 
 Increased investments in the region 
 Encouraging to professionalism 
 Increase of the employment rate

5.0 Resources Required 
 
• Human:  
- One safety Technician  
- One human resources analyst 
- Two operational instructors focused on the program (Suggestion: one Instructor for every 20 
participants) 
- One employee to assist the instructor 
 
• Features: 
- Study material (Caterpillar literature) 
- PPE’s and uniforms 
- Facilities (classrooms, toilets, etc.) 
- Auxiliary material (Notebook, Exercise books, projector, etc.) 
- Simulators (OHT, Wheel loader, Motor grader, Track type tractor) 
- Media outlets
