1.0 Introduction 
 
There is an opportunity for contamination to enter the clean side of the oil system on 793F, 795F 
and 797F during a routine engine oil PM. If the oil filter canister covers are not sufficiently clean, 
the contamination left on the covers can enter the clean side of the filters causing reduced engine 
life.  We have witnessed these covers not being cleaned and know that debris will enter into the 
clean side of the oil system. This contamination will damage the rod and main bearings, but by 
how much is dependent on the size and amount of debris in the cap. 
 
In addition, when removing the oil pump suction screen, contamination can enter into the system 
while the screen is being inspected and cleaned. 
 
This Best Practice aims at reducing the chance for contamination as well as reducing the amount 
of time required to complete the PM through the use of swing components.  Utilizing the "swing" 
cover approach will save around 10-15 minutes per PM, but the big advantage is in quality of the 
PM.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
For the benefits to be realized in PM efficiency, CC compliance, and achieving engine life, the 
dealer or customer will need to have at least one extra set of oil filter canister covers (with plugs) 
and an oil pump suction screen. Good Contamination Control practices recommend these 
components be placed in sealed plastic bags and delivered to the bay as part of a Standard PM 
Kit (BP number 1007-2.0-1105). When the new engine oil filter elements are installed a technician 
should place a new O-ring seal onto the clean replacement covers, and then install onto the oil 
filter canister. After removal of the oil pump suction screen, the clean replacement screen can be 
installed along with the cover. 
 
         
                    
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
• Procure a spare set of oil filter canister covers, plugs and engine oil suction screen 
• Determine method for cleaning and bagging of components 
• Implement cleaned covers and suction screen into the engine oil PM kit

4.0 Benefits 
• Reduction of contamination entering the engine lube system 
• Reduced time spent cleaning components 
• Suction screen will be cleaned and material will be inspected by a qualified technician for 
more consistent interpretation 
• Mine sites vary from $5K to $25K per truck per hour. If you use a conservative $10k per 
hour that is $166 per min or $1666 every 10 minutes. So you can see that in time savings 
alone you will pay for the swing components on the first PM on the first truck. Currently 
there is no way to quantify how much you are shortening the life of the engine if you 
choose to not use swing covers and screen.
