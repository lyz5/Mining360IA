1.0 Introduction  
 
Following the disassembly of excavators and dozers, the resulting guard pieces are not properly 
disposed in a storage area of the workshop. As such, the repair process can be delayed and 
inefficient due to parts being unevenly scattered around multiple pallets. 
 
 To overcome this issue, a guard storage vehicle was designed with a vertical partition feature. 
This feature will then allow the guard parts to be stored altogether and to occupy less space. 
Another design feature is the adjustability of the vehicle in the sense that technicians can 
customize the gaps between iron partitions according to their needs.  
 
Overall, there will be an increase in efficiency and simplicity; the relevant parts will be 
compartmentalized and transported simultaneously to the washing and measurement 
department.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
To build the designed vehicle, the welding department mounted the wheels under a sheet plate to 
provide mobility, and added partitions on top of the plate at specific intervals. Furthermore, the 
intervals between the partitions were designed to be adjustable as needed by the technicians. A  
handle was also attached to facilitate directions and usage. By using this vehicle, storing the 
guard parts has become more organized while transitioning between departments has become 
easier.

4.0 Benefits 
 
1- Improvement in process speed and efficiency  
2- Improvement in process flow due to parts’ compartmentalization (transporting, washing, 
removing-attaching process) 
3- Prevention of irregular placement of the guard parts. (The extra pallet occupation was removed) 
4- Gaps’ customization in between iron partitions as needed by the technicians.
