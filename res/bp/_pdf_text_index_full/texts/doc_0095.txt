1.0 Introduction  
 
Unnecessary time was being used to manually enter Continuous Improvement (CI) suggestions that 
were documented on paper into Excel. At the same time, a lot of paper was being used needlessly. 
Situations had occurred where ideas and suggestions were being misrepresented through double 
handling of information. Also, there had been instances of lost CI papers which lead to wasted time 
attempting to rectify. This process was also deemed not entirely efficient as decisions made about 
suggestions were communicated to the technicians manually. As a result, some technicians could not 
be informed about the final status of their continuous improvement proposal.  
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
In the current situation, technicians write their suggestions one by one on paper. The paper is left on 
the board and the quality expert takes it from the papers and manually makes an excel entry. The 
proposal is then evaluated by a committee and the technician is expected to be informed of the final 
status of the proposal by the relevant engineer and the relevant team leader. Borusan has come up 
with a more efficient process to capture CI initiatives and replace their CI board.  
There is a feature such as recording the data of the current application on a platform that can be 
accessed by the quality expert after the user clicks the button. Each record is transmitted to the online 
platform in an accessible format. Data can be opened in preferred formats such as Excel, pdf, html. In 
addition, there is an ability to send notifications to encourage technician. The desired notifications will 
be able to send to technicians and it is possible to add visuals to the notification content. If 
technicians will transmit suggestions by using the application, it will be prevented from entering the 
suggestions written on paper one by one in Excel. 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Step 1: Click the application icon and enter the application. While using the application, the device 
must be connected to the internet.

4.0 Benefits 
 
1) Fast tracking mechanism 
 
2) Process efficiency with digitalization 
 
3) Sharing the results of the suggestions instantly with the relevant technicians 
 
4) Instant notification of the technicians who needs feedback 
 
5) Simple and user-friendly application interface 
 
6) Elimination of the paper waste
