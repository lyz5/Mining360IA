1.0 Introduction  
 
Current process for installation of the 3500 engine oil filter base valve springs (PN: 7G-6713) and th e 
respective cover is performed manually. Compressing  the spring to the closed position of the cover, 
requires the technician to exert a considerable amo unt of force (17Kg) in a small area. This task 
involves and requires a great amount of physical effort, and with multiple oil filter base covers/springs 
this task becomes uncomfortable and tiring for the technician. 
 
 
Figure 1. Procedure manual installation of the cover 
 
This Best practice aims to facilitate the development of this task to design a user-friendly process that 
is fast and safe to use. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This tool applies to the bases of 3500 engine oil filters.  The press consists of a C-shaped piece made 
of 7/16" steel grade 514.  Compression is performed by an endless 7/8" screw with a pitch of 7 mm, 
with a C-shaped tip that rotates freely to accommodate the spring covers on the oil filter bases.  Also, 
two screws are used that are similar to the original cover screws but longer (4"), guide the cover while 
being compressed and prevent rotation. 
 
  
Figure 2. Tool Architecture. 
 
The tool weight is 1.7kg. The spring requires a force equal to or greater than 17 kg.  For this reason 
this system is in the form of a press which facilitates the installation.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
• Check the status of the tool to verify the integri ty before use. 
• Place spring and cover fitting with guide screws. (1) 
• Install the tool "C" ensuring proper engagement wi th the flat bottom of the base, turn the screw 
(down) until the tool is self-supporting the product of the force exerted by the spring (2). 
• Next bring down the screw cap to its final install ation position (3), then manually remove the 
interim guides and install the screws (bolts).  
• You should remove one guide and install one bolt, then repeat for the other, so that you never 
have the tool on the part without a bolt or guide screw in place. 
• Finally tighten to the required torque (4). 
  
Figure 3. Procedure for operation of the tool. 
 
• After installing the cap, release the lead screw to remove the tool  
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Easy fabrication 
• Easy operation 
• Improved component assembly 
• Spring secure installation, reduces accidents 
• Decreases fatigue avoid manual installation techni cian
