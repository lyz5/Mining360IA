1.0 Introduction 
Oil filters must be inspected during maintenance in order to detect malfunctions of the components 
in advance and to prevent future malfunctions. It is necessary to remove the filter from the 
machine, cut the filter and check the elements in the filter.  
T
wo best practices have been published in this area of work. One, the Oil Filter Cutting Bench, is a 
stand that holds the filter while it’s being cut, and aids in the process of removing oil from the filter 
to check for abnormal elements. The other best practice, OHT Transmission & Hydraulic Filter 
Replacement Tool, is a ladder stand that allows the technician to safely and easily reach the filter 
and securely hold it while it’s being cut. These best practices are more suited for shops and pits. 
However, there is not always an ability to check the filter in these areas. This portable filter cutting 
tool has been developed to provide easier service and maintenance processes in the field. 
T
his tool ensures that the filters are cut without damaging the natural environment and the working 
environment. For example, m ost of the operations performed in the field use a variety of methods 
that cause work accidents, such as hand injuries, but this tool helps to reduce those accidents. 
This tool was also developed with a large oil sump that is aimed at eliminating the risk of oil 
leaking to the environment. This tool allows for easy portability as well, allowing machine 
inspections and maintenance to be performed in different locations, such as service vehicles.
