1.0 Introduction 
For machine inspections, the general tool requirements are: 
• Pressure test gages
• Pressure hoses
• Blowby hose
• Blowby meter and its attachments
• Computer
• ET control device
• Aerometer
• Thermometer
• Hydrometer battery tester
• Voltmeter
• Filter cutter tool
T
his best practice provides an improvement it two areas: 
1. Handling
Handling this equipment in separate individual carry cases can or result six or seven cases being 
used. Since the cases can be heavy, the technician would need to make several trips (time 
consuming) or risk damaging boxes and equipment. Those plastic cases are often too firm, intending 
to protect the attachments and devices, but still often becoming damaged as they move around. 
2. Safety
When attempting to carrying out a variety of pressure tests with a limited number of gages available, 
a safety risk can occur. The safety risk occurs when a hoses and gages are required to be handled 
by the technician (both removal and re-fitting) in order to test various pressures without having 
sufficient time to allow oil and components to cool.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
The Machine Inspection Technician Toolbox enables its user to carry its inspection dev ices and 
attachments easily, quickly and safely. 
The toolbox consists of 5 shelves containing the following:  
1. Twenty pressure test gages of various ratings
2. Aerometer, Thermometer, Hydrometer battery tester, Voltmeter and a Filter cutter tool
3. Computer and ET control device
4. Blowby meter and attachments
5. Pressure hoses and a blowby hose
T
he fifth shelf is larger than other shelves providing space for  longer hoses. Sty rofoam is used to 
secure the equipment. More details and a sketch can be found in the Supporting Attachments / 
References part.
