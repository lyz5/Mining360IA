1.0 Introduction 
Taking time during a PM to inspect and clean reusable screens, filters and magnetic plugs can 
directly impact maintenance time. Utilizing a set of rotating maintenance parts, such as clean 
screens or a Centrifugal COF with new seals during the PM and cleaning the parts after the truck 
has been returned to service and before the next PM, will reduce the PM timeline and increase 
availability of a technician during machine downtime.  
2.0 Best Practice Description 
To reduce the PM timeline, it’s necessary to have additional parts so that the removed parts can be 
cleaned, analyzed and potentially repaired post PM. Those parts are proposed on the next table 
(1).  Rolling these parts reduce 3.5 hours on every PM.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
1. Train and inform about the PM technical personnel about the rolling parts that must be ready
for the maintenance
2. Every 6 month evaluate the rolling parts to assess if some parts are worn or have any defects
that could affect the performance
3. Send magnetic plugs to CM analyst to inspect & record results, creating a history of weight,
color, etc.
