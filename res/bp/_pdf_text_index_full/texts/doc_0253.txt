MINING DEALER BEST PRACTICE  
 
 
1.0 Introduction  
The assembly of the large planetaries (992) involves the manipulation of heavy gears. These 
gears create a danger for the technician during the lifting and placement of the gear and the 
process is time consuming. 
 
A lifting  tool was designed  to support the gear during installation and alignment with the pin 
bores. This tool reduces the safety risk, parts damage, and decreases the assembly time. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
• Easy transportation 
• Easy handling 
• Easy storage 
• Reliant and adjustable for different gears sizes. 
• High adhesive paint to eliminate contamination from the painted support surface 
• Minimum maintenance

carrier.

4.0 Benefits 
-The installation of the large planetary gears is safer for the technician and the assembly is faster. 
-With this tool the technician does not have to do any lifting of planetary gears. 
-This tool can also be used when a hydraulic press is needed to install the planetary gears that          
require pressing the pin in to the carrier.
