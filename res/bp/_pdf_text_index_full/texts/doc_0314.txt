1.0 Introduction  
Certain areas in dealer shops require frequent consultation of Caterpillar literature. Information in 
guides and manuals contains steps to accomplish assembly or disassembly, schematics of 
components, and other important information. 
 
This Best Practice presents a solution in the form of a shop-installed computer workstation 
connected wirelessly to the Internet, providing all needed Caterpillar information quickly and 
easily. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Optimizing resources, such as tools and labor -hours, is an important aspect within processes. In  
the case of shops, technical areas get support in the form of parts manuals and equipment 
schematics that are available in Caterpillar applications on Internet.  
 
Three years ago, necessary literature had to be printed and distributed to technicians of the shop 
by the literature analyst. This technique incurred costs like: paper, prints, and time of literature 
analyst and technicians to obtain parts manuals needed. A bottleneck was created while the 
literature analyst delivered manuals to technicians. To relieve this bottleneck, a new project was 
implemented. 
 
The project consisted of installing wireless workstations in each bay of the shop where 
technicians would have access to Internet and therefore to Caterpillar literature. 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
• Configure and/or install a wireless network. 
• Assemble wireless workstation, details are shown on figure 1. 
• Configure each workstation with needed options 
 
4.0 Benefits  
 
• Improved labor cost profit margins 
• Optimization of man-hour labor 
• Increased shop productivity and higher revenues  
• Reduce time on getting information from manuals 
• Online access to updated information in all areas 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
  
• Wireless network: Allow access to Internet and applications. 
• Training for technicians and supervisors to know how to operate workstation 
• Thin Client with wireless card: Allow thin client get connected to wireless network 
• 15’’ Flat Monitor 
• Barcode Scanner: Its function is to read technician’s ID from their ID card and code labeled on 
the piece or component 
• Keyboard with touchpad
