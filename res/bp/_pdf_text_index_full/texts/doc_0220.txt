1.0 Introduction 
 
This Best Practice is a result of Six Sigma methodology at Ferreyros’ Machine Shop.  After the 
final machining of top deck, we need to clean the metal spray waste from water passage holes, 
threaded holes, dowel holes, and corners of the top deck. 
 
Previously, reconditioned block with metal spray was cleaned using different file types and 
manual threaded taps. The job required 16 man-hours. Currently, using different kinds of tools the 
effective time is 9 man-hours. 
 
Both former and current procedures of cleaning will be described below.

Currently, for the cleaning of metal spray waste, the following is required: 
 
• Special taps 
• Special drill cutting tool 
• Cylindrical and conical cutting tool 
• Mounted stones 
 
The technique is simple: install the cylindrical or conical cutting pneumatic emery tool to perform 
cleaning camshaft inspection windows, curved areas and adjacent surfaces. Then, for cleaning 
dowel holes, mounted stones are used.  Next step is to clean cylinder head holes for which a 
device is used as a guide and special drill cutting tool. Finally in cleaning side and bottom holes a 
threaded is used with special tap (machine tap).

Works involved are: 
 
• Cleaning dowel holes & threaded holes: 80 min per cylinder block 
• Removing burrs: 30 min per cylinder block 
• Bottom cylinder block: 30 min 
• Sanding (finishing):30 min 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
• Define the areas to be cleaned. 
• Use the toolkit as detailed in subsection 5 “Resou rces Required” 
• Cleaning Process: 
o Cleaning camshaft windows 
o Cleaning curved areas 
o Cleaning dowels areas 
o Cleaning adjacent surfaces  
o Cleaning cylinder head holes area 
o Cleaning threaded holes 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
The main benefit of this Best Practice is to reduce cylinder block cleaning time, the following 
graphs show the cleaning time before and after. 
 
• Reduction of man-hours by 44% 
• Less time for cylinder blocks cleaning. Old proces s 16 hours, current process 9 hours. 
• Work improvement, more effective working with tech nology that facilitates the tasks.
