1.0 Introduction  
 
           The purpose of this document is to provide methodology to test, and assess the condition of DC Bus 
           (DC Link) Capacitors, DC Bus Precharge/Discharge Resistors, and the Ground Tie (Ground Fault)  
           Resistors on board the 7495 shovels. 
 
DC Bus Capacitors are utilized in the IGBT Skid (Motion Drive) to stabilize and store DC Current 
produced by the AFE (Active Front End) IGBT’s while working in the Motoring (Rectification) Mode. 
 
This current is then used by the individual Motion Inverters (Hoist, Swing and Crowd) to produce  
Variable Frequency AC Current, required for fluent motor speed and torque control: 
 
 
 
  ELECTROCUTION HAZARD - The electrical system contains hazardous voltage levels. 
DO NOT remove any covers that will expose energized hazardous-voltage electrical 
components without shutting down and discharging the electrical system. 
DO NOT perform any maintenance on the electrical system until after the electrical system has 
been shut down and the voltage has been discharged by qualified personnel. 
Read and understand the instructions and warnings found in the Operation and Maintenance 
Manual to properly shut down the electrical system and discharge hazardous-voltage 
electrical components. 
Failure to follow these instructions will result in personal injury or death. 
 
 
When service or maintenance must be performed in a hazardous-voltage component 
enclosure, always assume that a hazardous voltage level could be present. 
Qualified personnel should manually check for hazardous voltage using electrical measuring 
tools that have the capacity to measure the hazardous-voltage components being checked. 
The voltage level present must be confirmed to be below 50 Volts before any exposure to the 
hazardous-voltage components can take place. 
Failure to follow these instructions could result in personal injury or death.

In order to quickly verify whether or not the DC Bus Pre-charge/Discharge Resistors and the Ground 
Tie Resistors did not deteriorate with time and operating conditions, it is recommended to obtain 
quick reference reading of DC Bus Resistance, should the actual total DC Bus Resistance be 
significantly different then calculated value of 4817 Ω  +/_ 300 Ω 
 
To perform this task, ensure that all applicable safety procedures have been applied, and that the DC 
Bus has been discharged. 
 
At this point, locate the DC Bus (DC Link) Voltage Transducers U152 (wires 0842 & 0843) and U452 
(wires 0742 & 0743), and use a quality multimeter set to Ohm's scale, to read the DC Bus Total 
Resistance. Record this value, to be used later in Capacitor Test Calculator.

4.0 Benefits 
 
Early detection of DC Bus capacitance fluctuation will ensure solid power source for the IGBT 
Modules. 
 
It has been proven through historical machine failure data that failure of DC Link Capacitor will 
consequently lead to failure of the IGBT Module, and thus testing of the DC Link Capacitors 
should become a part of annual preventive maintenance practices. 
Implementation of this practice will increase machine reliability, and control Total Operating Costs 
of the 7495 Shovel.
