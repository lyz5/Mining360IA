1.0 Introduction 
The Caterpillar Pump Drive is one important component of the powertrain. Its  main purpose is to 
transfer power from the engine to the hoist , brake, steering, and transmission systems by driving the 
hoist, rear brake cooling, steering, and transmission pumps. Lubrication of the pump drive is provided 
by oil from the torque converter charging pump section. 
T
he field technician came up with the aim of a simple and practical way to better monitor the pump 
drive wear by installing the magnetic plug in place of the existing conventional plug. In addition to the 
maintenance and inspection procedures Caterpillar had already established, magnetic plug inspection 
will certainly be an advantage when the decision of whether repairing the pump drive must be taken.  
2
.0 Best Practice Description 
T
he main goal of this best practice is to provide a way for maintenance personnel to better monitor 
component wear  throughout its life upon magnetic plug inspection within scheduled maintenance 
intervals. Currently, customers only use the SOS program to analy ze the hydraulic oil condition, 
therefore it is not possible to determine whether  the wear is from the p ump drive o r from another  
component.

he purpose of the existing plug replacement by a magnetic plug is to determine if there are any

analysis.

4.0 Benefits 
• W
ear source can be found quickly.
• Reduced downtime with diagnoses that is efficient utilization of man hour. One technician can
perform the diagnostic in less than 1 hour.
• Better condition monitoring process and increased pump drive life.
• Avoid misunderstanding and misinterpretation of metallic particles source found in S.O.S.
• Reduce the unscheduled downtimes. The performance time to diagnose and R&I is reduced to
3 hours which is a 50% reduction.
