1.0 Introduction  
 
One of the main goals of mining companies around the operations of mobile equipment is to ensure 
safe conditions, good component lives, and the high productivity of technicians during maintenance.  
 
Most of the manufacturers worked to improve their machine serviceability to avoid component 
damages and deliver a better bottom line in maintenance. This includes filter relocations, design 
changes, new models to perform PMs, etc. All of it going to help the people in front of the line about 
this subject matter. 
 
However, there are innumerous site conditions that have their particularities that affect the 
technician’s mindset to perform maintenance achieving the technical quality levels recommended by 
Caterpillar. 
 
A Mining Site in Upper North Brazil uses to face high contamination levels into the 16M Tandem, 
affecting the brakes and oil cleanliness. Also, the shop technician’s feedback reported some difficulty 
to perform breather replace during the preventive maintenance. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
                 Picture 1 - Breather (4H-6112) place faces contamination around bauxite operations. 
 
This Best Practice: 
 
Caterpillar SEBU9228-17 literature recommends replacing the Breather (4H -6112) on the 
impossibility of removal for cleaning, or advanced state of damage. It is also recommended a 500-
hour period to replace this part during preventive maintenance.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This Best Practice prevents contamination by various types of debris, promoting changes in the 
Breather of the Caterpillar MG 16 Motor Grader, which reduces risks to the equipment's integrity, 
simplifying cleaning and adding dynamism to maintenance.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
For the procedure of repositioning the b reather, Caterpillar literature PECP5030 -11 was used for 
technical guidance on the execution of the assembly procedures, from a mining customer's need, 
this Best Practice was developed respecting the existing Caterpillar standardization for 
compatibility of the fluids and dimensioning of the threads on the terminals. 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
•  Reduced risk of Tandem contamination during routine operation. 
•  More simple maintenance/replacement processes during preventive maintenance. 
•  Improved accessibility. 
•  Decreased time required for maintenance.
