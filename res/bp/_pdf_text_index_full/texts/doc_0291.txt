1.0 Introduction  
This document describes how to modify 373-5448 Rocker Stand to 525-1901 Rocker Stand in 
quick and simple three steps. Using One Point Lesson describes the steps for drilling a hole with 
a jig made for the task. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
The improvement consists of the use of a simple procedure OPL to modify 373-5448 into 525-
1901   Rocker Stand. 
 
For machining the oil hole, it is necessary to follow these three steps: 
 
Step 1: Prepare a jig and locate it on a vertical milling machine. 
 
Step 2: Install the 373-5448 Rocker Stand on the jig 
 
Step 3: Drill the oil hole 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
Manufacture a simple jig, like the figure show below:
