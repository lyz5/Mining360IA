1.0  Introduction 
  
Most vertical parts washers are up to 14 feet tall and used to clean engine blocks, spindles, or 
baskets containing piece parts.  Most have the pumps and motors contained in the bottom of the 
washer cabinet with an additional base that is typically 3 feet high.  As a result, the loading 
platform of the washer is 4 to 5 feet above the floor level.  
 
The height of the platform above the floor level increases the time and effort required to load and 
unload parts and components

2.0  Best Practice Description 
A quick and simple way of improving the of lifting and handling parts mount the washer is a  “silo” 
or pit in the floor so that the load platform is close to floor level.  The silo will need to be large 
enough to provide access to the base of the washer for routine maintenance and repair.  The 
exposed silo area can be covered with removable grating.

3.0  Implementation Steps 
 
Construct a silo considering the dimensions of the washing machine so that the loading platform 
is located at the height of the floor.  Several dealers who have implemented this practice have 
been very pleased with the benefits. 
 
4.0  Benefits 
 
• Reduces the time of entry and exit of components t o the washing machine. 
• Improves technician ergonomic.  
• Reduce time for cleaning the solvent tank.
