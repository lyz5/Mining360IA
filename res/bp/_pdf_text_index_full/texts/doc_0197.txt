1.0 Introduction 
 
C175 Engines that have the top deck milled at overhaul may require a thicker cylinder head 
integral seal than the standard seal (reference Reuse and Salvage Guideline SEBF9185 for this 
specification).  This Best Practice describes a method to call attention to a field service person, or 
a CRC partial repair bay, to an engine requiring the thicker seal. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This Dealer has implemented a practice of affixing a stamped aluminum plate to each valve cover 
using epoxy on an engine that was milled under the specification.  An additional plate is put next 
to the Component ID tag on the engine.  
In this example, the plate reads: 
 
ENGINE BLOCK REQUIRES 
THICKER HEAD GASKETS 
USE P/N 394-0295 
 
RENO CRC

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Have these plates made up, and add necessary processes to control documents as required.  
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
This practice ensures the correct seal is installed during a repair that involves removing the cylinder 
head, to eliminate oil leakage and potential engine damage.
