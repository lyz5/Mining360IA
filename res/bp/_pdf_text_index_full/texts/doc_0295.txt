1.0 Introduction  
 
When the 385C FS Engine (EAM prefix) was tested with a DYNO, the oil leaked from the rear 
housing of the engine with the gears installed.  To prevent this, the gears were not installed 
during the assembly of the engine, they were installed after the engine was tested. While this 
method fixed the problem with the oil leakage, it has caused extra movements to the engine like 
the assembly – test – more assembly. 
 
This tool allows testing of the engine with the gears installed without the oil leakage.  
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Borusan Makina Kazakhstan has developed the cap-like adapter that is installed into the rear 
housing of the engine by using 3 retaining bolts, CAT screws (8 x 0T-0772 & 2 x 0T-0320) & CAT 
seal (1 x 3D-8417) and prevents the oil leakage during the DYNO test.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Prevents the oil leakage during the engine DYNO te st. 
• Removes the necessity to install the gears after t he engine DYNO test. 
 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• 10 x CAT screws (8 x 0T-0772 & 2 x 0T-0320); 
• 1 x CAT seal (1 x 3D-8417); 
• 3 x retaining bolts made by the turner; 
• 1 x 6V-5043 SEAL-O-RING
