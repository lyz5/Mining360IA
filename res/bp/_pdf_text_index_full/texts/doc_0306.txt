1.0 Introduction  
 
For the accurate measurement of the backlash between the bevel gear and pinion in Caterpillar 
wheel loaders, it is important to keep the pinion gear in a fixed position while taking 
measurements.  This Best Practice is a simple fabricated tool to lock the pinion gear while 
measuring backlash.  Refer to machine Service Manuals for differential bevel gear assembly, 
procedure to test / adjust bevel gear backlash.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Ferreyros has developed a very simple tool to fix the pinion, for measuring the backlash of the 
bevel gear and pinion. 
This tool allows easier measurement of the backlash without repeating the procedure several 
times. This tool enables accurate and reliable measurement to be performed in the least amount 
of time. 
 
 
 
Process: 
 
1. Set the tool to the housing pinion with 4 bolts. 
 
2.  Adjust the jaws according the diameter of the splined shaft pinion. 
 
3. Tighten 2 bolts to secure jaws to the pinion.
