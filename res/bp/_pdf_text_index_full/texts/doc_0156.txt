1.0 Introduction  
 
A compartment breather filter plays a critical role in extending component life. The breather filter 
must allow air to easily pass through it while preventing microscopic particulate particles from 
entering the compartment and contaminating the fluid with abrasive debris. 
 
Fuel, hydraulic and powertrain systems use a wide variety of breather filters, which means a mine 
must stock multiple filters to accommodate all machines in its fleet. There are no specific design 
guidelines for the size, type, or efficiency of breather filters. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
On newer machines, increasingly sophisticated, high-performance systems and components 
require very clean fluids. Most standard breather filters are not efficient enough to provide 
maximum protection to new systems. In addition, a mine must stock multiple part numbers of 
standard breathers to service a fleet. 
 
An alternative is to standardize on the use of spin-on High Efficiency 4-micron fuel filters as 
breathers for all compartments. This includes fuel, hydraulic, transmission, etc. These filters are 
very high performance and reduce the number of breather part numbers that have to be stocked. A 
common practice is to use the same HE fuel filter as is used on one or more of the machines in the 
fleet.  
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
When used as breather filters, HE fuel filters are mounted upside down so that falling dirt and 
debris are deflected from the top of the filter. The filter is mounted on a combination mounting stud 
and mounting bracket that is fabricated by the dealer. A hose is then installed between the 
mounting bracket and compartment. Care must be taken to insure that only non-vented 
compartment caps are used so that all air entering and leaving the compartment must pass 
through the breather filter. 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
Dust entering fluid compartments through inefficient breather filters is often a source of fluid 
contamination. This can be easily prevented with the use of HE fuel filters as breathers. When 
used as fuel tank breathers, HE filters have reduced or eliminated premature fuel filter plugging in 
extremely dusty applications. HE fuel filters are also larger than standard breathers and have much 
greater capacity. There have been no documented cases of these filters plugging. They are 
typically replaced only once or twice a year. 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
Mounting brackets and hoses must be fabricated and installed by the dealer. This requires very 
simple machining, welding and hose-making capabilities. See diagram contained Component Life 
Management Master Document PDF (click on attachments in this file).
