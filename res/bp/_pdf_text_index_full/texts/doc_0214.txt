MINING DEALER BEST PRACTICE  
 
1.0 Introduction  
 
Improved threading tool allows a considerable reduction of time and costs in cleaning blocks, 
implemented in the machine shop of Ferreyros. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The focus of this Best Practice is to reduce the time and costs for cleaning waste such as grease, 
oxides, Lock-tite. This improvement occurs as a result of the high cost that generated use 
manuals HSS Taps Thread, increasing the cleaning time of blocks (See picture 1 and 2).
