1.0 Introduction  
 
In Caterpillar Service Information System, there is a procedure for an installation of a new rod pin 
bearing. In this procedure SEBF8242, the pin bearing of the rod is heated between 177 to 260 °C 
by using 6V-6996 Induction Heater.  
Borusan Makina Kazakhstan installs the pin bearing of the rod by cooling bearing in a freezer 
instead of heating the rod. It significantly reduces the time spent for the installation. 
 
This method of installation of new rod pin bearing is applicable for any connecting rods, the 
general procedure is the same as in SEBF8242, but the bearing of the rod is cooled instead of 
being heated.  
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
In order to install the new rod pin bearing, all the steps (except heating) included in SEBF8242 
reuse and salvage guideline are performed. The current method is to take the rods which are at 
the room temperature (they are at roughly 26.6°C). After that, the bearings are cooled to 
approximately -70°C in a freezer and then they are assembled, by using 5P-8639 Hydraulic 
Press. 
 
In our company we are successfully performing this process and for today, no problems occurred. 
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Below is an example of installing of rod pin bearing by cooling for the connecting rod of 3500 
series engine. Refer to SEBF8242, for detailed procedures.  
 
1) Make a visual inspection of the rod.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• The heating of connecting rod requires time to heat, and every rod is heated separately. 
While in cooling, all the bearings can be cooled at the same time in the freezer and it 
significantly saves time. 
• Cooling is also safer than h eating. When heating the rod between 177 to 260 °C  the 
operator must be very careful, because overheating may damage the rod. Also heating 
might harm the operator.
