1.0 Introduction 
Borusan M
akina Kazakhstan has developed a stand for storing adapters and plates used in 
transmission and torque converter tests. The stand is very practical and improves safety when 
storing and handling adapters and plates.  
2.0 Best Practice Description 
The adapters and plates used in transmission tests used to be stored on the ground or leaning on 
the wall. Some of the plates weigh more than 50 kg. If the plates fell or slipped, they could cause 
serious injuries to people nearby.  
BEFORE 
To address this hazard, a storage stand has been designed for the plates. The stand is made from 
several different sized sections (keeping the Six Sigma 5S principles in mind), to hold 18 adapters 
and plates up to 950 mm wide. The dimensions of the stand can be changed to fit the needs of the 
dealer.
