1.0 Introduction  
 
The timing of the PCR of the powertrain components is one of the main factors that impact the 
availability and performance of the equipment, due to the high downtime required for execution. Smaller 
components can also significantly impact performance indicators and should be taken into account to 
meet site maintenance strategies. 
Therefore, all possibilities for mitigating the impact on the replacement process of these components 
must be considered. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This BP describes the strategy adopted by this customer to optimize the components' replacement time 
through the pre- assembly of component peripherals in a prepared maintenance box with dedicated 
people to carry out the activity. So, the new components will be ready for installation as soon as the 
previous one is removed, using the pit stop methodology to perform these activities. 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
3.1 Area and layout definition of the component box: 
 
The defined area must be strategically located close to the PCR execution box of the components in 
order to facilitate the load handling logistics and optimization of the performers' travel time. It is 
necessary that the layout allows for the best arrangement of components for pre- assembly of 
peripherals. This can be standardized with the preparation and demarcation of the layout of the work 
area in a clean, safe environment, with enough favorable space for the movement of people and 
equipment and without interference between maintenance teams, as well a good contamination control.

3.3 Acquisition of resources and tools: 
 
A list of all the basic and special tools necessary for the assembly and installation of the peripherals for 
each major component is carried out. Lift devices and hand tools listed must meet the specific demand 
for preparing these components, without including unnecessary tools in the process. 
 
    
Figure 9. Hand Tools Board Figure                                   Figure 10. Special Tools 
 
3.4 Listing/purchasing peripheral kits to install on the engine and radiator: 
 
A list of all the parts and materials necessary for the installation of the components to be prepared must 
be made. These parts must be available and prepared for installation in the components, reducing 
downtime for maintenance technicians in the search for these parts and necessary resources
. 
 
    
Figure 11, 12. Parts needed for preassembly process 
 
3.5 Alignment between execution and planning areas: 
 
All resources, parts, labor must be available according to prior planning in order to optimize the 
execution time similar to a pit stop when replacing components during scheduled maintenance of 
equipment.

4.0 Benefits 
 
4.1 Services quality improvement (provide efficient utilization of man hour and resource): 
 
With all the necessary resources available, it is possible to perform a quality service applying all the 
recommendations specified by the manufacturer, such as adequate torques, layouts and proper routing 
of hydraulic hoses and electrical harnesses, correct positioning of clamps, among others. 
 
4.2 Contamination control improvement: 
 
Working in a planned and organized way, there is enough time to carry out an effective cleaning, good 
preparation of the parts, as well as the use of appropriate chemical compounds during the assembly of 
these in the engine. 
 
4.3 Safety: 
 
Eliminates the risks of injuries, working in an appropriate place, on a flat floor, without obstructions and 
properly demarcated, eliminating the technician's exposure to the inherent risks of other activities. 
 
4.4 Time reduction of major R&I components: 
 
This methodology significantly optimizes the time required between the removal and subsequent 
installation of larger components such as the engine/ converter, radiator, suspension and front wheel 
assembly, with a consequent reduction in machine downtime.

4.4.1 Engine/Torque Converter: 
 
Engine replacement time on an LMT is reduced by 42 hours (table / graphic 01) as the components 
are pre-assembled by performing: 
• Torque converter installation; 
• Installation of the front bar with its subcomponents; 
• Installation of front and rear mounts; 
• Installation of cooling lines; 
 
    
Figure 13, 14. Engine/ TC ready to Install on the truck 
 
4.4.2 Radiator: 
 
Radiator replacement time on an LMT is reduced by 9 hours (table / graphic 01) as the component 
will be ready to install it by performing: 
• Full flushing; 
• Core and seals replacement; 
• Installation of fan hydraulic motor; 
• Installation of fan and all guards; 
• Installation of handrails and stairway; 
 
    
Figure 15, 16. Radiator ready to Install on the truck

4.4.3 Front  Wheel, Brake and Suspension Assembly: 
 
Front Wheel, Brake and Suspension Assembly replacement time on an LMT is reduced by 80 hours 
(table / graphic 01) as the front suspension will be ready to install it by performing: 
• Brake and Bearings replacement; 
• Front wheel and duo cone seals installation; 
• Bearing pre-load procedure with more reliability (Due to spindle shaft is on vertical position);
