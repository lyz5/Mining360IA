1.0 Introduction  
 
Trakindo’s Component Rebuild Centers (CRCs) have a large amount of Work in Process (WIP) when 
providing Component Rebuild engines. Two of the larger components in the CRCs are crankshaf ts 
and camshafts waiting to be installed. The current storage method for these specific components is 
inefficient, which affects the turnaround time. The CRCs end up with valuable space being taken up 
by WIP, resulting in the area looking congested, and exposing the components to potential damage.   
 
When considering contamination control and 5S, the components (3500 series crankshaft and 
camshafts or smaller) when placed on the floor using the current method take up valuable space and 
creates a potential safety hazard. In some instances when the parts are small enough they are placed 
on desk space in the CRCs.  The current method makes it difficult for the technicians to retrieve the 
component when needed.  As a result, Trakindo needed to implement a new method to allow 
crankshafts and camshafts to be stored efficiently in a clean and tidy environment, which makes 
possible safe and easy staging and transport. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description  
 
This Best Practice is for storage and staging management of the 3500 S eries Crankshafts and 
Camshafts.  It can be also applied to other models. The focus should be on the staging of the shafts 
for contamination control, easy access, maximum floor space utilization, component quality (shafts 
not bending), and technician safety.  Examples from Trakindo have been presented as well as some 
of the best in class storage methods gathered from around the world. 
 
Trakindo Crankshaft Storage 
The new method that has been implemented is to store the crankshafts in the vertical position using 
racks and a lifting bracket that is bolted onto the flywheel bolt holes in the crankshaft.  Plastic wrap is 
applied to the components to provide protection while in storage.  This provides a safe and efficient 
method of storing, lifting, and moving the crankshafts. 
 
The lifting bracket is vital so the shaft can be removed vertically without moving the other shafts in the 
rack.  The rack gage dimension must be wide enough to allow clearance for vertical lifting of the 
shaft. 
 
Trakindo created dedicated stands that can store 10 crankshafts each. A step, ladder, or crane hook 
guiding pole is required for access to the stand to lift the crankshaft into place.      
 
At least two standing rack s are needed - one for the crankshafts that have been just remove f rom 
engine and waiting for rebuild, and another one for the crankshafts that have been recondition and 
waiting to be installed.

Trakindo Camshaft Storage 
The new method that has been implemented to store the camshafts is to develop racks to hold the 
components in a vertical position.  Plastic wrap is applied to the components to provide protection 
while in storage. 
 
At least two standing rack are needed - one for the camshafts that have been just remove from 
engine and waiting  for rebuild, and another one for the camshafts that have been recondition and 
waiting to be installed. 
 
For 3500 engine series camshaft or bigger, Trakindo uses a webbing sling to lift the camshaft to avoid 
the scratching and other damage when transporting.

3.0 Implementation Step 
 
- Determine area in shop to be designated for storage and staging rack. 
- Designate Incoming and Outgoing racks in rebuild/processing work bay. 
- Assure the area has adequate space and access for equipment to move the component with a 
crane – no lift trucks.  Best to be able to move components vertically and horizontally. 
- Install the layout/equipment changes on the shop floor. 
- Promote change feedback as installation may make new problems surface. 
- Train employees to use the new procedures/equipment. 
- Review process/equipment performance once established. 
- Establish and install any adjustments as needed. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
Safety  
Well designed/organized standing rack will provide a safer and cleaner working environment. 
 
Organization  
Components are consistently staged for incoming and outgoing status.  Floor space is maximized by 
the components taking up minimal storage space. 
 
Quality  
Simplified staging and tidy WIP areas promotes technician professionalism and higher standards, 
while providing shaft protection, contamination control, and shaft cleanliness resulting in increased 
brand image. 
 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
Facility 
Fabrication material. 
Reorganized work area to allow for vertical storage area with incoming and outgoing staging. 
 
People 
Establish the new process, layout, and equipment improvement through the team. 
Remove replaced items from operation to prevent less effective storage system.
