1.0 Introduction  
 
During the NPI (New Product Introduction) process of the 794AC truck in Brazil, one of the challenges 
encountered was the lack of clear guidelines regarding the specifications and positioning of Chassis 
Stands for use as support points for the truck during the assembly process. 
 
The Customer safety guidelines recommend the positioning of the chassis must be done on fixed 
Stands and without the use of wood parts, thus requiring the manufacture of Chassis Stands in the 
correct size for each stage of the assembly process. 
 
After some information received from the experiences of other Dealers and some initial studies, the 
Chassis Stands for the trucks were made and utilized on the first assembly of 794AC Trucks. 
 
Some Chassis Stands size and placement details needed to be changed and adjusted during the first 
assembly. Based on the experience acquired, it is possible to determine the specifications of the 
Chassis Stands, which were used and validated in a new assembly at the same client, but at another 
site/mine. 
 
Based on the difficulties encountered and because it is a critical item related to cargo handling, the 
objective of this Best Practice is to present the sequencing of use and specifications of the Chassis 
Stands, to use as information reference about assembly. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
With the introduction of new equipment by the engineering team, the planning of each stage of the 
execution and assembly process needed to be defined. The teams did not have previous experience 
in the assembly of 794AC model. 
 
We searched SIS2.0 for information and recommendations for the Chassis Stands (height, 
dimensions of the upper base and other specifications) and we did not find them.  
 
We also research Jacking Locations (SEBU9099-08) on the dimensions of the mainframe support 
surfaces and did not find the information easily. Items illustrated below (1), (2) and (3). 
 
             
 
With the information provided from other dealers, about the assembly process, we got the dimensions 
of the main chassis support bases and some examples of Chassis Stands. But these models were 
not in line with our goal of prioritizing safety and productivity during the assembly process.

The objective was to work with the mainframe as close to the ground as possible and on the front 
axle, to simplify the technicians work and minimize risks according to Customer Safety guidelines. 
 
Unlike the assembly procedure (M0090261) which mentions the movement of the main frame about 
the front axle to install the fixing links, we choose to leave the main chassis static on the Chassis 
Stands and move the axle front about the main chassis during the link installation step. (Safety 
Reasons) 
 
With the Activity, we managed to minimize the risks and exposure of our technicians to the load 
suspended by cranes.

An important factor was to keep the chassis close to ground level during the assembly of the 
components, thus increasing the safety of the technicians. We positioned the main chassis on 900 
mm stands, ensuring that the front axle, rear axle, and other components could be moved and 
installed without difficulty.

After installing the Front Axle, we use a 500 mm support only to support the center of the front 
axle. 
             
 
After installing the Rear Axle, we use 700 mm Chassis Stands. To support the Axle at a height 
compatible with the installation of the rear suspensions. Thus, avoiding unnecessary load movements 
and the use of support equipment reduces costs and increases the safety of the activity.

Instructions for positioning the Chassis Stands can be found in the assembly instruction: Assembly 
Procedure for the Off-Highway Truck - 794AC (M0090261) 
 
 
          
 
          
 
Our strategy was to work installing as many parts and components as possible with the truck 
supported by four 900 mm Chassis Stands height and load capacity of 80 Metric Tons. 
 
After installing the superstructure, we leave the equipment ready for the installation of the front 
and rear wheels. With the support of two cranes, we lift the equipment and position it on the  
1600 mm Chassis Stands, thus providing the standard height for the tire handler to install the 
equipment wheels and tires.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
After defining the strategy for the assembly process, we defined the technical specifications and 
developed the supplier to manufacture the Chassis Stands Kits.  
 
The design with the isometric view was validated by our Mining Engineering and the calculation 
memorial was performed by the supplier's engineering.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Standardization of Chassis Stands for assembly of 794AC trucks. 
 
• These Supports are manufactured according to technical specifications, with approved 
structural calculation. 
 
• Chassis Stands with rubber top base to prevent metal-to-metal contact. 
 
• Kits are available in the assembly area before equipment arrival.
