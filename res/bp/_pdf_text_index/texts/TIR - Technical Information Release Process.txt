1.0 Introduction  
 
Technical Information Release (TIR) is a Caterpillar Dealer produced document used to 
communicate product problems, interim resolutions, dealer repair/setup procedures with 
appropriate groups within the dealership. TIR’s are an effective and efficient path to inform 
multiple areas of the dealer, customers, and 3rd parties (where applicable). This best practice will 
outline and explain TIR structure, data collection and distribution. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This Best Practice provides guidelines on how a TIR should be structured to effectively 
communicate information, display illustrations, capture data, document accessibility and 
distribution among the Dealership. 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
TIR Document Structure:  
 
• TIR Header should include Dealer name and confidential level  
• Media number required to track and identify each TIR – the use of the publication date 
may be an option (example: TIR2021-11-15) 
• Publication Date – Date of TIR Publication 
• Date Updated – Date of latest revision 
• Distribution shows the confidential level of the TIR such as “Dealer only”, “Customers”, 
“3
rd Party” 
• Priority shows severity of the TIR such as “Information Only”, “Take Necessary 
Precautions”, “Immediate Action”

TIR Introduction: 
 
This section is intended to provide background information on the technical information 
release. Details as to why this is required and how it will be accomplished.  
 
TIR Problem: 
 
This section provides details on the problem outlined by the TIR. Clear and descriptive 
information is required to communicate historic, current and potential issues this 
problem may cause. If possible, include (pictures, diagrams, drawings etc) to ensure the 
problem is fully understood by the intended audience. 
 
TIR Solution.  
 
Parts/Tools Required:

This will ensure parts and tooling can be ordered ahead of time and planned 
accordingly before the work gets done. It will also be used for future reference when the 
particular TIR requires repairs or maintenance related tasks.

TIR Illustrations: 
 
The use of relevant illustrations helps communicate information efficiently. Ensure to use arrows and 
descriptions to point out the correct part of the illustration to avoid misinterpretation. 
• Refer to the operation manual for the device that you are using for advice on how to achieve 
quality photos and videos. 
• Allow for ample lighting to reduce the number of photos that will be discarded due to 
blurriness. 
o Try to use natural light whenever possible. 
o Good lighting will also reduce or prevent shadows from developing on the components 
you are photographing. 
 
• Capturing Photo, Video, Or Audio Evidence of Machine Issues  
 
• Review the photos before forwarding them off to ensure that the photos show the problem 
clearly.

TIR Distribution / Communication: 
 
Technical Information Release has a wide range audience, including service, technical and 
depending on the issue, parts department. It is recommended that a TIR email distribution list is 
created to ensure consistent communication. Other platforms such as MS Teams, SharePoint and 
other shared sites are recommended for extended end-user reach. 
 
Draft version of the TIR is sent to the Technical Team for peer review, discuss any background 
information, DPPM and CPI Status. Review any special procedures or tooling required to complete 
the work. 
 
All current TIR’s are reviewed by the Technical Team on a quarterly basis to ensure the latest 
information is communicated and outlines the best solution currently available.

Communication Path (Examples) 
 
• Email - Send email to appropriate contacts 
• MS Teams – Create a Library of TIR’s within a dealer MS Teams Site 
• SharePoint – Create a Library of TIR’s on a dealer SharePoint Site. 
• Technical Meeting – Share and discuss newly published TIR’s in customer technical 
meetings. 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Ensures crucial information is communicated effectively and efficiently 
• Easy and quick to publish 
• Data is returned consistently to the technical team 
• Early warning process for potential emerging issues
