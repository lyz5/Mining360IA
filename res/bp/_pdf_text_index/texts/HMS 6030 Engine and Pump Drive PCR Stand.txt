1.0 Introduction 
P
lanned component replacement is an event which occurs several times over the life of a mining 
machine such as the 6030 or similarly sized mining shovels. These machines seldom enter a 
workshop environment; hence the work is very demanding on those executing the shut-down. There 
are multiple sources of contamination and various inefficiencies associated with this type of work.  
2
.0 Best Practice Description 
S
upporting a fleet of 10 x 6030 with similar hours on a MARC, our dealership looked at options to 
take some of the work from the field into the workshop and reduce the time required for the planned 
replacement of engine, pump drive and pumps. Our maintenance strategy has these components, as 
well as some hoses around them, listed for replacement at roughly the same time.  
W
e designed special stands which will allow us to join engine and pump drive (with coupling) in the 
workshop and then add the remaining power train / pumps onto the PTO. Through spare harnesses 
and new hoses, we can complete most of the power pack in the workshop and ship it to site as one 
unit, before the machine has even been parked up.  
T
he removed power pack can then be shipped back to the workshop / branch and dismantled for 
overhaul in the workshop. 
3
.0 Implementation Steps 
K
ey measurements of engine and pump drive were taken and a stand for each side power unit 
designed. This stand can also be used to ship just the engine on it’s own, should this be required. 
A
 few weeks before the machine is due to shut down, the spare engines and pump drives are 
installed to the stands. The team then proceed to install new/reman pumps into the position where 
pumps are due for replacement. In some instances, a field swap may still be necessary, e.g., if pump 
#3 has not yet achieved life due to an earlier replacement, the workshop power unit will not receive a 
new pump in this place.

4.0 Benefits 
• T
ime savings – less downtime for machine, ~24 hours per engine and pump combination
• Critical processes done in workshop.
• Better contamination control
• Shop labor is cheaper.
• Timing – build up of the power unit can be done in times of lower priority activities.
• Less waste – 1-time packaging only
5
.0 Resources Required 
• S
pare components and harnesses for the exchange
• Hose supply / hose shop
• Stands – cost around USD $2,500, per stand.
• Overhead crane
• Suitable shop transport / large forklift
• Standard hand tools
