1.0 Introduction  
 
The service team of one of the Borusan Makina Kazakhstan’s projects has made a tool to safely, 
easily and quickly replace transmission and hydraulic filters of 777D/F trucks in the site during big 
PMs. 
 
Previously, different on-hand things were used like ladders, barrels to reach the filters, which was 
not safe. Two technicians were needed to do this task. Besides, during the removal of the filter 
the used oil from the filter might spill out onto the technicians and the floor of the pit stop that 
violated the rules of Contamination Control (CC) and safety. The filter replacement process with 
the old way required more time and more effort. 
 
This tool helps the service team replace transmission and hydraulic filters quickly, safely and by 
using only the efforts of one technician. Moreover, this tool can be modified for other models of 
trucks if it is required.  
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This tool consists of three main parts: 1. Baseplate with the two types of filter base adapters on it 
– it is needed for keeping removed filters fixed and helps to disassemble filters; 2. Used oil 
collection tank; 3. Ladder itself that makes the tool mobile, which is important for field service 
people.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
The tool is in use.  
 
1. Place the tool under the truck. 
2. Step up the ladder and remove the filter. 
3. Put the removed filter onto the tool. 
4. Take out the tool under the truck. 
5. Using the filter base adapter, disassemble the filter. 
6. Then by using the reverse order, install a new filter to the truck.
