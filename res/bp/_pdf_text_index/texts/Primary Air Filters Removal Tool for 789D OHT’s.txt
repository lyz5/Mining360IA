1.0 Introduction  
 
Reusing air filters is a good practice recommended by CAT to preserve the environment and 
save costs. CAT establishes the reuse and cleaning of primary air filters up to two times; this 
represents a cost savings compared to air filters that were used once and discarded due to the 
extraction process. 
 
In this operation, some complications have occurred when removing the primary air filters on the 
789D as they get stuck inside the pre- cleaner when the machines are in PM (Preventive 
Maintenance). In some situations, the filters become so stuck that the technicians have to drill 
into the filters with a pry bar and attempt to pull them out. Once the filter is punctured, it can no 
longer be reused, and costs will increase due to the need to acquire more new filters to replace 
the discarded ones. 
 
 
  
  
 
This Best Practice is intended to make a tool to easily remove the air filters from the 789D without 
damaging them. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This Best Practice intends to use a removal tool to  easily remove the primary air filters during 
PM in the 789D. The tool must be attached to the filter to be removed and supported to the pre-
cleaner. Once the tool is attached to the filter, the technician must turn a handle a few times and 
the filter will come out of the pre- cleaner. It is not necessary to remove the entire filter from the 
pre-cleaner, removing the filter one inch will be enough. 
 
The tool must  be removed in the opposite way of installation. The filter can now be easily 
removed by hand from the pre-cleaner.

The tool is manufactured with a bracket with 4 supports and a worm screw welded to a rotating 
handle. The tool also incorporates a round plate, a hard washer, a nut, and a lock nut. There is 
a rubber band supported to the round plate with a clamp. When removing the air filter, tighten 
another clamp on the opposite side of the rubber band to tighten the filter. 
 
  
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
A. Remove the pre-cleaner cover. 
 
    
 
 
B. Attach the plate with the rubber band to the filter and tighten the clamp to it.

C. Attach the bracket to the plate by inserting the tool pin into the hole in the plate. 
 
  
 
D. Attach the bolt with a hard washer, locknut, and nut to the plate. Tighten the nut with the lock 
nut to prevent them from turning freely relative to the bolt when removing the filter. 
 
   
 
E. Turn the tool handle counterclockwise and you will begin to see the filter come out of the pre-
cleaner. Continue turning until the filter comes out approximately one inch.

F. Remove the tool in the reverse order of installation. 
 
G. Finish removing the filter from the pre-cleaner by hand. 
 
H. Perform the same procedure for the air filter on the opposite side. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
This tool will facilitate the removal of the primary air filters on the 789D. These filters are prone to 
get stuck inside the pre- cleaner when trying to remove them in the PM. In some cases, the filter 
must be punctured with a pry bar to remove it and can no longer be reused. The tool facilitates the 
removal of the filters and maintains their functionality. The filter can be reused two more times after 
the first use.
