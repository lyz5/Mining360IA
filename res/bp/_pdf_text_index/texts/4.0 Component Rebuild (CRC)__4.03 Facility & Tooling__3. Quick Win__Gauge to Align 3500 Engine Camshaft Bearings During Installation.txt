1.0 Introduction  
 
Camshaft bearings must be installed with the oil holes in the bearing aligned exactly with the oil 
passages in the cylinder in order to assure proper lubrication to the camshaft.   
 
The install procedure states to position the oil hole on the bearing 20 degrees (plus or minus 5 
degrees) off the horizontal centerline.   
 
1. Install camshaft bearing (1) on tooling (A). 
 
 
 
2. Position camshaft bearing (1) and Tooling (A) in the bore.  
Note: The joint in camshaft bearing (1) must be on the horizontal centerline of the bore. The 
upper oil hole must be 20 ± 5 degrees above the horizontal centerline of the bore. The lower oil 
hole of camshaft bearing (1) should be 20 ± 5 degrees below the horizontal centerline of the 
bore. The joints of camshaft bearing (1) on the right side of the engine must face the inside of 
the cylinder block. The joints of camshaft bearing (1) on the left side of the engine must face 
the outside of the cylinder block. 
This Best Practice provides a very simple gauge to assure proper alignment.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This is a very simple angle gauge to mark a position on the block to use to align the camshaft bearing oil 
hole. 
 
Bend a steel plate at 90°.   Make a notch of 3mm wide at a distance of 92.00 ± 0.06mm (3.620 ± 0.002inch)  
(A).     The notch is used to locate the proper position on the block for alignment of the bearing  
       oil hole.   The block is marked with a fine point marking pencil. 
                
 
                   
                                                                          
            
                                         
  
 
 
Place the gauge on the edge of the block as shown and mark the bearing alignment location (notch) with a 
marking pencil
