1.0 Introduction  
 
The back lighting intensity of the dashboard signal lamps does not have another level. Signaling 
LEDs have a strong light for nighttime operation, and some operators complain of eye discomfort 
due to the brightness. As a result, they paint or place stickers on the panel to reduce the light. The 
addition of a potentiometer will allow the operator to manually control the LED lighting intensity. 
 
 
 
Illustration 1: Signal lamps cap painted by operators. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
By installing the potentiometer in the LED' s electrical circuit, it was possible to adjust the lamp’ s 
intensity. We installed the improvement in a truck and monitored the results and the operator ’s 
opinion. It has improved the comfort of operation at nighttim e and as result of reducing the  
brightness there have been no more complaints from operators.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
We elaborated the step-by -step to implement the improvement. The first step was searching for 
parts compatible with the equipment's electrical system and specifications. The parts defined were 
potentiometer #610-2112 and knob #432-2913. Both parts already are used in the equipment 
system. 
                         
 
Illustration 3: Parts  
 
We design the electrical schematic to support our technicians in this job . We installed the 
potentiometer in series with the negative wire 200-Y1 BK-20 cable. We used the pin A and B. 
.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Reduce ergonomic discomfort for the operator's eyes during the night. 
• Quick regulation of the intensity levels of the LED’s  
• Avoid damage to the panel with paints and adhesives to reduce brightness. 
• Low cost
