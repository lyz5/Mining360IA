1.0 Introduction  
 
Safety, as well as the company’s image, play a very important role when in comes to the 
relationship with customers.  As a result , the parts del ivery and reception is a critical element in 
the customer experience. E ffective and safe transport makes the merchandise arrive to its 
destination in excellent packaging condition; which supports a costs reduction and shorter 
delivery times .  When it comes to a repeating  order to fulfill a need regar ding components 
exchanging of damaged parts, the parts return process  time is decreased due to mishandling o f 
parts when they are delivered, keeping the merchandise’s integrity. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
It is po ssible to deliver and receive merchandise in excellent condition by making adjustments in 
the transport used.  This can be achieved by adapting the initial structure of the truck and using  
attachments to improve the loading  and unloading process.   
 
The following advantages are obtained by adapting the truck: 
 
 Side Curtains:  Provides weather protection for the parts in the truck’s compartments. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Latches:  Keep the load safe and allows the merchandise to be locked.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
 S tructures were designed and made ( inside shelves) that allow each section to be set up 
differently 
 
 L ights and folding doors were installed, allowing a larger opening and increasing access, 
visibility and ventilation when it comes to the handling of the merchandise 
 
 Latches were installed onto the folding doors in order to protect and keep the load secure 
 
 R ings were installed on the panels in order to secure single loads safe  
 
 F astening elements are available (slings) in order to keep loads secure

 Storage Efficiency:  I ncrease on the storage density  due to the modular shelves inside of 
the truck.  The load distribution is carried out in an organized way. 
 
 Faster Delivery:  It gives the transport and delivery process flexibility because it makes the 
planning, distribution and sorting out of the load easier regarding the delivery route. 
 
 Costs R eduction: The load arrives to its destination safe, therefore; it’s not necessary to 
replace parts that are damaged while transporting. 
 
 Loading Time Reduction: Due to the flexible folding door  on two or more vertical hinges 
that allows one door to be folded one against another, there is more space to place the 
load. 
 
 Increased Load Capacity:  Increasing the load capacity provides a reduction on the 
number of trips necessary to fulfill the customer’s needs, and at the same time it results in 
increased profitability.
