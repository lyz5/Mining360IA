1.0 Introduction 
W
ith the increased use of our infrastructure  equipment, including the D11 and D11T track  tractor, 
which operate in the S11D mine, there was a need to improve productivity in the activity of changing 
tracks, mitigating risks of accidents, and reducing the exposure of people during the replacement 
and interaction with the equipment. 
In addition, this tool makes the process of maintaining and replacing the conveyor more practical, 
as it eliminates the use of chains or cables using the component itself used for the replacement of 
the new component. 
2
.0 Best Practice Description 
T
he tool was developed aiming at greater safety of those involved in the activity , since the 
technicians are no longer exposed to involuntary movements of the tractor, the movement of lifting 
equipment and projection of materials. 
Another important gain is in productivity, with this tool we can change the track  with a time gain of 
approximately 42%, due to greater agility in removal.

2º Step: The tool (10) is designed to make the conveyor to be replaced slide onto the new conveyor,

4.0 Benefits 
• M
itigation between human-machine interaction.
• There is no need to use a forklift and/or crane to reposition the conveyor.
• No exposure of people in the line of fire.
• With the elimination of cables and/or chains to remove the conveyor, the risk of material
projection and whipping was eliminated.
• Agility in the replacement of the conveyors.
• With the improvement to remove the conveyor, we gain in time, because the movement of the
conveyor with auxiliary feature is no longer necessary. With this the removal becomes a
continuous and agile activity.
• Increased productivity.
• Before the tool, we changed a track with approximately 6 hours, meeting all safety requirements.
With the new tool, this same activity can be performed in 3.5 hours and with a higher safety
factor.
5
.0 Resources Required

or the manufacturing of the tool, a project was developed in collaboration with the engineering team, 
and materials found in the client's area were used. We also utilized internal welders for the fabrication

materials used and the allocated labor, we estimate around $1,550.00 for manufacturing.
