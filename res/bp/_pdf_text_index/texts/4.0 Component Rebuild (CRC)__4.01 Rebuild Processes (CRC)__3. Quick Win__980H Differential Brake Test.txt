1.0 Intr
oduction 
In t
he past, no inspection procedures were being conducted during a 980H brake test without 
installing the differential first. Periodically, components were delivered to customers that hadn’t 
been tested in the workshop prior, which resulted in issues down the line. This can cost a lot of 
money and tarnish the customer relationship. For example, in one instance, an O-ring was 
damaged in a differential component, we didn’t have test procedure at that time and without 
realizing, the component was delivered to a customer. After that, our customer couldn’t run the 
brake and sent it back and our technicians had to spend eight hours fixing it, which cost around 
$500. In addition to this financial cost, our reputation with that customer was tarnished. To avoid 
situations such as this, a test procedure is being rolled out in 980H brake thanks to one of our 
engineers. 
2.0 Best
 Practice Description 
This test procedure doesn’t require the brake to be installed in the equipment. It provides an  
opportunity to test brake in the workshop area. The test is very easy to perform. Hydraulic  oil is 
slowly sent through the brake with the help of a hand-operated pump. 
At the same time, the pressure is checked via a pressure gauge and hydraulic oil is pumped 
until the pressure gauge indicates 450-500 PSI. The pressure gauge is observed for 
some time (approximately 1 minute), the brake test is passed if the pressure stays stable  
around 450-500 PSI. The test has failed if the gauge shows a dramatic decrease (1% change)  
from 450-500 PSI (4.5 – 5 PSI change). If the test fails, then possible problems can be   
investigated and fixed before returning the component to the customer. A detailed procedure is   
included in the sections with the addition of a video of the test in the attachments.

4.0 B
enefits 
1) It can save potential redo costs including cost of parts and labor.
2) It can save freight costs associating with rework from customer.
3) It increases quality of revision.
4) It can protect our reputation with customer by having less redo work.
5) It will save time by not having to install transmission to test it.
