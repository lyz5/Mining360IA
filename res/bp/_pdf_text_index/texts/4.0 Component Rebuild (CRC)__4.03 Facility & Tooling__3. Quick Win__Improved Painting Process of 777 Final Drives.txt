1.0 Introduction  
 
The preparation of one final drive for painting takes 45 minutes, and one roll of the packing tape 
is used for it. Borusan Makina Kazakhstan has fabricated multi-use covers to protect the places 
on the final drive that should not be painted.  
 
For example, 40 covers were made for 2 x 777D/F Final Drives. The use of those covers has 
considerably reduced the component paint preparation time (from 45 minutes to 1 minute per 1 x 
Final Drive).   
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The multi-use cover is made from the pipe welded to the tin-plate pattern. The pipe is selected as 
per the diameter of the final drive bolt (see attached drawings for 777D/F pipe and plate 
specifications).

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
 
• Obtain necessary material and fabricate the covers 
• Install covers and your final drive is ready to be painted!  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Improved painting process with the use of the multi-use covers. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• The use of multi-use covers considerably saves time when the component is prepared for 
painting (from 45 minutes to 1 minute per 1 x Final Drive), and that expedites the painting 
process accordingly. 
 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• Machine shop (lathe & milling machine). 
• Time and material to fabricate
