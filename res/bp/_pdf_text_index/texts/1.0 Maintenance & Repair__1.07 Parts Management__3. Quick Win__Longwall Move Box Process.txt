1.0 Introduction 
Description: 
Providing prepackaged boxes with critical spares for Longwall moves greatly enhances customer 
satisfaction by preventing delays during the move period. Equipment can quickly be put back into 
service reducing the downtime and subsequent cost to the customer. 
      Scope:  
      This Best Practice will describe a process that will enhance a dealer support strategy to be 
implemented at any Underground Longwall operation. 
      History: 
      The move box process pre-dates the Caterpillar Inc. acquisition of the EMP products. In the 
underground mining business, the delays brought about by having to travel a long way to get the 
necessary parts to keep production equipment running can be more challenging than surface 
mining. This process has been in existence to help mitigate those challenges for the last 15 years. 
      Impact: 
      Having immediate access to all critical spares identified previously allows maintenance personnel 
to quickly react to situations that could have jeopardized the Longwall move schedule and 
subsequent production loss due to missed start up dates. It is an opportunity for a dealer to work 
closely with their customer and improves the overall customer/dealer relationship. 
      Resource Requirements: 
      This process requires minimal capital expense to initiate and in essence is an extension of the 
warehouse management that already exists at all dealerships. 
 
2.0 Implementation Steps 
      Target Customer: 
       Once a target customer has been identified, research should be done or at least confirmed on 
the specification of the equipment that customer is operating. 
       Critical Parts Listing: 
       Service personnel should then create the lists of parts based on their experience and product 
knowledge. These lists should cover all utility vehicles and shield haulers as well as parts likely to 
be damaged on the Longwall equipment being relocated. 
       Training: 
       Employees who are involved in this process need to be trained on the process itself and aware of    
their role and responsibilities in order to ensure a successful implementation. 
       Equipment: 
       Once it has been determined how many boxes may be required, a purchase order request needs 
to be submitted, approved and then the purchase made. The boxes used by CB Mining are 
typically around $700 US. (see addendum 1)

3.0 Best Practice Description 
Pre-delivery steps: 
Check warehouse stock of the previously created critical parts lists. Create a quote from those 
CPL’s. A dealer representative should then set up a meeting with the target customer and present 
the program and the CPL proposals. Any requirements from the customer over and above should 
be added to the CPL’s and re-submitted to the customer. Establish the protocol on additional 
larger items such as electric motors, axles and winches as to whether the dealer keeps them 
locally or in the mine warehouse. 
This should be done 4-6 weeks before any Longwall move is scheduled as they have to approve 
the parts quote and allow time for the logistics. 
Delivery: 
Once a PO has been received the production department sets up a work order to which the 
warehouse can then issue the quoted parts. An inventory contents list needs to be created for 
each move box being supplied and affixed externally and a copy enclosed within the box itself. 
The boxes are then locked and delivered to the mine. Upon receipt at the mine warehouse a 
dealer representative should schedule a visit and handover the keys to the boxes once the 
contents have been inventoried and the contents approved by the customer. The boxes can then 
be transported underground to a convenient location for the move. 
Post-delivery: 
When the Longwall move has been completed the move boxes are transported back out to the 
mine warehouse. The contents are inventoried and the contents approved by both a dealer 
representative and the customer before being returned to the dealer warehouse. The keys are 
handed back to the dealer representative at this time and the boxes shipped back to the 
dealership. The results of this exit inventory determines what the customer will be invoiced 
Qualifiers: 
If no preference is given by the customer with regard to new or exchange components then the 
dealer retains that discretionary right of supply. 
1. When a new part is supplied no used core will be accepted in return. 
2. Any exchange part core not returned will be invoiced at the new retail price. 
3. Move boxes will be provided for a period not to exceed 4 weeks. If this time period is 
exceeded the box contents will be invoiced for the full value of the quote. 
4. All the parts will be quoted at the normal customer list price including applicable discount. 
5. The customer PO has to be a minimum deposit of $1,500.00 US per box. The deposit is 
required as sometimes the customer will damage the box (see addendum 2).  The deposit 
amount can vary depending on the costs by the dealer to produce the box. 
6. Any maintenance or replacement of boxes will be deducted from the deposit 
7. No parts can be returned after the exit inventory and boxes have been returned to the 
dealership

Addendum 2 
 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
The intent of the program is to immediately make available parts which may be required during a 
Longwall move, both on the Longwall itself, or the equipment being utilized to facilitate the move. 
This reduces the downtime and helps the customer achieve his production startup target date. 
This availability of parts increases opportunity of the dealer capturing the part sale with an OEM 
part. 
The implementation of this program enhances both the reputation of the dealer and the customer 
satisfaction and loyalty
