1.0 Introduction  
 
Borusan Makina Kazakhstan (BMK) has designed a fixture that prevents the bolts from spinning 
in R1300G final drives during their multiple tightening. 
 
Previously the BMK mining project teams constantly faced the problem of the bolts spinning in 
R1300G final drives during tightening. To remedy this, they welded those bolts to the surface of 
the final drive. When this final drive came to the CRC for overhaul, the CRC technicians had to 
clean the surface of the final drive from welding. Thus, the spinning bolts have become the 
common challenge both for the mining and for the CRC people. 
 
That’s why the CRC has created the fixture that allows everybody to forget about this concern. 
The application of this fixture enables the mining technicians to easily tighten those bolts during 
the operation and has saved the CRC people from cleaning the surface of the final drive from 
welding during the overhaul. 
 
At time of publishing, Borusan Makina Kazakhstan has reworked 16 hubs and have not 
experienced any failures in the fields for nearly a year.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This fixture consists of 2 x Locking semi-rings. These semi-rings have the holes for the bolt heads 
that lock the bolts. They are bolted to the final drive surface with smaller bolts (10 pcs).  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Semi-rings with locking holes for R1300G Final Drive Bolts (P/N 308-9259).

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
R1300G Final Drive Bolt Spinning Prevention Fixture is in use . 
 
1) Bolt 2 x Locking Semi-rings to the surface of th e final drive.  
2) Install CAT Bolts into to the final drive. 
Note: Loctite 640 is applied to the smaller bolts.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
R1300G Final Drive Bolt Spinning Prevention Fixture is in use 
 
  
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Prevention of the final drive bolts from spinning during their tightening. 
• There is no need to clean the surface of the final  drive from welding during the overhaul in 
the CRC.
