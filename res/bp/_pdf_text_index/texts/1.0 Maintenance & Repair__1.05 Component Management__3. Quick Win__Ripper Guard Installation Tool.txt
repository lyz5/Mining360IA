1.0 Introduction  
 
This Best Practice provides instruction for an easier way to replace ripper shank guards through 
the use of a purpose built lifting jig.  
 
Changing a ripper shank protector is currently a difficult task.  The single shank protector (9N-
4621) on the D11T weighs 74kg and requires multiple technicians to maneuver the heavy guard 
onto the ripper tooth before it is positioned in order to install the retaining pins.   
 
This maneuvering is often performed in an awkward position due to the ripper carriage overhead.  
This can result in unsafe lifting practices and potential injuries. 
 
Use of this tool reduces the need for multiple technicians required to complete this task while 
removing many of the risks associated with ripper protection replacement methods. 
  
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
 
Tool Design: 
Tool is required to be installed in the lower ripper pin mounts.   
 
Key dimensions for D11R/D11T single shank ripper assembly: 
 
 Distance between outer pin faces (assembled) = 847mm 
 Hole depth (each pin) = 63mm 
 Hole diameter = 36mm

Shank Protector Ratchet Assembly: 
 
Key Design Requirements: 
 
1. Side mounted belt tensioner 
2. Twin carabineers  
3. Hooked ratchet strap ends 
 
 
 
Ripper Protector Replacement Procedure: 
 
Step 1. Ripper set-up 
Ripper pin puller to be used to extend out ripper shank to allow approximately 1.5 meters of shank 
to be extended below the ripper frame. 
 
Step 2. Isolate Machine 
Make sure that the machine is parked on a flat level surface, all implements are lowered, engine 
has been stopped and engine has been isolated (and tagged as appropriate). 
 
Step 3. Prepare Lifting Frame 
The spring loaded locking pin must be in the unlocked position and the Swivel pin opened out. 
The manual latch on the other end should also be in the unlatched position.

Step 4. Fit Swivel end of Lifting Frame to Ripper bed 
Insert the swivel end pin into the the ripper tilt pin. Lift up the lifting frame from opposite side 
(manual latch side) until the locking pin has clicked into the locked postion. A distinct “click” should 
be heard when the pin is locked. Be sure that the swivel pin stays in position while lifting from the 
other end.

Step 5. Fit manual pin end of Lifting Frame to Ripper bed 
 
Once the swivel pin end is locked into position, push the manual gun barrel style pin into postion 
and lock it into place. The lifting frame should now be secure, test it by moving the frame side to 
side and back and forth to ensure it is safe. 
 
   
 
 
  
  
  
 
Step 6. Attach ratchet chain block to lifting frame 
Place the ratchet chain block onto the rail of the lifting 
frame. Make sure hook is secure and cannot fall off the 
rail or come lose. 
 
Step 7. Ripper guard set-up 
The ripper shin guard should be placed so the ripper 
side is facing down. This allows the ratchet strap to be 
attached in the correct position.

Step 8. Fit ratchet strap to ripper guard 
Position the ratchet strap, so when tightened, the lifting point will be just below the top of the 
shank. The correct location of the ratchet strap will make the guard positioning easier. 
 
 
 
Step 9. Lift ripper guard. 
Attach the chain and block hook onto the lifting rings of the ratchet strap. Ensure that it is attached 
securely and cannot fall. Lift up the guard and slide along the lifting frame rail until the guard is 
positioned above the ripper shank.

Step 10. Lower ripper guard 
Carefully lower the ripper guard onto the shank. Ensure that the retainers are in the correct 
position. When the shin guard is aligned, the retaining pins can be knocked into place using a soft 
face hammer. 
 
 
 
Step 11. Remove ratchet strap and chain. 
After the ripper shin guard is secure, release the tension from the chain block and un-hook the 
ratchet strap. Remove the chain block from the lifting frame rail. 
 
Step 12. Unlock Swivel  
Pull back the locking pin on the swivel end of the lifting frame. The pin should be moved around to 
the unlocked position. 
 
Step 13. Remove lifting frame 
Holding the frame from the manual pin end, slowly pull the frame away from the machine so the 
manual pin is clear of the machine. Once the manual pin is free, lower the bar slightly and push 
the bar so the swivel end releases from the machine. 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
1. Design and fabricate the fixture  
Ensure fixture is certified to local standards as required. 
 
2. Document the removal and installation procedure  
Provide easy to follow instructions, use clear pictures and detailed instructions. Safety 
warnings and helpful tips should also be included.
