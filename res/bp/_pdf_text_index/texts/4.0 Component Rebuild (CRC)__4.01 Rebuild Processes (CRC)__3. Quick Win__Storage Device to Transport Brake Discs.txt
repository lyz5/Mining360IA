1.0 Introduction  
 
This Best Practice is a fabricated adjustable basket for the quick, safe, and easy storage and 
transport of brake discs. Previously, a disc storage “tree” style rack was used.  The “tree” rack 
was typically top heavy and difficult to move from a staging area to the work bay when needed. In 
general, parts in process are staged in a staging area instead of using work bay space for 
staging. Parts are delivered “just in time” as required.  This new device reduces the risk of 
damage to the discs due to tip over of the “tree” design racks.
