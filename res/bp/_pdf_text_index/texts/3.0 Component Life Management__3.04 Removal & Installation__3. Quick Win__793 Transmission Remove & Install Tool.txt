1.0 Introduction  
The ideal method to removal or install a transmission in an Off Highway Truck is to remove the 
truck body.  This method provides clear access for an overhead crane to lift the transmission 
during the removal and installation process.   
 
It is not always practical to remove the truck body, however, with the body on the truck, the body 
interferes with the lifting cable and it is not possible to lift and position the transmission properly 
for installation. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
                                  Truck Body Interferes with the Hoist Cables 
 
In addition, transmissions should only be lifted using the lifting eye locations on the center of the 
transmission housing.  Using other locations such as the rear of the housing can cause cracks in 
the housing.

Trakindo has developed a fixture comprised of a rail and a lifting cage to perform removal 
installation without removing the body.  The rail is positioned on the truck frame.  The cage, 
equipped with three hand hoists that hold the transmission, is placed on the rail.  The cage then 
can be moved to the proper position and the transmission is raised or lowered using the cage 
hoists. 
 
This Best Practice is a Quick Win project. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
This fixture is a lifting devi ce and needs to be designed and built to conform to local regulations 
and requirements. 
 
Dimensions will depend on truck model, and may not work with “F” series trucks. 
 
Tool Concept 
 
Rail:  To be placed on the truck frame. 
 
Cage:  Equipped with three one ton or higher hoists.  The cage is lowered on to the rail with an 
overhead crane, and then moved along the rail using a ratchet puller to the transmission 
compartment location.  Hoists are used to raise or lower the transmission.

o 25 tons (or more) over head crane 
o Three hand hoists (at least 1 ton each) to lift the transmission. (1U-9207) 
o 8S-9906 ratchet puller to pull the transmission group
