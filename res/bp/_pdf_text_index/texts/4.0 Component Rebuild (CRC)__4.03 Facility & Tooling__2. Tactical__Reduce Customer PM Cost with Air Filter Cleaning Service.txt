1.0 Introduction  
 
Fleets of Mining Off Highway Trucks working 24 hours a day, 365 day a year, consume up to 70 
air filters per machine per year.  Air Filters thus represent a significant preventative maintenance 
cost.  Some dealers have invested in high technology air filter cleaning equipment and offer a 
filter cleaning service to their customers.   There are many suppliers for air filters and the 
business is very price competitive.  A filter cleaning service creates an advantage that most 
competitive filter suppliers do not offer and can play a major role in capturing air filter sales 
business. 
 
Cleaning and re-using air filters up to 10 times is especially effective in controlling costs of 
Maintenance and Repair Contracts, Customer Support Agreements, and Rental fleets and helps 
to lower customer PM costs. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Dealers who provide a filter cleaning service usually do so with a “filter exchange” program. 
Customer returns a dirty filter with no visible damage and receives a cleaned filter. 
There is a flat rate charge approximately equal to 25% of the cost of a new filter.  I 
 
Data from dealers who provide filter cleaning service report that air filter can be effectively 
cleaned between 3 and 10 times before they can no longer be reused.  Life of the cleaned filters 
is close to 90% of the life of a new filter and air filtration is equal to that of a new filter. 
 
Caterpillar no longer provides air filter cleaning equipment.  The preferred equipment for air filter 
cleaning is the Sonic Dry Clean Air Filter Cleaning System.  The system is a sonic vibration and 
synchronized air injection cleaning booth with dust collection system and inspection lighting.  
Other required equipment includes a filter dryer, air compressor, filter track, racks, and a dust 
collection and disposal.
