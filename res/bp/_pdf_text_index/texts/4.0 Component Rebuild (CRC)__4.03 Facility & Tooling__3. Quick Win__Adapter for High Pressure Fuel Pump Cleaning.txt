1.0 Introduction 
  
This improvement describes how to perform the cleaning process in the lubrication line for high 
pressure fuel pumps, during the process of C175 engine partial overhaul, without failure by 
utilizing an adapter tool that Ferreyros designed. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The improvement consists of using an adapter on the high-pressure fuel pump of C175 engines 
and connecting it to the upper oil inlet line, using the oil-supplied injection pump test stand to 
perform the cleaning and remove the remaining used oil. 
 
The following steps are required to use this tool. Refer to Section 6.0 for images. 
  
1. Install the high-pressure pump lifting bracket (P/N 487-8643).  
2. Install the high-pressure pump on the injection pump test stand. 
3. Install the adapter on the top oil line of the high-pressure pump. 
4. Install the test stand oil supply hose onto the adapter. 
5. Turn on the test stand. 
6. Regulate the oil supply pressure between 45 to 50 PSI. 
7. Flush with clean oil for approximately 30 minutes. 
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Design the adapter to be used for connection to the lubrication line of the high-pressure fuel 
pump. The adapter is shown below and should be designed according to the blueprint attached in 
Section 6.0.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Prevents contamination in the process of repairing the engine by removing remaining oils that 
may contain contaminants inside the lubrication system of the high-pressure fuel pump; 
• Avoids related failures due to contamination.
