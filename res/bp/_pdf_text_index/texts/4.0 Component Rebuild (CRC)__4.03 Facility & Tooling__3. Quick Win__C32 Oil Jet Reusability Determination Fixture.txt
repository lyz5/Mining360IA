MINING DEALER BEST PRACTICE  
 
1.0 Introduction  
 
Borusan Makina Kazakhstan has made the fixture to easily check oil jets  and make the right 
decision if it reusable or it should be replaced.  
 
The wrong decision about the reusability of oil jets may lead to the incorrect angle of supplying 
the grease and cooling the piston head, excessive wear of the liner wall and piston skirt,  the 
piston head overheating and finally to the emergency shutdown of the engine.  That’s why it is 
very important to prevent the reuse of the damaged oil jets.  
 
During the visual inspection of the injectors it is almost impossible to determine the bent or 
damaged oil jets  with the naked eye. This fixture helps us to see if the oil jet  is OK or it is 
damaged.  
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This fixture is made of the polyester resin. A new injector has been used to mold the correct form 
of the jet.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
The fixture is in use.  
 
It is simple  to use this fixture. If the  oil jet  inspected does not match the pattern, it cannot be 
reused and should be replaced. If it matches the pattern, this oil jet can be reused.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Increased defect list quality. 
• Reduced defect list preparation time. 
• Prevention of the troubles that occur if the overlooked damaged oil jet has been reused. 
 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• Polyester resin and the new injector in order to make the pattern.
