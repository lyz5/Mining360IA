1.0 Introduction  
 
In the newly modified master track link, the torque application requires a high degree of accuracy 
and also the required degrees (90 ° +/- 10 °) are not easy to mark in the hexagonal bolt, generating 
an incorrect torque application to the track link. See Fig. 1. 
 
This Best Practice provides a quick and safe solution for the precise marking regarding rotation 
degrees when applying the final torque on the tractor master track link bolts. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The current degree marking is done through the technicians´ knowledge or expertise which generates 
errors that ultimately result in redoes for torque loss of or a failure in the bolts due to torque excess.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Logistics: 
 
• Data collection regarding the procedure of degrees marking. 
• M agnetic gauge kit for the degrees marking. 
• Creation of a standard operating index card for controlling the torque. 
• Disclose the Best Practice to the personnel. 
 
Operation: 
 
• Clean the area around the bolt. 
• Apply the pre-torque to the bolts according to the suggested sequence. 
• Install the gauge matching the initial reference point with one of the vertices of the hexagonal 
bolt head and mark this point. Also, mark the final point of rotation as indicated by the gauge 
(see Fig. 2). 
• Remove the gauge and implement further rotation in the route from the starting point to the 
end point marked. 
• Perform the procedure for all the bolts in the same order of the pre-torque application (see Fig. 
3).

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Reduction of 90% of redoes for torque loss of the master track link bolts. Under 65% of failures 
in less than 300 working days to 5.4% failure in the f leet of 37 machines.  (See report 
attached). 
 
• More accuracy regarding degrees marking. 
• Easy to use and it applies for any bolts that require further rotation 
• The lack of dependence on the skill and / or knowledge of the technician to determine grades. 
• Incr eased equipment availability.
