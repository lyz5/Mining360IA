1.0 Introduction  
 
Handling large wheel groups during rebuild can be a difficult task. A wheel group typically arrives 
to the CRC mounted on a shipping stand. It must then be rotated from the horizontal position to a 
vertical position before disassembly. After rebuild, it must be again rotated from the vertical 
position back to a horizontal position for mounting on a shipping stand. 
 
The process normally involves the use of two overhead cranes and multiple chains to perform the 
necessary rotations. It also requires several technicians to perform the operation.  
 
Industrial equipment commonly called “upenders” are manufactured by several suppliers and are 
well-suited to the task of efficiently and securely rotating heavy components from horizontal to 
vertical positions and back. 
 
 
            
  
 
 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Industrial “upenders” are manufactured by a number of suppliers, such as Bushman and Titan. 
They are available in sizes ranging up to 30+ ton capacity. They typically can move a wheel 
station from horizontal to vertical position in less than 30 seconds. 
Some dealers have fabricated their own wheel group upender tooling.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
It is best to work with a reputable supplier to identify proper requirements before installing an 
upender. You will need to know the maximum forecast load weight and floor space available in 
the wheel group rebuild area.  
 
Some upenders have power options, such as electric or hydraulic. It is important that an upender 
used for wheel group handling has both power up and power down. 
   
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Raising and lowering wheel groups takes 50%+ less time. 
• Much reduced risk of damaging the wheel group duri ng handling. 
• Requires only one technician to operate. 
• Frees up overhead cranes for other tasks. 
• Lesser risk of accidents.
