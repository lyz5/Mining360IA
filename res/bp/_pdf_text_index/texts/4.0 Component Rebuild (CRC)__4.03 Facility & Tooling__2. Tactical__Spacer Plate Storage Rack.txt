1.0 Introduction  
 
Borusan Makina Kazakhstan has developed a special rack for spacer plates of all CAT engines 
that holds spacer plates in a vertical position. 
 
Previously spacer plates were stored in horizontal position. During preparation for assembly, 
assembly technicians had to spend time on locating the needed spacer plate and handling other 
spacer plates that were above the required spacer plate.  
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
To eliminate the need for searching and handling spacer plates, the special rack was developed. 
This rack has baffles that hold long spacer plates and prevent from leaning on other plates. 
Number of baffles depends on the dealer engine turnover. There is also a bottom compartment 
for storage of smaller spacer plates of 3500 series engines.
