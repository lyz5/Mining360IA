1.0 Best Practice Description  
 
This Best Practice deals with reminding truck operators to select and engage the proper gear for 
descending a grade when using Automatic Retarder Control (ARC). For effective use of ARC, the 
truck must have the proper transmission gear engaged before descending the grade. To remind 
operators to make the gear change, mines have placed signs indicating the proper gear to be 
selected and engaged. The proper gear signs are placed near the beginning of the downhill 
grades. Each sign informs the operator which gear should be selected and engaged according to 
the machine’s Operation and Maintenance Manual (O&MM). 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
2.0 Implementation Steps 
 
• Establish the proper gear to be selected and engaged for each grade in the mine as per the 
O&MM. 
 
• Train operators 
 
• Place the correct gear indicator signs prior to each downhill grade.  
 
• Additional signs may be advisable along the grade to remind the operator of the correct gear.

3.0 Benefits 
 
Proper gear selection while descending grades allows for effective use of the Automatic Retarder 
Control. ARC is designed to reduce brake wear and brake system overheating. Many operators 
overlook the fact that the transmission must be engaged in a particular gear suitable for 
descending the grade. Without the proper lower gear, the ARC will not be effective. Engine 
overspeed, accelerated brake wear, and brake system overheating can result. 
 
4.0 Resources Required 
 
All that is required are signs appropriately placed before downhill grades that inform the operator 
of the proper gear to use while descending the grade. The sign may be a simple as a symbol of a 
gear with a number at the center. The number indicates the correct gear to be selected and 
engaged before descending the grade. Such a pictograph avoids any language problems. In 
situations where different truck models require different gears to be selected and engaged, the 
sign may indicate the particular truck model: 793, 797, etc.
