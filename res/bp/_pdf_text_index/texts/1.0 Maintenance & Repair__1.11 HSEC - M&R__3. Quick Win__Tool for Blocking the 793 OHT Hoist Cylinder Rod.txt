1.0 Introduction  
 
Hoist cylinders are essential  for proper functioning  of mining trucks . Due to the weight of these 
components lifting and handling are a high safety risk. When suspended the rod can come off the 
cylinder and cause an accident . Chains are used to retain the rod and  reduce the risk. During the 
manipulation of this component, the risk for an accident is still present.  
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
In search for safer working procedures, a tool has been designed in order to make the repairs easier 
and reduce risk . This consists of a clamping mechanism that  retains the rod and cylinder while 
working on the component. 
 
 
 
 
Figure 1 
The tool is designed to be installed prior to removing the cylinder.
