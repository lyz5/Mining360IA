1.0 Introduction  
 
The two pressure sensors (PN 534-4587) and the temperature sensor  (PN 534-4592) of HMS 
6060AC swing system are located on the rear swing motors closest to the walkway, as shown on 
the picture 1. Therefore, due to the  position of these sensors, they can be easily damaged by 
people walking on the walkway or at service / maintenance work in this area. 
 
 
Picture 1: Pressure sensors PN 534-4587 applied at rear swing motors, near the walkway. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
To avoid damages of these sensors, they should be  removed from the rear swing motors  to the 
front swing motors. Pictures 2 to 4 show details about this modification. In this area, the sensors are 
protected from almost all external damage, and this saves costs and downtime.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Note: Parts list in the "5.0 Resources Required" 
 
1. Stop and shutdown the machine. Lock the safety device preventing the machine from switching 
on, apply a warning sign. 
2. Remove the sensors installed on the rear swing motors and install  the plugs and seals (items 
#1 and #2 of parts list) at same location. 
3. At front swing motors, install the  manifolds (items #3 and #8 of parts list)  one on each motor 
and install all the flanges and adapters (items #4 to #7 of parts list). 
4. Install the pressure and temperature sensors at the manifolds installed on the front swing 
motors; 
5. Develop 3 electrical harness using the wire and conduit ( items #11 and #12 of parts list). The 
connectors of these harness are Deutsch type (items #9 and #10 of parts list). Each harness 
has 4 meters and a female connector in terminal and male connector in other; 
6. Connect these harnesses at original harness and at the sensors. 
 
The pictures 5 and 6 show details of all swing motors after installation. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
1. Maintenance: Eliminate failures by people walking near the sensors. 
2. Productivity: Contribute to improve the Mean Time Before Failure (MTBF) of the machine. 
3. Safety: Easy access. 
4. Commercial: All items involved have CAT part numbers.
