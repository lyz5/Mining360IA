DEALER BEST PRACTICE SERIES 
 
1.0 Introduction  
 
During the 2012 Mining Dealer CRC Conference held at Ferreyros in Lima Peru, there were many 
inquiries about the materials Ferreyros uses as protective covers on their workbenches.  This 
Best Practices is to provide the material specifications information. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Since 2009, Ferreyros has been using Shore A Polyurethane 90-92 for workbench table tops.  
The dealer uses a ¼” thickness.  The material is sourced from a local supplier. 
 
The dealer is very satisfied with the performance of the material.  Experience has been that the 
material is extremely durable, easy to clean, resists most fluids and chemicals, and can handle 
high temperature.  It also will not scratch or damage metal pads.
