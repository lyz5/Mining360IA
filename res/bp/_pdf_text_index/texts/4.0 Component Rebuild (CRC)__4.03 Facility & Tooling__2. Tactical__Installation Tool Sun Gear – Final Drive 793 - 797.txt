1. Introduction  
 
Ferreyros has developed a tool to improve the assembly process of the final drive truck 793 - 
797. The tool is designed to install sun gear for the technical component does not manipulate 
with their hands, also prevents damage to the sun gear to move its base. Caterpillar does not 
have a special tool for this process. 
 
2. Best Practice Description 
 
The installation of the sun gear in the Planetary was very manual, making the technician use their 
hands on the sun gear to align with the planetary of the final drive.  The technician then releases 
the sun gear, moving towards its base. 
 
 
 
  
This procedure does not allow the technician to quickly and safely install the sun gear. The 
alignment of the sun gear with the planetary is difficult and endangers the hands of technician.

At time of installation, the technician does not use their hands on the sun gear until tool is

Remove the tool once installed safely in the sun gear. This same tool should be used for the 
removal process, because it minimizes risks during the operation for the sun gear and the 
people around.
