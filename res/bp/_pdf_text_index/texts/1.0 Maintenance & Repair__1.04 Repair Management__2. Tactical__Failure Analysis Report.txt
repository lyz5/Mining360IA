1.0 Introduction  
 
Trakindo is one of the largest dealerships in the world, due to the nature of the archipelagos they 
have over 60 branches and 20 site offices that make up their dealership network. Each one of the 
branches has specific capabilities from parts distribution, field service capability to component 
rebuilds. From these outlets they have put together a series of best practices that they have 
recognized internally to help all the branches.  
 
This best practice is dealing with Failure Analysis Management. It begins with the complete 
report to the specially supporting document for warranty, any redo reports and finally product 
problems.  
 
The reason for this best practice is that Failure Analysis Management has a big impact on 
Trakindo’s working business. After carrying out surveys at their major branches Trakindo has 
found that there was no consistency in a standard process and document to ensure all the 
complete documentation is available at time reporting for Failure Analysis.  
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Previously Trakindo had no standard documents for Failure Analysis reporting. The documents 
that was available where incomplete in content.  
 
This new standard document will ensure that the technician or respective person can fill out all 
the correct available information and follow up with the respective person if any information is 
missing. Long term it will also standardize the Failure Analysis Report management and ensure 
that with cross transfer of people they are using the same process across all branches.  
 
This best practice will also save time in reporting Failure Analysis, hopefully speeding up 
warranty claims and carry out in solving problems that occur.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
- Determine the personnel who will be responsible to fill out the Failure Analysis Report form.  
- Advise where the form is located in the portal or system.  
- Advise all respective persons to implement using the form in the day to day process.  
- Promote change feedback as installation may create new problems.  
- Train all employees who have to use the new form regarding why this is required and the 
importance of having all completed documentation available on time at job closing.  
- Review process performance once established.  
- Complete any adjustment as required.  
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
Efficiency – With the implementation of this new format the failure analysis management will 
become more efficient. It will save the administration time to check that all required information is 
complete. If not they will also be able to see which persons is require to send the missing related 
information.  
 
Increase Quality – More effective form simplifies the process - promoting high technician 
professionalism, higher standard and increasing brand image.  
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
People – Training for those involved with Failure Analysis to advise on the importance 
completing the form, and the use of the new standard document.
