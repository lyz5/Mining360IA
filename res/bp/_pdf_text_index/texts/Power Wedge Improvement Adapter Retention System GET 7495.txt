1.0 Introduction 
I
n the introduction of GET 7495 period 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019 no events were observed in the 
adapter hammerless system. In the year 2022 we present loose adapter wedge events and evaluate 
the need to implement a retention cap with a clamping screw. 
 
T
he 7495 machine is of high production and rarely do we have times of opportunity to be able to 
intervene in the equipment and perform adjustment checks on the 3983032-power wedge that allow us 
to maintain reliability in the System.

To gu
arantee the permanence of the wedge in the adapter and avoid possible misalignment of the 
wedge, an improvement is made to the cover and a screw is added. 
• T
he design of the wedge screw changed to be able to place a cover that contains a hexagonal
head screw to hold the cover.
