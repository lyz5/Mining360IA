1.0 Introduction 
A support tool  has been designed for the assembly process of the  oil pan on the C175 
models. This tool helps keep all parts of the oil pan level and evenly distributed during  the 
time of installation which makes it an easier and more effective process. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
Place the oil pan in the dedicated slots of the support tool. U se the adjustable supports to 
help assemble all parts of the oil pan until you get it completely level.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Manufacture support according to the dimensions of oil pan 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
   
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Through the implementation of this tool, the following benefits have been observed: 
 
• Reduced installation time: 
o Before: 40 minutes 
o Now:  15 minutes 
• Significant safety increase by reducing the risk of hand injuries to technicians 
 
 
4.0 Resources Required 
The total cost to develop and fabricate this tool was $2,348
