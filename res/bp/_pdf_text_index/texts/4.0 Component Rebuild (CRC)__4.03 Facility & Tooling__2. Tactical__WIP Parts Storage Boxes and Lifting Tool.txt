1.0 Introduction  
 
Trakindo’s Component Rebuild Centers (CRCs) at all times have Work in Process (WIP).  WIP when 
not stored and arranged well can affect the centers efficiency, cleanliness (Contamination Control), 
quality, and safety.  To assist in the storage and efficient arrangement of the WIP, Trakindo has 
implemented a standard WIP Parts Storage Box (yellow box) to keep parts or small components 
organized and safe in while waiting to complete a job. 
 
In some CRCs with a high volume of work orders, many WIP Parts Storage Boxes may be required.  
These boxes are usually placed on the floor and take up a large amount of space. Currently the 
boxes are placed only one high and not stacked, decreasing floor space utilization.   
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
To increase available floor space and make the workshop area more efficient, Trakindo recommends 
that the boxes be stacked two high using a bench (rack). Currently they do not have the capability in 
the workshop to use forklifts to lift the boxes. 
 
In order to solve the inefficiency with the single height stacking practice, Trakindo has developed a 
lifting tool to stack the boxes on a bench and increase floor space, in tu rn increasing workshop 
efficiency.

The lifting tool is fabricated from standard ‘U’ beam and box section steel with a thickness of +/- 5cm 
(see picture in Section 6.0).   There are two separate models of fabricated steel, with an additional 
chain that will lift the box safely and easily, and not damage the box. 
 
The tool is designed to make it ea sy and safe  for double stacking the boxes , resulting in a dditional 
space and cleanliness within the shop.  This is especially useful when a shop does not have sufficient 
space to use a forklift.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
-   Fabricate the tool. 
- Determine area in shop to be designated for rack. 
- Fabricate rack - if not currently available. 
- Assure the area has adequate space and access for a crane to move the boxes. 
- Assure crane is available (jip crane or overhead crane) to move the boxes to and from rack. 
- Implement the layout changes into the shop. 
o Suggest the racks are staged in the assembly bay. 
- Promote change feedback as installation may create new problems. 
- Train employees to use the new procedures/equipment. 
- Review process/equipment performance once established. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
Safety 
- Well designed/organized storage rack will improve cleaning efforts and safety. 
 
Organization 
- Yellow Boxes are consistently staged in specific areas for each process step.  
 
Quality 
- More effectively placed WIP simplifies staging and the cleaning process. 
- Promotes high technician professionalism. 
- Meets cleanliness (contamination control) requirements. 
 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
Fabrication and Facility 
- Steel standard ‘U’ beam (5mm). 
- Reorganized the work area to allow for stacking of incoming and outgoing boxes. 
 
People 
- Provide training on use of lifting device. 
- Establish the new process, lay out, and equipment improvement through the team. 
- Remove replaced items from operation to sustain the gains.
