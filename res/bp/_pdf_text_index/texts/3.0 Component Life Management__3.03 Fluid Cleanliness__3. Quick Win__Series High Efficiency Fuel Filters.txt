1.0 Introduction  
 
Modern high-pressure, electronically controlled fuel systems are used on nearly all Caterpillar 
engines. These systems deliver better performance and improved fuel economy over older 
mechanical systems. In general, electronic injectors should last until engine overhaul if relatively 
clean fuel is used along with high quality, high efficiency fuel filters. 
 
However, because modern injectors produce higher injection pressures, (typically 25,000 – 
30,000 psi) they are subject to accelerated wear from abrasive particles in the fuel. Using fuel 
with high levels of particulate contaminants or continuing to operate the machine after the 
plugged fuel filter light has been activated can cause rapid injector wear.  
 
 
 
Adding an additional fuel filter in series with the standard fuel filter can significantly improve 
injector life. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
  
3500 Series engines use two 4-micron fuel filters in parallel to provide adequate debris holding 
capacity. This arrangement provides single pass filtration, which is adequate if: 
• The fuel is reasonably clean 
• F uel filters are changed before or immediately after the plugged filter light comes on 
  
The filter media (paper) in the filter, which actually traps and holds the particulate debris, is 
capable of single pass efficiency in excess of 98%. However, as the debris holding capacity of the 
media is reached, flow restriction through the media increases and the fuel pressure drop across 
the media starts to increase. This pressure difference between the inlet and outlet sides of the 
filter is commonly referred to as “delta p.” Filter efficiency typically remains high until the filter 
reaches about 18 – 20 psi delta p. At this point, the pressure differential exceeds the ability of the 
media to hold trapped debris particles and begins to push debris through the filter media. As delta 
p increases, the size and number of particles pushed through the media increases sharply. These 
particles cause injector abrasive wear and loss of performance.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Factory arrangements are not available. However, the series filter can be added by fabricating a 
mounting bracket and hoses.  
 
 
 
  
4.0 Benefits 
 
Installing series filtration can protect the injectors and help them last until engine overhaul. It is 
not uncommon for applications with poor fuel quality to replace injectors at engine mid-life. On a 
3516, the cost of mid-life injector replacement is $20,000 or more. Using series filtration can often 
eliminate this cost.
