1.0 Introduction  
 
A fabricated stand has been designed for use in the Ferreyros S.A. weld shop in the repair of D10R rear 
frames.  The stands makes it easier, safer, and much more efficient to align the frame tube and support for 
weld repairs.  Stand drawing are not available, but photos are provided to show the design concept. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The fabricated fixture consists of two stands mounted on a level platform, one to mount the frame tube and 
the other for the support.  The two stands are designed to center the tube to the support for welding the tube 
to the support.  (See picture 1). 
 
 
 
 
Picture 1. Frame Rear D10R 
 
To repair cracks between the tube and the support (zone A in photo), the tube must be correctly aligned to 
the support.

4.0 Benefits 
 
 
• Time required to set up and align the tube to the support is reduced by 70%. 
• Parts alignment is very precise. 
• Time required to perform the welding is significantly reduced.   
• Weld quality is easier to control. 
• Material handling is easier to perform.
