1.0 Introduction 
A
 new tool has been designed for tightening the rear PTO drive shaft bolts on C175 engines. 
2
.0 Best Practice Description 
T
his Best Practice covers an upgraded design to the standard CAT tool P/N 478-1934.  The 
standard Cat tool allows 9 of the 12 rear PTO bolts on the C175 engine to be tightened before the 
tool is repositioned, and due to cylindrical geometry of the bolt contact area, creates a potential for 
contaminate generation with constant use.  The new design is shaped to allow 11 of the 12 rear 
PTO bolts to be tightened before repositioning the tool and utilizes a grooved bolt contact area 
that minimizes the risk of generating contamination.

3
.0 Implementation Steps 
T
he use of the new tool is similar to the CAT tool (478-1934) it is placed in the rear housing and 
allows to lock one bolt of the rear PTO drive shaft of the C175 engines and tighten 11 bolts to the 
specified torque, then turn to lock another bolt and tighten the last bolt.
