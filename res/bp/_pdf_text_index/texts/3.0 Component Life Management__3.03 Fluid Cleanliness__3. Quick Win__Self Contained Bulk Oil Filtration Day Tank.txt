1.0 Introduction  
 
It is important to fill newly rebuilt or repaired engines and powertrain components with clean oil. 
Dealers and customers are looking for multi-purpose oil storage tanks for their shops, which can 
also clean new oil.  This system provides a consistent source of clean oil based on daily usage. 
 
• Nearly all large shops have bulk oil tanks for various grades of oil. Unfortunately, most oil 
picks up particulate debris from the transportation and storage process before it reaches the 
end user. 
• Filtering large quantities of oil requires large circulation/filtration units and must be operated 
continuously, often producing unpredictable results. 
• In order to minimize contamination related failures of new rebuilt, or repaired components, it is 
important to minimize three sources of debris: 
o Assembly debris in the component 
o Break-in debris generated by the component  
o Debris in new oil used in the component 
 
• Many machine powertrain and hydraulic systems use electro-hydraulic valves to provide 
precise control of implement operation and clutch engagement. These valves require very 
clean oil to function properly. Cleaning bulk oil before it is put into the machine system 
reduces the opportunity for contamination-related failures.  
• Providing a system of filtering only the oil that will be used in a specific time interval reduces 
filtration time as well as increasing fill oil quality. 
 
Using clean bulk oil reduces early hour contamination failures and helps to improve reliability. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The self-contained bulk oil filtration day tank automatically provides clean oil for shop use. This 
unit draws oil from the much larger bulk storage tank and holds an adequate amount to supply 
the shop for a day’s operation. The unit also filters the stored oil automatically through an 
integrated pump and filter system, which has a number of safeguards to assure proper operation. 
This automatic fill and filtration system provides the user the option of “off time” fill, manual 
override, programmable kidney loop duration and on demand delivery to point of use. The 
following are major features: 
 
• Double walled tank to minimize spills and leakage. 100 or 280 gallon capacity. 
• Single-walled tanks available for containment areas. 
• 3 hp electric circulation pump provides a steady 25 gpm flow to the filters to maximize filtration 
efficiency.   
• 3 micron or 5 micron series high efficiency filter elements with 30 psi filter bypass and 
electrical plugged filter indicator. 
• 12 gpm 6:1 air operated supply pump with thermo relief supplies clean, filtered outlet oil to the 
oil delivery system. 
• Secondary tank breather filter prevents dirty air from entering the outer tank chamber 
• Desiccant breather filter provides clean, dry air to the inner primary tank.

4.0 Benefits 
• Using consistently clean oil reduces early hour contamination failures and improves reliability.   
o Finning Chile utilizes two of the units for HYDO and Engine pre-fill oil applications 
achieving consistent 16/13 cleanliness levels. 
o Whayne supply utilizes one of the units for engine pre-fill oil providing consistent 
16/13 cleanliness levels. 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• Investment Costs: $12,000 - $18,000 (depending on size and installation location). 
• Facility: It is recommended this unit be installed in an enclosed location where temperatures 
remain between 15 – 32 °C (60 – 90 °F) to ensure consistent oil flow rate and minimize 
internal tank condensation. 
• Tools: standard maintenance tools. 
• Support equipment: A low-pressure delivery pump from the bulk tank to the day tank.  This 
may be controlled by the day tank processor when fill is required. 
• People: Daily monitoring of operation and filter condition is recommended. 
• Training: SOS sampling and/or Particle Counter operation is recommended.
