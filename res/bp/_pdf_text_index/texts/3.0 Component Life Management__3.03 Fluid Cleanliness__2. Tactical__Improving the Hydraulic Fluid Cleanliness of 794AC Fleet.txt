1.0 Introduction  
 
Guaranteeing the fluid cleanliness is one of the biggest challenges of the maintenance manager in 
his quest to guarantee performance of components. 
 
Maintaining the fluid cleanliness below or equal to the Caterpillar guideline/ISO 18/16/13 is a 
challenge for most of the major mining operations in Brazil even before fluids go into the machine 
due to oil transport, storage and transferring between tanks and machines.  Additional factors can 
affect the cleanliness inside of the machines’ hydraulic systems  due to leaks in damaged hoses, 
broken seals, and overall unintentional contamination during maintenance; in conditions of high 
utilization where maintenance has to be done in windows of opportunity, which may occur outside 
of a clean bay, contamination control becomes more and more important but also more and more 
difficult resulting in the need for filtration. 
 
One of Sotreq’s largest customer s encountered the following SAE 10W SOS results out of 
specification in the hydraulic systems of their 794AC Fleet and bulk storage tanks. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Source/Picture 1: Customer/Dealer SOS Results and SAE10W at oil tank in site 
 
This customer already has an oil filtration system for the bulk storage tanks, so they requested the 
support of the dealer to find a solution to maintain oil cleanliness on board the trucks.  This type of 
solution would guarantee clean oil throughout the machine, independent of existing conditions, and 
result in improved component life and reduced downtime during PM’s (preventive maintenance).  
With this solution the customer is also looking for improved serviceability of the electric drive trucks, 
lower number of tools used in the overall maintenance and an overall lower maintenance ratio.   
Their vision was to apply the principles of conventional off -board kidney looping machine 
(filtration/dialysis, participle count, etc.) typically used during PMs to an on-board kidney looping 
system 
 
Sotreq developed an on-board continuous filtration (oil dialysis) system.  This aftermarket solution 
aligns with market practices of continuous cleaning for hydraulic compartments for mobile 
equipment. 
 
Implementation of this system i n the 794AC trucks has shown good results – clean oil, clean 
compartments, and lower maintenance ratio.  Longer life for the hydraulic c omponents is the 
ultimate goal for this system; it is expected that this will be the ultimate result of the developed 
system.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The 794AC hydraulic system of the 794AC trucks is comprised of approximately 11 principal items,  
with filters at the entrance of each component capable of filtering particles of 4 microns, as shown 
in the figure below.  As a note, 10W  oil is used throughout the Brazilian market.   
 
The customer requested that Sotreq come up with a “bulletproof” solution for keeping the hydraulic 
system clean that didn’t adversely affect the components or overall functionality and reliability of the 
truck.  The challenge was to transform a conventional off board dialysis system to a system 
integrated (onboard) with the OEM equipment (base machine). 
 
 
Picture 2 – Steering Filter, (2) Radiator Fan Filter, (3) PTO filter, (4) Hydraulic Tank Filter, (5) Hydraulic 
Tank, (6) Steering Tank, (7) Radiator Fan Tank, (8) PTO, (9) Lift Tank, (10) GP Li ft Valve, (11) Lift Filters . 
 
As seen in the Best Practice 1.01.210827-1, this Brazilian customer operates a fleet of 794AC trucks 
with maintenance based on the lean concept with focus on improving the productivity of the PM.  To 
do conventi onal dialysis in  the shop demands preparation time, connection checks, regular 
maintenance and testing of the dialysis machines and allocation of personnel/time for the actual 
dialysis.  Conventional analysis does  not support improved PM productivity and is not 100% 
effective in the long term of operation.    
 
Sotreq evaluated OEM dialysis systems in its existing Sotreq network and partnered with an OEM, 
that they had previously worked with on other applications, to create an on-board filtration system 
to 1) deliver high quality compartment cleaning, 2) conform to the recommended Caterpillar ISO 
specification, 3) facilitate quality and efficient maintenance and 4) cause no risks to the machine (as 
it is connected directly to the machine). 
 
A filtration unit with 1.458 L of flow and volume for the hydraulic system of the 794AC at 3 times per 
12 working hour shift  was chosen.  It was sized to hold 4 Kg of dirt, 2 L of water and 4 Kg of oil 
oxidation.  It had the capability to filter 98.7% of the particles of 3 microns or above and 50% of the 
particles between 0.8 to 3 microns.

This on-board dialysis system complements the filtration system of the 794AC.  It provides additional 
protection and redundancy in situations 1) where the machine hydraulic system has a bypass due 
to high contamination and 2) on some 794AC  trucks, there are no electronic restriction 
indications/alarms since in most cases there are no sensors that are ultimately connected to the 
machine monitoring system. This system has the potential to be a powerful tool for the long-te rm 
extension (above 4,000 hours) of the fleet’s hydraulic oil (oil oxidation retention). 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Picture 3: On-board filtration unit selected 
 
The basic design of this on-board solution follows the principals of the conventional dialysis with oil 
suctioned from the hydraulic tank, passed through the filtration unit (now onboard vs. off board) and 
then returned to the same hydraulic tank.  A clean oil supply at all time is the result. 
 
NOTE:  EACH CUSTOMER OR DEALER CAN DEFINE THE BEST ON-BOARD FILTRATION UNIT 
FOR THE FLEET WHILE RESPECTING THE TECHNICAL ASPECTS AND RISK FOR THE 
EQUIPMENT.  THIS BEST PRACTICE DOES NOT AIM TO PRODUCE A COMMERCIAL/FOR SALE 
SYSTEM OR TO TRANSFORM IT INTO A PRODUCTION SOLUTION.  THE BEST PRACTICE 
DEMONSTRATES RESULTS OF A FIELD TEST PERFORMED FOR THIS CONCEPT.

Note: Caterpillar genuine parts are used for the hoses, connectors, clamps,  and other parts to 
support the configuration and installation of the on-board filtering system.  The filtration unit itself is 
the only non-Caterpillar part, which is available from an OEM that Sotreq has previously partnered 
with in applications for mining, construction, industrial and power generation. 
 
Note:  If the on-board filtration system has any failure in the unit or a connection between the 
unit/installation, the system will not damage the truck hydraulic system. 
• If the on-board filtration system has any restrictions in the hydraulic line, the unit discharges the 
oil into the tank without any damage since it is not connected to the main hydraulic line. 
• There are no restriction valves installed throughout the unit. 
• If there is an electrical failure no damage will occur as the unit hydraulic lines work separately 
from the main truck hydraulic lines.  
• The assumption is the on-board filter connection is the same as connecting an off-board system.

chosen by on the fleet’s characteristics): 
1) Installation of the filtration system power supply. 
2) Installation of the filtration system unit on board the truck and attached to the platform 
3) Training of the mechanics on both operation and maintenance

1. Improved Cleaning of the Hydraulic System: 
 
The on-board filtration system has proven effective in delivering results even with leaks and 
other existing contamination onboard as well as the high particle count (ISO > 18/16/13) in 10W 
oil in the customer’s oil tanks that are used to fill the 794AC’s.

2. Improved Reliability and Performance: 
 
No exchange events in the hydraulic system, minor components and PTO were encountered 
between 0 and 4,000 hours . The onboard filtration system has contributed to a reduction in 
unscheduled downtime, even with outstanding CPI issues that have caused wear elements in 
the oil , or other downtime events. Ultimately, we expect to see major components  
meeting/exceeding life expectancy with the addition of this on-board filtration system. 
 
 
Downtime Drives Accumulated (4,000 hours) – Hydraulic System had zero downtime events.  
  
 
 
Downtime Drives Breakdown (4,000 hours) – No hydraulic system issues. The Brake System in this case was CPI 
failures about the piston wear out, not around the oil condition.  
 
 
3. Improved PM Productivity: 
 
Sotreq’s execution team conducted an evaluation of the time and actual task routes/movements 
of the lubrification process, including fluid cleaning and supply of the trucks.   Use of the on-
board filtration system instead of the off -board kidney looping machine has eliminated 6 hours 
and 20 minutes of work from the PM process.  Additionally, this system has both increased the 
PM productivity and reduced the maintenance ratio of the machine.
