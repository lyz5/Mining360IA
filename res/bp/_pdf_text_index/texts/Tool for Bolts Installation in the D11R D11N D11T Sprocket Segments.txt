1.0 Introduction  
 
The Installation of the sprocket segments on TTT is a task in which we must be especially careful 
when tightening the bolts since such failure could cause an unscheduled shutdown of the machine 
and risks the safety of the technical personnel performing the task.  Technicians are exposed to pinch 
points and arm fatigue. 
 
Currently for the torque procedure of the bolts a lever resting on the final drive is used in order to hold 
the nuts exposing the technician to a possible accident and it ensures the correct torque on the bolts 
is used. Fig.1.  
 
 
 
 
     
 
 
Fig. 1. Former old bolt torque process. 
 
 
The figure above shows the way the technicians are exposed when seizing the wrench holding the 
nuts. 
 
This Best Practice provides the design and manufacture of a tool for fastening the nuts when 
performing the bolts torque procedure.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This applies when changing the sprocket segments in D11N, D11R and D11T Tractors where 
there have been delays in the proper installation of the bolts because there is no tool to hold the 
nut while tightening the bolts. 
 
Geometrically, the tool has a “U” shape, in one of its end, it has a 1 ¼ " and 4 ¼" diameter bar in 
length and in the other end it has a diameter of 2 ¼ " and a length of 1 3/8" bored concentrically 
with the silhouette of the head of the bolt to be installed. The two ends are joined by a 1" plate 
triangle shaped having rounded edges due to the difference in diameters of the two ends. 
Made entirely of ASTM A36 steel, this tool also has a handle so that the technician can 
comfortably hold it with his hand when installing or transporting. 
 
In Fig. 3. We can see the way it holds the head of the bolt and it takes advantage of the segments 
teeth in order to lock itself and thus it reduces the exposure of the technicians to possible 
accidents and it improves the torque of the bolts reducing the installation time by up to 40 %. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Figure 2. Sprocket segments bolts holding tool.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
 
o Design of the tool. 
 EDITOR’S NOTE:  Caterpillar recommends considering making this tool with a 1” square 
drive so it can work on smaller tractors. 
 
o Manufacturing of the tool. 
 
o Operation of the tool (see Figure 3) 
o From the inside of the sprocket, place the largest diameter end of the tool in order to 
hold the bolt head with the bored silhouette. 
o Make sure the opposite end of the tool (1 ¼ " bar) is placed in one of the spaces 
between the sprocket teeth. 
o Install the torque meter along with the adapters required to tighten the nut on the 
outside of the sprocket, make sure when rotating the tool that it is locked with one of the 
sprocket teeth thereby preventing the rotation of the bolt. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Figure 3. Operation of the tool for applying torque the screws segments sprocket 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
 It improves the safety of the task. 
 It reduces the installation time of the sprocket segments. 
 I t improves the torque application. 
 There is higher availability of the equipment.
