1.0 Introduction  
 
This Best Practice is a dealer fabricated oil filtration tool to accommodate Field Service Operation on 
doing Maintenance and Repair to filtrate new or use oil until meet CAT Roll off Specification. 
 
This tool solves power source problems in remote areas that have no existing electricity or 
compressor sources, and provide better services to customer by simplifying the job by minimizing the 
tools required to complete the filtration service by compartmentalizing the process into a semi-
compact filtration cart. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The purpose of fabricating this tool is accommodating Field Service Operation on the oil filtration 
process while the job is located at a remote area without existing electricity or compressor.  
 
Technicians do not need to find electricity or bring compressor to job location for filtration process 
because it is all incorporated in a semi-portable gasoline engine driven filtration tool. Just like ordinary 
filtration cart, connect the hose to the tool and machine for kidney looping or connect the hose to the 
tool, machine and oil barrel for transferring new oil.
