1.0 Introduction  
Demand planning for key replacement parts & components is critically important as the Cat 
dealer works with the local mine site to achieve machine availability targets as well as other key 
mining metrics. In order to properly plan for upcoming parts needs at the site, many dealers have 
been using elements of the Planned Component Replacement (PCR) process, a process in place 
since approximately 2005, to understand what, when, and how many major components will be 
required for the Traditional Mining machines operating on sites in their territory. And given the 
parts commonality typically inherent with traditional Cat machines, the PCR process has worked 
well in preparing the enterprise supply chain (both the dealer and Caterpillar) to ensure the right 
mining parts are available at the right time and delivered to the right location to enable machine 
uptime. 
 
However, with the introduction of Expanded Mining machines into the Cat mining product 
portfolio, it was observed the PCR process may not be sufficient in providing an accurate 
depiction of what key replacement parts & components are required. One of the primary drivers 
for this is due to the lack of parts commonality that typically exists in the Expanded Mining 
machine models, as these machines can be more custom in nature and have parts lists that are 
not widely shared even within the same product line. Therefore, in order to ensure the correct 
parts are being planned for by the site, dealer, and Cat, the Plan for Every Serial Number 
(PLESN) initiative was launched at the beginning of 2015. Not only does PLESN provide greater 
insight into the correct key replacement parts & components that will be needed for an upcoming 
rebuild or replacement event, but PLESN also helps drive:  
 
• I ncreased communication between the site, dealer, and Cat on the current & desired repair 
strategy for a specific mining machine 
• Accurate identification of future parts & service sales opportunities 
• B etter understanding of the required parts, labor, and tooling that will be required to 
service the machine and maximize uptime 
• I mproved mining parts and machine availability to achieve customer satisfaction & 
expectations 
• E nablement of the Seed / Grow / Harvest model to achieve POPS-C and OPACC targets.  
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
This Best Practice describes the internal processes that Finning Canada is implementing to better 
leverage and take action on PLESN data. The primary areas of focus for this Best Practice are: 
roles & responsibilities; process used by Finning’s mining parts supply chain team; and the 
process used by Finning’s mining parts commercial team. 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
Before beginning on a successful journey with the Plan for Every Serial Number, it is imperative 
the dealer’s senior leadership team are committed and engaged, and are supportive of the effort 
at the outset. The PLESN initiative crosses functional areas at the dealership, is resource-
intensive, and does require much hard work in order to develop and stabilize the necessary 
processes. Without this level of engagement and commitment, it is not likely to be successful. 
Similarly, Cat region leadership (both in the Global Aftermarket Solutions Division [GASD] and 
Cat Global Mining [CGM] must also fully support and proactively engage in the PLESN initiative

After commitment to the initiative has been given, a process map for how PLESN will functionally 
and tactically work should be developed. Cat has a high-level demand planning process map that 
can be leveraged, but the dealer should then customize the details within that high-level map to 
ensure local considerations are taken into account. Please see the attached file ‘PLESN Process-

action the data. To reiterate, however, each dealer should localize the process map to 
incorporate any relevant local nuances. 
 
Once the process map has been established, a Roles & Responsibilities (R&R) document can be 
created to ensure key departments, and individuals within those departments, are identified for 
the process. This helps ensure each understands his or her responsibility in the PLESN process, 
and how his or her work contributes to the success of the PLESN initiative in order to fully 
maximize its potential. An example of the R&R can be found in the supporting attachment titled 
‘PLESN Required Data and R&R (April 2016).pptx’ for reference. 
 
As these implementation steps are completed, communication throughout the organization is 
critical to ensure people are aware of what is happening, what the change means to them, who 
they can turn to for questions and clarity, and how they can support the endeavor. For example, 
Finning developed both an internal dealer document, as well as an external customer document, 
to ensure the initiative was fully communicated and understood by those who would be impacted. 
By more thoroughly communicating with key individuals and groups, Finning was able to gain 
better engagement both inside and outside the dealership. 
 
Additionally as part of the internal communication strategy, Finning developed an internal site that 
can be accessed by dealer employees to not only learn more about the PLESN initiative, but 
access important documents such as PLESN FAQ’s, presentations, templates, status updates,

Also, Finning leveraged the Tableau software to be able to effectively and efficiently summarize 
and share completed PLESN data results received from the account representatives. To explain 
the Tableau screen (see Exhibit B below), Finning customized the report to include the following 
areas: 
 
• Upper right quadrant: provides a high-level, consolidated view of SKU (Stock Keeping Unit, or 
line item) and the associated extended dealer net opportunity by the probability of sale % 
o This gives the dealer a quick view of the total dealer net opportunity from the completed 
plans submitted by the account representatives, and the associated probability of 
closing those opportunities as won. 
• Upper left and middle quadrants: provides an understanding of what machines are active 
compared to the total population by region (left quadrant) and how many PLESN’s have been 
completed compared to the total active population by region (middle quadrant) 
o This provides the dealer a quick glance at how many machines are active in a given 
region, and also how many of those active machines in the region have a completed 
PLESN 
• Lower left quadrant: is a high-level consolidation of SKU and the associated extended dealer 
net opportunity by % to PCR category; those within the 0% to 74% to PCR range are 
considered “On Time”; those within the range of 75% to 99% are considered “Close to 
Replace”; those over 100% are considered “Overdue” 
o This quantifies for the dealer the various opportunities coming due and the value of the 
opportunities that need immediate or near-term attention 
• Lower right quadrant: shows how many SKU are coming due for order and when the order 
should be placed (based on operational hours being forecasted as well as the Cat lead time to 
ship the part to Finning (from order to arrival)), as well as the current stock levels for Finning & 
Cat 
o This provides Finning a quick snapshot of the volume of orders that will need to be 
placed on Cat to ensure the necessary parts are in the correct location when needed to 
support the rebuild / replace / repair event

4.0 Benefits 
There are multiple benefits, both for the customer and the dealer, that result from a robust Plan 
for Every Serial Number process. First, concerning the customer, the following benefits should be 
realized:  
• Higher machine availability and therefore productivity due to understanding the what part is 
needed, when it is needed, and where it is needed 
• Thorough understanding by the dealer and Cat of the customer’s mining machine, its 
current condition, and the repair strategy 
• Improved parts availability and therefore a reduction in emergency freight and fees due to 
future demand signals being provided to the dealer & Cat, and associated action being 
taken when and where necessary 
• Unavailability metric due to parts delays for component replacement should be minimized 
as proper planning & scheduling takes place between the site, dealer, and Cat 
• PCR compliance metric should be maximized through the coordination and 
synchronization between the site, dealer, and Cat of what replace / rebuild / repair events 
are expected to occur and when they are expected to occur 
 
Similarly, there are also many benefits to the dealer that flow from a solid PLESN process in 
place. Some of these include: 
• Improved visibility and understanding of parts & service opportunities coming due with key 
mining customers & sites  
• Potential to grow parts & labor sales and POPS-C (Percentage of Parts Sales – Caterpillar 
(equipment)) with mining customer base 
o As an example, Finning was able to develop an effective proposal / quote and then 
close a ~$4 million opportunity for Expanded Mining ERS (Electric Rope Shovel) 
undercarriage through an opportunity highlighted by the PLESN effort 
• Improved parts margin & OPACC (Operating Profit After Capital Charge) by lowering 
emergency / expediting freight and fees through improved demand planning 
• Improved coordination of planning across departments (Parts, Product Support Marketing / 
Sales, Service, CRC (Component Rebuild Center)) at the dealership regarding future 
product support business 
• Change from reactive position responding to the customer’s demands to a proactive 
position 
o Dealer is able to engage the customer early to discuss options rather than after 
failure or after the customer has decided on a competitive alternative 
• Opportunity to further the Seed / Grow / Harvest model and strengthen the dealer’s PINS 
(Percentage of Industry Sales) position through customer product support satisfaction 
• Better position of PSSR (Parts & Service Sales Representative) / Account Representative 
as proactive consultant to the customer  
o By leveraging the Tableau software, Account Representatives can drill down to a 
specific machine serial number / part number to understand what is coming due and 
when; this allows the Account Representative to better plan for the next visit to the 
site, understanding what opportunities need to be quoted and/or closed to allow for 
the necessary order processing and lead time to have the part ready when required 
by the customer

5.0 Resources Required 
In order to develop an effective PLESN process at the dealership, resources are required, 
primarily in the area of personnel. Although Finning has invested in electronic resources / 
systems to better facilitate the aggregation, summarization, and communication of PLESN, this is 
not a requirement. However, active engagement from key personnel is critical to PLESN’s 
success.

responsibilities should consistently be part of the PLESN process if the initiative is to be 
successful and achieve the aforementioned benefits. These roles & responsibilities can also be
