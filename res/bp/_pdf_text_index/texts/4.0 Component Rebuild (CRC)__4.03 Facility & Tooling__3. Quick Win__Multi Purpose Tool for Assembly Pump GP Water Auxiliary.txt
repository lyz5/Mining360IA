1.0 Introduction  
 
This best practice was developed to reduce human involvement during the pump GP auxiliary 
assembly by using a unique tool that Ferreyros developed for this purpose. This best practice 
gives the specification and design requirements to build the tool, as well as the process steps to 
implement and use the tool in the assembly process. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The tool might be used with 3508 692, 3512 662, 3516 722, 3508 746, 3508B 26R 3512 8BR, 
3512 962, 3516 2PK, 3516B 77R, 785C APX 992G 994 94F 777D 3PR, 785D DMC engines.  
This tool will be used for installing the bearing P/N 127-5400, ceramic seal, impeller and cover 
following a sequence below. 
 
The design specs for the tool are shown in the supporting attachments section, and below is what 
the finished tool will look like.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Decreases human effort during the installation of the bearing, seal, impeller and cover during 
pump GP auxiliary assembly; 
• Simplifies the assembly process for the pump GP water auxiliary.
