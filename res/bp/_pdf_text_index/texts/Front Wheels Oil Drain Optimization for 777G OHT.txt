1.0 Introduction  
 
Maintenance efficiency is frequently optimized due to its importance. Thus, maintenance schedules, 
special tools, and several artifices are used to improve the physical availability of equipment and meet 
customer expectations, regarding product quality, workmanship, and environmental policies. 
 
Considering this scenario, it was observed that, during the activity of draining the hydraulic oil from the 
front wheels, part of the oil was spilled on the wheel or even on the ground, contaminating the ground 
and leaving marks on the wheel during the operation of the equipment, once there was no suitable tool 
for such activity. 
 
To carry out the task of draining the front wheels  of the 777G fleet, field technicians used to place a 
bucket below the drain plug as Picture 1 illustrates. It does not stabilize on the wheel and eventually 
falls or moves out of position, causing oil to spill over the wheel and onto the ground. This entails, in 
addition to soil contamination, time loss during the preventive procedure, as the technician team needs 
to clean up the area affected by the oil in the equipment and on the floor. 
In addition, the technician often needs  to hold the bucket during the entire oil drain period to avoid 
spillage and contamination.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• R eduction of preventive time; 
 
In the event of an oil spill during collection, it was observed that the technician spent about two 
hours providing resources and cleaning the floor and wheels. With the tool, we started to avoid 
this type of event. in this way, we reduce preventive maintenance time by approximately two 
hours per piece of equipment.  
In addition, the technician can carry out tasks while the oil is drained, without needing to hold 
buckets.  
He gains about 40 minutes doing other preventive activities. 
 
• Reduction of soil contamination.
