1.0 Introduction  
 
The optimization of maintenance activities is often enhanced due to their importance and impact on 
asset performance. Therefore, the search for special tools or aids to increase equipment physical 
availability, accuracy, and speed in fault diagnosis is necessary to meet customer expectations. In this 
scenario, this Best Practice will present a tool option that provides easy and quick diagnostics 
(approximately within 5 minutes). 
 
This tool improves fault diagnosis in the air conditioning system, guiding the technician to the damaged 
circuit: 
 
• Electrical Supply (White LED 24V Description). 
• Grounding (Green LED with GN Description). 
• Thermostat circuit - high and low circuit (Yellow LED with SW Description). 
• Compressor Clutch Energy Supply (Red LED with CLU Description).

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Significant delays occur daily in diagnosing air conditioning and electrical systems, due to the numerous 
checkpoints required to identify the fault. Lack of accuracy and delays in the diagnostic process are 
recurring issues. The time can range from 2 to 3 hours depending on who is responsible for operating 
it or the available resources. 
 
The Main Problems Are: 
 
• Insufficient technical resources and equipment to carry out the activities. 
• Limited technical knowledge of the teams involved. 
• Numerous checkpoints in the air conditioning system.
