1.0 Introduction 
During big PMs, oil in final drives is changed. Previously, to drain the used oil from the final drives 
the service team used various materials on hand (carton box, tin boxes, etc.) as a bridge between 
the wheel hub and the cart for used oil. Using makeshift items such as these can create potential 
for safety risks. For example, if a cardboard box is used it can get wet resulting in oil being spilt on 
the floor of the workshop. Spilt oil presents a significant slip hazard.  
Borusan Makina Kazakhstan has developed a simple fixture that allows draining the used oil from 
the final drive without spilling it on the floor. 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
This fixture consists of the tank (1), the filler (2), the tap (3), 2 x handles (4) and 2 x bottom plates 
(5) of the different height for providing the slope of the fixture.
Us
ed oil drain fixture 
This fixture enables to drain the used oil from the final drive with no spillage of the oil on the floor 
or ground.

3.0 Implementati
on Steps 
 Decide on what model of OHT trucks this fixture will be used (it will help to determi ne the
size of the fixture). Our fixture was designed for 785C;
 Prepare the drawings required;
 Fabricate the fixture;
 Give the fixture to the service team for use.
4.0 Benefits 
 Easy to use and store;
 The used oil is drained from the final drive with no spillage of the oil on the floor or ground.
The contamination Control is followed.
5.0 Resources Required 
 Materials to fabricate this fixture;
 Welder with welding unit.
