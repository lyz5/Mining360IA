1.0 Introduction  
 
Maintenance and Repair Contracts (MARC) can provide a great service to customers and a 
worthwhile return for dealers, but only when developed and executed properly. Experience in 
Global Mining has shown contracts being unprofitable for the following reasons:

This Best Practice describes the macro-level components to ensuring a successful Maintenance 
& Repair Contract (MARC).  
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Key components to ensuring profitability for a MARC, encompass the following: 
 
1. Accurate Rates 
a. Make commercial decisions with a clear understanding of the cost base 
 
2. Careful Rate Discounting 
a. Account for all discounts when determining rates – customer demand for lower rates 
cannot outweigh the bottom line. 
 
3. Contract Language 
a. Use contract language that balances the interests of the customer and dealer. 
 
4. Application / Operations 
a. Understand the relationships of costs in regards to time and contract age

5. Maintenance Management 
a. Consider contract costs along with availability 
b. Have the proper management tools in place 
 
6. Product Quality  
a. Understand that product improvements are not certain, and cannot be guaranteed to 
lower costs 
 
Other contributing factors to ensuring success: 
• Balance the interests of PINS with concern for profitability and risk 
• Avoid start up & implementation deficiencies 
• Consider the MARC as a value differentiator, versus a merchandizing tool 
• Have realistic expectations of Caterpillar support resources, and plan appropriately  
 
Effective management of the contract should also consider the following: 
• Ensure a line of site to performance targets  
• Use consistent MARC practices throughout the entire organization 
• Combine accountability and authority 
• Negotiate contracts with experienced individuals given full information and with Cat interest 
in mind 
• Have tools (“ready-sell-bid-negotiate-execute-claim”) in place 
• Ensure full understanding within Caterpillar and Dealer 
• Bring all dealers to use consistent practices 
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
It is strongly recommended that a MARC Management Plan be established. The Management 
Plan will address the following: (Please refer to the 0906-3.3-1029 - Developing a MARC 
Management Plan Best Practice Publication.) 
 
1. Organization Structure and Communication Plan: 
a. Determine who & how to support MARC’s; and determine the communication strategy. 
2. Financial Plan 
a. Determine the key measures to track and report regarding the health and success of 
contracts. 
3. Parts Plan 
a. Used in determining what components will be needed to support contracts. 
4. Information Systems 
a. Set-up the Dealer Business System to support contracts 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Fair, realistic expectations between Caterpillar, Dealer, and Customer 
• Contracts that will benefit both the Dealer and Customer, and subsequently provide higher 
potential for profitability
