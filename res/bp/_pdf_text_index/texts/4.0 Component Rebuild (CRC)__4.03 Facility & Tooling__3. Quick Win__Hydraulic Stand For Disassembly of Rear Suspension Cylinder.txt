1.0 Introduction  
 
The disassembly process for rear suspension cylinder takes one and a half hours approximately. 
In order to improve that time Ferreyros has implemented a hydraulic stand that allows them to do 
the same process in 20 minutes.  
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The focus of this Best Practice is improving the efficiency, safety, and ergonomics of suspension 
cylinder disassembly. The hydraulic stand can be used for two cylinders simultaneously (See 
picture 1).  Using the stand, both cylinders are located vertically and the operator does not have 
to strain to handle the cylinders manually. 
 
 
 
Picture 1. Suspension cylinder into hydraulic stand   
 
 
Before this Best Practice, the cylinder was positioned horizontally (See the picture 2). That 
operation took a long time and the technician had to do more physical activity.

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Safety operation – less manual handling. 
• Reduce the disassembly time by 80%. 
• Two suspension cylinders can be disassembled simultaneously.
