1.0 Introduction  
 
Component repair center  (CRC) receives diverse customer’s components daily and it is v ery 
important to check the correct p osition of the assemble parts  aiming to prevent failures related to 
assembly mistaken. 
 
Since Caterpillar has distinguished left and right side of  Housing Motor (425-3520 RH / 425-3521 
LH), it is required to have close attention to assembly process, once it is possible to assembly Gear 
Coupling (199-4719) in the mistaken position. 
Image 1 shows Gear Coupling assembled in the mistaken position. 
 
 
Image 1 – Gear-Coupling mistaken assembly. 
 
Thus, CRC technicians developed a tool which avoids the mistaken assembly of the final drive 
gear-coupling 199-4719. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This Best Practice proposes to use an old Body AS 542-1323 of the Motor GP-Travel from a down 
equipment as a standard. In this case, it was defined to paint the structure in blue to differentiate it 
as a tool, as shown in image 2:

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
1. Define an old Body AS of the Motor GP -Travel PN 542-1323 from a down equipment as a 
standard. 
2. Choose a different color to paint the part due to set it as a tool. 
3. Insert the tool in the bottom of the Housing Motor 425-3520 RH / 425-3521 LH as a gauge. 
4. Put the Gear-coupling PN 199-4719 on the top of the Housing Motor matching the flange. 
5. Check if the flange position of both parts are matching correctly. 
6. Position the bolts in their position. 
7. Torque the bolts using the specified measure. 
8. Remove the tool. 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
• Certify the correct assembly of the Gear Coupling (199-4719) in the Final Drive GP 
avoiding assembly mistakes. 
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
• An old Body AS of the Motor GP-Travel PN 542-1323. 
• Paint.
