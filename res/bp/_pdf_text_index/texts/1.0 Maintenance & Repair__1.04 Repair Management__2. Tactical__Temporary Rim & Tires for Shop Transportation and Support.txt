1.0 Introduction  
Assembly and rebuild procedures for Off Highway Trucks present a number of challenges due to 
their size, weight and the equipment required to carry out these procedures. This best practice 
introduces a custom rim and tire group intended to facilitate the rebuild process.  
 
During the assembly or rebuilding process, Off Highway Trucks need to be adequately supported 
and may need to be transported to various locations within the dealership to accommodate the 
assembly progression. Employing this rim and tire combination facilitates and improves build 
efficiency and improves machine transportation when compared to traditional methods. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
These custom rims were developed by WesTrac, Guildford branch, to support the execution of 
LOHT rebuilds for mine sites located within their territory. 
 
Dealer assembly/rebuild procedures require machines to be adequately supported using a safe, 
efficient and effective method. At present stands are the most commonly employed supporting 
device; these stands provide adequate support but cannot be used during machine transportation. 
A common procedure for the transportation and relocation of a machine requires the use of cranes 
for partially assembled machines and a set of standard rims & tires for an assembled machine. 
This custom rim and tire group serves two important purposes: they can be used as a supporting 
tool during machine assembly/rebuild (See Figures 1 & 2) and can be used for transportation 
purposes. As an example assembled Off Highway Trucks can be driven around the dealer yard on 
the custom rims and tires, eliminating the need for the standard rim and tire groups.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
• Fabricate custom rim group based on machine model (See attachments for stud patterns). 
 
• Contact local supplier to manufacture and install custom rubber tires (solid) onto custom  
 rims. 
 
• Introduce a modified assembly/rebuild procedure to incorporate this custom rim and tire  
 package. 
 
4.0 Benefits 
• Safety - provides excellent machine support during the assembly/rebuild process and  
 reduces working height for technicians. 
 
• Resources & Efficiency - custom rim and tire group is significantly easier to remove and     
 install when compared to the conventional rim and tire package. Special equipment 
 such as a tire handler is not required for this procedure.  
 
• Resources & Efficiency - custom rim and tire group provides easy access to the machine 
 and eliminates the need for stands. 
 
• Resources & Efficiency - machines can be easily transported and driven on custom rim 
 and tire groups without the need for additional equipment, such as overhead cranes.  
 
• Storage - custom rim and tire group is compact, little storage space is required when 
  compared to the standard rim and tire combination.
