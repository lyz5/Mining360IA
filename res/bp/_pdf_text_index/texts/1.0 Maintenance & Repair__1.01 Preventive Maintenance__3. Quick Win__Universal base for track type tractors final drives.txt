1.0 Introduction  
 
During the disassembly process, the TTT final dr ives are transported vertically, except when 
they are washed, as it is done horizontally. 
This is due to the fact that it is necessary to have access to wash the component internally on 
both sides, as illustrated in the following image. 
 
 
 
 
 
After removing the brake and the planetary reduction from the Dozer final drives, the spindle-
wheel assembly is sent to pre-wash on two wooden pallets; this handling and transport is 
complicated and unstable due to its geometry, generating an unsafe condition for those around 
during the execution of the task. 
This Best Practice proposes the design and manufacture of a universal base for the secure 
attachment and transport of the Spindle-Wheel assembly in all models of Dozer Final drives when 
they are sent to the washing area during the disassembly process.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
This best practice applies to all models of final drives on D9, D10 and D11track-type 
tractors. The base is manufactured in A36 Steel; it has two fastening points to transport it 
by means of the forklift. In each one, a curved support is installed; these are separated 485 
mm from each other, where the side of the Spindle and the wheel rest. The first one rests 
to 732mm and the second one rests to 462mm measured from the ground. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
 Take measurements to the different models on Dozers final drives. 
 Design of the base according to the measures taken. 
 Load Analysis of the base.  
 Manufacturing of the base. 
 Conduct tests at the shops with different models of final drives.  
 Start-up, after disclosing it to the operation with its change format. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
 It is safe when transporting the component. 
 It is safe for the personnel when transporting. 
 There’s some reduction in transporting times due to its reliability by the fastening of the 
component to the base.
