1.0 Introduction  
 
During daily lube service and preventative maintenance services it is critical to refill fluid 
compartments with the correct type of fluid.  Further, it is also recommended to refill in a manner 
that minimizes open compartments and fluid contamination.   
 
Daily lube services are performed either at the fuel island or by the mobile lube truck.  It is very 
easy for a serviceman to put the wrong type of oil into a given reservoir.  The same is true for PM 
services in the workshop.  This practice was put into place to minimize the probability of this 
happening. 
 
This practice can be easily replicated at any mine site and involves minimal effort and cost to 
proactively prevent incorrect fluid handling. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Simply, quick connections are installed on all oil fill ports.  Each type of oil has a different size 
connector.  Each connection is labeled to ensure proper filling.   
 
When the technician recognizes a need to fill an oil reservoir he/she identifies the proper oil type 
and connects the dispensing port in the workshop/fuel bay/lube truck to the fill port on the 
machine.  Only the correct connections will fit together to guarantee proper filling of the reservoir. 
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
1. Identify all fittings needed for all machines on site 
2. Purchase fittings for machines, service lube truck, fuel bay, and work shop 
3. Install all fittings 
4. Train service personnel to use the proper fittings

4.0 Benefits 
 
• Improvement of service accuracy - avoidance of compartments being filled with the wrong 
fluid, resulting in extended life and/or performance of a part or component. 
• Increased service quality and consistency. 
• Time savings during maintenance operations. 
• Oil spills are more easily avoided, increasing operator/technician safety.
