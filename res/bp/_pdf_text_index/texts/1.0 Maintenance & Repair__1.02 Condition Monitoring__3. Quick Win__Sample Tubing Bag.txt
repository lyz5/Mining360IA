1.0 Introduction  
Creating a custom bag for sample tubing that has an easy access hole and contamination cover is a 
convenient way to organize and carry sample tubing in service trucks.  This prevents unnecessary 
contamination and waste of tubing. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
The bag design is portable, able to hold a full roll of sample tubing and will include a sealable cover to 
keep contamination out of the access hole where the end of the tubing can be accessed.  
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
Insert a full roll of tubing into the bag. Put the end of tube through the access hole and close the cover 
over the end of the tube. To access and use the tubing, pull back the cover and dispense the correct 
amount of tubing without contaminating the rest of the roll. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
Contamination control. 
Reduction in wasted tubing.
