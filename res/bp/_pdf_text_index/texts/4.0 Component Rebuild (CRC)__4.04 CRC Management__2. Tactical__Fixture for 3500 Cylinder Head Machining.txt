A new device in order to improve machining of head as cylinder series 3500 has been 
implemented at the Machining Workshop in Ferreyros S.A. 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The focus of this Best Practice is reduce the centering time during the rebuilding of cylinder 
head at the Milling Machine. This kind of device is mounted on the table of the milling machine 
and tightened by four fixation devices. The adjustment fixation device must be tightened very 
slowly and accurate
 (See the picture 1). 
 
 
 
Picture 1.  Centering Device

now using this kind of device the new time of centering is 5 min. So, there is a 75% reduction of 
centering time.
