1.0 Introduction 
C
urrently, the monitoring of the levels of wear on the blade edges is carried out by the maintenance 
team, in which it is necessary to travel to the place of operation and request the equipment to stop 
to perform the measurement. 
T
hese downtimes impact equipment productivity, use of resources and labor. 
The idea is that the operator uses the Color Gauge before the start of his wo rk shift to check the 
wear range of the blade edge and, thus, communicate the maintenance, providing a follow -up of 
the edge wear. 
T
he objective of this Best Practice is to assist the operators in monitoring the blade wear edges, 
creating, through a Color Gauge, bands with colors, which will guide the operator to inform 
maintenance team about the current level of edge wear. This tool will also allow the operator to 
know if the edges of the blade are in proper condition for operation. 
2
.0 Best Practice Description 
T
his Best Practice proposes the elaboration of a Color Gauge with three colors that will classify the 
wear levels into three bands. These tracks have the following categories: 
G
reen Band: it shows that the edge measurement is within the work specifications. 
Y
ellow Band: it indicates that the edge is close to the minimum range limit specified for work and, 
in this case, maintenance technicians must schedule its replacement as soon as possible. 
R
ed Band: it guides the operator and maintenance t eam that the edge has already exceeded the 
specified work wear limit and it must be replaced immediately. 
I
n this way, before starting the service, using this Color Gauge, the operator can measure the edge 
wear level and inform the maintenance team. 
M
aintenance, in turn, through the information shared by the operator, can monitor the edge wear 
before the start of each work shift.

4
.0 Benefits 
1)
Time Saving
The use of the Color Gauge will reduce machine downtime for edge measurement, as the
operator itself evaluates the edges before starting the operation.
2)
Efficiency Improvement
Color Gauge  will allow a better control over edge wear monitoring and  changing edges
improved predictability.
3) R
esource savings
The use of Color Gauge to measure the edges will prevent the maintenance team from
moving towards the equipment on a daily basis and will also prevent damage to the edge
fixing base due to lack of wear monitoring.
5
.0 Resources Required 
1) C
olor Gauge - Supply the color gauge as per the specified colors and measurements.
2) O
perators – train operators how to use the Color Gauge efficiently.
3) M
aintenance team - Train the maintenance team in managing the flow of information about the
measures passed on by the operator.
T
he 4T-8317 edge is the recommended option for MG`s 16H, 16M, 16 and, for this reason, the gauge 
measurements are based on the specified wear parameters for it.
