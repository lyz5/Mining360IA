1.0 Introduction  
 
Removing cylinder head(s) when doing a partial repair in the CRC can allow debris to fall into the 
main oil gallery through the oil supply port.  Likewise, camshaft bearing installation during an engine 
overhaul will generate small metal shavings that will fall into the main oil gallery.  This Best Practice 
illustrates a simple method of catching any contaminants so they can be removed.  
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
In this example, 75mm PVC tubing has been cut in half lengthwise, and cut to block length, and 
inserted into the main oil gallery of a C175 engine.  As the camshaft bearings are pressed into the 
block, any small metal shavings that are generated during installation will fall into the main gallery and 
be caught by the tube (tube has been slid out partly for illustration purposes).

The tube can also be used during a partial repair during cylinder head(s) removal and installation by 
installing it into the oil gallery through the engine front housing (tube would need to be longer to take 
into account the width of the front housing).  
 
The tube should be thoroughly cleaned before and after each use, and stored in a protected manner.  
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Obtain, cut, and de-burr the proper diameter PVC tubing, and implement a storage method. 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
Maintains a clean main oil gallery prior to engine start-up.  
 
5.0 Resources Required 
 
Very minimal as described above.
