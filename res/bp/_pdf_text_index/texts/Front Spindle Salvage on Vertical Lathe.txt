1.0 Introduction  
 
This Best Practice was developed with the purpose of making an optimal, effective, and reliable 
salvage in the front wheel spindle of trucks, guaranteeing its specification according to the criteria 
CAT Inspection and Salvage of Front Wheel Spindles for Certain Off -Highway Trucks (SEBF9309- 
10). 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Illustration # 01 and 02, The critical areas of the bearing’s journals observed when they present wear 
spec out measure, they must be rebuilt with arc spray processes according (SEBF9309- 10). 
 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
In the machinery workshop of Barloworld Mongolia, we developed a device which allows vertical 
clamping of different front spindles Off -Highway trucks models 773 to 797, achieving an easy, 
efficient, and reliable salvage process in vertical lathes.
