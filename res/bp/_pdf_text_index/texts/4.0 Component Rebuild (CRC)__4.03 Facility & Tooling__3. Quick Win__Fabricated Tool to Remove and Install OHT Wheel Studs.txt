1.0 Introduction 
There is no Caterpillar designed tool to remove and install Off Highway Truck wheel studs from 
the flange except for the 797wheel group.  For other models, the common practice is to remove 
the studs manually with a hammer.  This process creates considerable noise in the shop, is time 
consuming, and fatiguing for the technician 
Some dealers have designed a hydraulic press operated tool for stud removal and installation.
