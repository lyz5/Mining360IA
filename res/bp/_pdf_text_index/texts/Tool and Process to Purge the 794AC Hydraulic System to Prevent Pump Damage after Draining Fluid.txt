1.0 Introduction  
 
To prevent the hydraulic pump from sucking in air and damaging the hydraulic system it is 
necessary to purge air from the hydraulic lines whenever the fluid has been drained such as during 
commissioning, certain maintenance, and component changeouts.   
 
The procedure from Caterpillar to pressurize the hydraulic tank and purge the air from the pump's 
hydraulic system for the 794AC is not clear and there is no single tool for efficiently  isolating the 
breather and pressuring the system to ultimately purge the air. 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The Tooling and Process described throughout this document is a Best Practice used to prevent 
damage/wear and shortened component life by efficiently purging air from the hydraulic sys tem 
components and lines during commissioning, certain maintenance,  and major component 
changeout.
 
 
Sotreq successfully applied this Best Practice during the commissioning of a fleet of 794AC Trucks 
in Northern Brazil. The customer acknowledged the success and efficiency of the process and 
mandated it for future hydraulic system maintenance. 
 
Please see the instructions and reference images below for the air purging process. 
 
 
Figure 1 – View of the hydraulic tank and adapter  
 
 
1. Temporarily remove the breather (E) at the top of the hydraulic tank and cover the hole. See 
Breather (Hoist and Brake Tank) - Replace (SEBU9099-08). If necessary, use tool 185-3630 
to remove the breather.

2. Insert an air adapter and regulator into the vent location. 
3. Pressurize the hydraulic tank to 70 kPa (10 psi) to hydraulic priming system. Do not add 
pressure on the hydraulic system. (70 kPa/10 psi Maximum) 
 
 
3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
Sotreq used a filter (4T -6788) to  develop the tool. The filter is cut, and part of the filter head 
(threaded plate) is used. Additionally, a plate with a pressure tap is installed.  
 
With this tool ready, the b reather at the top of the hydraulic tank must be removed, allowing the 
tool to be installed in place of the breather. The pressure entry must be connected to the pressure 
inlet to the air hose, with a pressure regulator limited to 10 psi.
