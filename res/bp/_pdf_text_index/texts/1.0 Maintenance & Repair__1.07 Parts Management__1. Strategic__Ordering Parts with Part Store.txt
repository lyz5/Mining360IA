1.0 Introduction  
 
Many customers are automating their procurement processes and integrating with supplier 
systems to streamline their operations and reduce transaction costs. Since 2001, Caterpillar has been 
an industry leader in the integrated procurement of parts by facilitating hundreds of connections 
between customer business systems and their Cat® dealer’s business systems. Customers who 
procure parts and receive electronic invoices through these connections experience transaction cost 
savings of as much as 50% or more, depending on their current processes. One customer’s process 
assessment showed that over half of the customer’s manual process steps were eliminated during 
the following processes: 
• Ordering 
• Shipping/Receiving 
• Invoice Reconciliation 
• Accounts Payable 
 
2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
The PartStore™ IP Deployment Program will enable a dealer’s largest parts customers to 
automate their parts ordering process.  There are two approaches to this automation:  PartStore Web 
and PartStore IP.   
  
“PartStore Web” is an internet application that is accessed through the dealer’s web site that 
allows Customers to check for parts availability and pricing and place accurate parts orders on-line.   
 
“PartStore IP” allows us to integrate our customers’ enterprise systems with our Dealer’s 
business system to enable the following transactions to be completed electronically: direct orders, 
stock orders, planned orders, order responses, pay-upon-receipt transactions, invoice updates and 
electronic funds transfers.

Locate the Dealer’s Web Site or Dealer StoreFront 
 
You can access to your Cat Dealer’s web site in one of two ways: use the Dealer’s URL or use 
the 'Dealer Locator' on the Caterpillar Inc. web site (http://www.cat.com). 
 
The Dealer’s URL or web address is found through communication with a representative of the 
Dealer, printed on an invoice from a previous purchase, or advertised inside or outside the 
dealership.  Type the URL into the 'Address' box of a web browser and the Dealer web site is 
displayed. 
 
To use the 'Dealer Locator' on the Caterpillar, Inc website: 
• Open a web browser. 
The browser window is displayed. 
• In the 'Address' text box type: http://www.cat.com 
The Caterpillar corporate web site is displayed in the browser window. 
• In the main navigation, upper right, click 'Find a Dealer.' 
The 'Find a Dealer' search screen is displayed. 
• Enter the information for the dealer or location information and click 'Find.' 
The search results are displayed. 
• For a selected dealer, in the 'Links' column, if the link exists, click 'Go to Website.' 
The specified dealer's web site is displayed in a new window.

indicates registration is successful.  The message is in green font.  (F i g u r e  4)

3.2 PartStore Integrated Procurement (IP) 
Connecting to PartStore Integrated Procurement is a much more involved process.  The 
customer should first contact the dealer and request a project be started to connect   PartStore 
Integrated Procurement is quite flexible, and has only two key requirements:  Be able to send 
data via XML, and that data must be sent via a HTTPS secure post.  See attached White Paper 
for more details on implementing an IP connection. 
 
 PartStore Integrated Procurement can be customized based on the customer’s needs.  
Business rules are set at the PartStore eHub level, which can be set around: 
• Backordered parts 
• Price discrepancies 
• Invalid Part numbers 
• Replaced Parts 
• Virtually any other customized need the customer may have 
 
There are 2 main integration options available to the customer who wishes to use PartStore 
Integrated Procurement:  Direct or via third party.

Direct Connection 
 
Advantages with Direct Connection to the Caterpillar E-hub 
• High availability, currently over 99%, due to a single connection point 
• No transaction fees to a third party 
• All Caterpillar e-hub services and connections are available without 
waiting for a third party to provide this access 
• Management is potentially easier with two parties instead of three 
 
Third Party Connection 
 
 
Advantages with Connection through a Vendor Hub or Marketplace Partner 
• Customer can utilize the same technical connection for multiple suppliers 
• Less reliance on the customer’s IS department to establish and maintain 
the connection 
• Agreements can include other services or even full hardware and services 
outsourcing. 
 
Note: In the majority of cases if an organization is capable of connecting to a 
vendor hub or marketplace partner they will also be capable of a direct 
connection.

4.0 Benefits 
 
Automating the parts ordering process creates benefits for both our customers and for the dealer.  
For our customers, the benefits include: 
 
• Improved parts availability and reduced equipment down-time due to instant availability 
on emergency orders, reduced order-delivery times, and improved order accuracy; and  
 
• Reduced internal costs through automation of stock replenishment transactions, 
elimination of manual steps and duplicate data entry in all transactions, and reduced invoiced 
discrepancies. 
 
For the dealer, the benefits include:  
 
• Increased customer satisfaction & loyalty, with the potential for increased market share 
and sales; 
 
• Reduced transaction costs through the elimination of manual steps and duplicate data 
entry, fewer returned spare parts, and reduced invoice discrepancies; 
 
• Faster payment through automation of invoicing and payments; and  
 
• Migration of our eBusiness activity to the most cost-effective platform (Cat eHub, 
Seamless migration to future releases of Cat’s DBSi).

5.0 Resources Required 
For connecting to PartStore Web, the buyer is typically the only one needed to get access.  For 
PartStore IP projects, it is a bit more involved.  Typically, weekly conference calls are required to 
keep the project moving.  As part of the project, the following people should be involved: 
 
• Caterpillar Project Leader (can be requested via eparts@cat.com) 
• Caterpillar PartStore Developer 
• Dealer PartStore Admin 
• Dealer PartStore sales rep/manager (optional) 
• Customer Procurement manager 
• Customer Buyer 
• Customer IT representative 
• 3rd Party representative (optional)
