1.0 Introduction  
 
Under normal operating conditions, premature wear can occur on the retainers and bolts for the 
rollers on the D10T/R/N Track-Type Tractor(s).  This wear is due to mixed material flow (abrasive 
and high impact) across the external face of the rollers.  The result of this wear can cause a 
decrease in roller service life. 
 
Oil leaking from the worn roller retainers and bolts may cause unplanned downtime in the field.  
In some cases, rollers have to be changed at 50% to 60% of their service life.   
 
This Best Practice will outline a procedure to protect the retainer and bolts from abrasive wear.  
The objective is to allow  120% of service life on the  roller shell without failure in the field .  
Ultimately, improving roller performance and reducing cost per hour.

2.0 Best Practice Description 
 
Some applications for D10N/R/T Track -Type Tractors consist of highly abrasive material.  These 
applications can greatly reduce service life on the rollers, retainers and bolts.   
 
To help increase service life in these applications an Abrasion Resistant Material (ARM) coating 
may be applied to the roller retainers (both single and double flange).  This coating is applied b y  
welding Tungsten Carbide ARM particles on the retainers. 
  
The coating may be applied before installing new rollers or during routine maintenance (shell and 
seal replacement).  It is good practice to coat all rollers at the same time to allow for even wear 
distribution. 
 
The Tungsten Carbide coating adds 200 grams per roller, which equals a total of 3.2 kg for all 16 
rollers.  This added weight does not have an affect on equipment operation.

3.0 Implementation Steps 
 
In order to properly coat the rollers with Tungsten Carbide ARM, these steps must be followed: 
 
1. Clean the surface  of the roller to be coated with Tungsten Carbide ARM using a grinder with 
steel circular brush attachment.  Ensure all paint, rust, debris, etc. is completely removed. 
2. Pre-heat the roller to 302 ºF (150 ºC) using therm al furnace equipment ( maintain this 
temperature while coating with Tungsten Carbide ARM).  This allows proper adherence to the 
roller surface. 
3. Weld and/or coat with Tungsten Carbide ARM using a tungsten material dispenser.  Ensure to 
maintain a roller temperature of 302 ºF (150 ºC).  
4. Check your work area to ensure that there are no slag penetration problems. 
 
 
4.0 Benefits 
 
Benefits associated with this Best Practice include: 
 
• Reduce unscheduled downtime in the field due to leaks or over-heated rollers (dry gaskets).   
• Reduce roller cost per hour. 
• Increase the service life of the rollers.
