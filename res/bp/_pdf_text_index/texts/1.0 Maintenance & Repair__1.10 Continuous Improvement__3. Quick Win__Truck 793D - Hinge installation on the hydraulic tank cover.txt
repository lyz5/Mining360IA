1.0 Introduction 
  
We have in the routine of preventive maintenance to remove the cover of the hydraulic tank, to make 
cleaning inside the hydraulic tank; we evaluate and observe a risk in this activity. 
We then decided to develop a best practices, installing a hinge on the hydraulic tank cover has an 
ergonomic criterion and safety action.

2.0 Best practice description.     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
The purpose of installing a hinge on the cover of the hydraulic tank; It has the objective of reducing 
the risk, during the removal of the cover, to clean the internal part of the hydraulic tank, due to the 
weight (42kg), with difficult lifting point, time gain and simplifying the activity, being necessary only to 
be only one person is needed to do the job. 
 
 
  
 
 
View of the open tank, with the aid of hinges and facility to open and close the hydraulic tank.

3.0 Implementation steps. 
 
This step is very simple; With the hydraulic tank empty, the cover in place, with the specific torque 
applied to the screws 30 lb / ft; weld two hinges (328-6156) one at each end of the cover so that the 
space for the cover fixing screws is free to be installed and removed.. 
 
 
Hinges installation location.  
 
 
 
4.0 Benefits. 
 
       • Reduces  the risk of injury when removing the cover from the hydraulic tank. 
 
       • Time savings during the cleaning activity of the inside of the hydraulic tank. 
         
       • Reduces  the number of people to do the internal cleaning of the hydraulic tank.

5.0 Resources required. 
 
We can use the PM 4000 opportunity window where the hydraulic system oil is drained or repairs 
to the system, replacing the brake cooling pump, cleaning the tank, to apply the improvement. 
 
One hour of the welder's labor, two hour of the mechanic and Parts total cost (~$200.00).
